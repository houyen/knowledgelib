# Best Dog DNA Test Kits (2026)

## What are the best dog DNA test kits in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Embark Breed + Health (~$139) — 400+ breeds, 270+ health conditions, Cornell-developed, best Relative Finder & inbreeding (COI) data.**
**Best value: Wisdom Panel Premium (~$160 direct) — 365+ breeds, 265+ health conditions, plus a free vet consult on at-risk results that Embark does not include.**
**Best budget: DNA My Dog (~$65) — cheapest credible breed-ID kit, 350+ breeds, results in 2-3 weeks.**
For 2026 the two-horse race is Embark vs Wisdom Panel; everything else fills a niche.
[<a href="https://www.petmd.com/dog/vet-verified/best-dog-dna-test-kit">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.caninejournal.com/best-dog-dna-test/">src2</a>]

## Summary

The 2026 dog DNA market is a two-horse race between **Embark** and **Wisdom Panel**, with a handful of niche alternatives. **Embark Breed + Health** (~$139 street, $199 MSRP) is the consensus best overall: developed with Cornell University's College of Veterinary Medicine, it screens 350-400+ breeds using ~200,000+ genetic markers (the most of any consumer kit), reports 250-270+ health conditions including MDR1 drug sensitivity, and uniquely offers a Relative Finder plus a coefficient-of-inbreeding (COI) score. [src1, src2, src5]

**Wisdom Panel** wins for most owners on value. The **Premium** kit ($159.99 direct from Wisdom Panel, or ~$120/kit via Amazon's intermittently-stocked 2-pack ~$240) covers 365+ breeds and 265+ health conditions and — critically — bundles a free 15-minute veterinary consultation if results flag a health risk, which Embark does not. Its results also arrive about a week sooner on average (2-3 weeks vs Embark's 2-4). The **Essential** tier (~$120) is the budget Wisdom option but drops health screening to ~30 conditions. [src2, src3, src4]

Beyond the leaders: **Basepaws** (~$80 on sale, $149 list) screens the most conditions (280+) via next-generation sequencing but is the slowest (4-6 weeks); **Orivet GenoPet Plus** (~$130) offers veterinary-lab provenance and 330+ conditions; and **DNA My Dog** (~$65) is the cheapest credible breed-only option. Note that **Ancestry Know Your Pet DNA**, previously the breadth leader (400+ breeds), was discontinued at the end of 2025 and is no longer sold. [src2, src3, src6]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Embark Breed + Health (~$139) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/embark-breed-health)
The consensus top pick across PetMD's vet-verified roundup, Canine Journal, and DNA Weekly. Developed with Cornell University's veterinary college, it reads ~200,000+ genetic markers — roughly twice as many as Wisdom Panel — to identify 350-400+ breeds, reports 250-270+ heritable health conditions (including MDR1 drug sensitivity and exercise-induced collapse), and adds an Allergy Risk Score. Its Relative Finder lets you message owners of your dog's genetic relatives, and every dog gets a coefficient-of-inbreeding (COI) score. Embark results are accepted by the OFA where a DNA registry exists. [src1, src2, src5]

### Best Value: Wisdom Panel Premium (~$160 direct) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/wisdom-panel-premium)
For roughly 80% of owners, Wisdom Panel Premium is the smarter buy: 365+ breeds, 265+ health conditions, behavior and weight-predisposition insights, genetic-diversity reporting, and — the feature Embark cannot match — a free 15-minute veterinary consultation if a result flags a health risk. Results arrive about a week sooner on average. It is $159.99 for a single kit direct from Wisdom Panel (where our buy link points); Amazon's 2-pack (~$240, i.e. ~$120/kit) is a cheaper per-kit option when it is in stock. [src2, src3, src4]

### Best Budget: DNA My Dog (~$65) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dna-my-dog-essential)
The cheapest kit that reviewers still consider credible for breed identification. It covers 350+ breeds with a simple at-home cheek swab and 2-3 week turnaround. There is no genetic health report at this price, and reviewers note the breed call is less granular than Embark/Wisdom — but for "what mix is my rescue?" curiosity at a third of the price, it does the job. [src2, src4]

### Max Health Screening: Basepaws Dog DNA (~$80-149) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/basepaws-dog-dna)
Basepaws (owned by Zoetis since 2022) screens the most health conditions on this list — 280+ across roughly a dozen disease categories — using next-generation sequencing rather than the SNP-array chips most rivals use. The tradeoff is speed: results take 4-6 weeks. A natural pick if you already use Basepaws for a cat in the same household. It currently sells for $79.99 (from its $149 list) at Basepaws.com, where our buy link points, since its Amazon listing is presently unavailable. [src2, src3]

### Vet-Lab Pedigree: Orivet GenoPet Plus (~$130) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/orivet-genopet-plus)
Orivet is a veterinary-genetics company, and GenoPet Plus carries that lab provenance: 365+ breeds, 330+ genetic health conditions plus 50+ traits, and a "Personalized Life Plan" that turns results into age- and breed-specific care recommendations. Turnaround is 3-5 weeks. Best for owners who want a clinical feel and breed-specific purebred panels. [src2, src6]

### Embark Without Health: Embark Breed ID (~$109) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/embark-breed-id)
If you want Embark's class-leading breed accuracy and Relative Finder but don't need the health panel, the Breed Identification kit delivers 99% breed-ancestry accuracy for mixed breeds, the family tree, and COI at a ~$30-50 discount to Breed + Health. Note you cannot retroactively add health to this kit, so only choose it if you're sure you don't want disease screening. [src1, src5]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Embark Breed + Health vs Wisdom Panel Premium
The defining matchup. Embark reads about twice the genetic markers, has the deeper ancestry/Relative-Finder toolset, and the stronger published-research record. Wisdom Panel Premium counters with a free vet consult on at-risk findings, results ~1 week sooner, and a generally lower street price. Embark is the genetics enthusiast's pick; Wisdom Panel is the better default for most owners. [src2, src3, src5]

**Pick Embark Breed + Health if:** you adopted a mystery-ancestry rescue, want Relative Finder / COI, or care about research provenance.
**Pick Wisdom Panel Premium if:** you want the included vet consult, faster results, or a lower price for comparable health depth.

### Wisdom Panel Essential vs DNA My Dog
The budget bracket. Wisdom Panel Essential uses the same accurate 430+-breed engine as Premium but strips health down to ~30 conditions (mainly MDR1) for ~$120. DNA My Dog undercuts it at ~$65 with 350+ breeds but no health report and a coarser breed call. Essential is the better breed-ID if you can stretch the budget; DNA My Dog wins on pure price. [src2, src4]

**Pick Wisdom Panel Essential if:** you want top-tier breed accuracy and a basic MDR1 safety check.
**Pick DNA My Dog if:** you only need a rough breed mix at the lowest price.

### Basepaws vs Orivet GenoPet Plus
The two deep-health alternatives to Embark/Wisdom. Basepaws screens 280+ conditions via NGS sequencing (slower, 4-6 weeks) and pairs well with a Basepaws cat. Orivet brings veterinary-lab provenance, 330+ conditions, a Life Plan, and slightly faster 3-5 week turnaround. Both are health-first picks for owners who want more than the mainstream brands. [src2, src3, src6]

**Pick Basepaws if:** you want NGS sequencing depth or already use Basepaws for a cat.
**Pick Orivet if:** you want vet-lab provenance, the most conditions, and a care-plan output.

## Decision Logic

### If budget is under $80
--> **DNA My Dog** (~$65) for breed identification only — the cheapest credible kit. Accept that there's no health report and a coarser breed call at this price. [src2, src4]

### If primary goal is genetic health screening
--> **Embark Breed + Health** (~$139) or **Wisdom Panel Premium** (~$160). Both screen 250-270+ conditions. Choose Wisdom Panel if the free at-risk vet consult matters; choose Embark for deeper marker coverage. Avoid breed-only kits (Embark Breed ID, DNA My Dog, Wisdom Essential) — they screen 0-30 conditions. [src1, src2]

### If you adopted a mixed-breed rescue with unknown ancestry
--> **Embark Breed + Health** for Relative Finder + the most markers, or **Wisdom Panel Premium** for full health screening plus a vet consult. [src1, src2]

### If you already own a cat tested with Basepaws
--> **Basepaws Dog DNA** (~$80) to keep both pets in one platform; accept the 4-6 week turnaround. [src3]

### If you need OFA-accepted or breeder-grade clearances
--> A consumer kit is not sufficient on its own. Use **Embark for Breeders** or a per-disease panel from **Paw Print Genetics** with genetic counseling. [src1, src2]

### Default recommendation (unknown requirements)
--> **Wisdom Panel Premium** (~$160). Best blend of accuracy, full health screening, included vet support, and faster results for the typical owner. Step up to **Embark Breed + Health** if lineage tools or research provenance matter (and it is now the cheaper of the two). [src2, src3]

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **It's a two-horse race**: Embark and Wisdom Panel dominate every credible 2026 roundup; the rest (Basepaws, Orivet, DNA My Dog) compete on single dimensions — NGS depth, vet-lab pedigree, or price. [src1, src2]
- **Ancestry exited pet DNA**: Ancestry stopped selling its Know Your Pet DNA kit at the end of 2025 (the line was acquired by BioPet Laboratories in 2026), removing the market's largest breed database (400+) from sale and further concentrating the category around Embark and Wisdom Panel. [src2]
- **Vet support is the new differentiator**: Wisdom Panel's free at-risk vet consult has made bundled veterinary access — not just data — a buying factor. Embark notably does not include a live consult. [src2, src3]
- **NGS vs SNP arrays**: Basepaws' next-generation sequencing (vs the SNP-chip arrays most kits use) lets it claim the most conditions (280+), signaling where the technology is heading — at the cost of slower turnaround. [src3]
- **Health depth ranges 8x across tiers**: from ~30 conditions (Wisdom Essential) to 280-330+ (Basepaws, Orivet). Buyers must match the tier to their goal; "DNA test" alone says nothing about health coverage. [src2, src3, src6]
- **MSRP is fiction**: Embark's $199 list almost never holds — street price is $110-159. Basepaws lists $149 but sells ~$86. Always check the live Amazon price. [src1, src2]
- **Consolidation continues**: Zoetis owns Basepaws (acquired 2022) and the broader Wisdom Panel/Mars portfolio; institutional ownership is reshaping pet genomics toward integrated vet ecosystems. [src2]
