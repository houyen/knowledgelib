# Best Riding Lawn Mowers (2026)

## What are the best riding lawn mowers in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: John Deere X354 (~$5,100) — Consumer Reports' 2026 best-overall lawn tractor with perfect handling, cutting, bagging, and mulching scores plus best-in-class owner satisfaction.**
**Best value: Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB (~$2,999) — 24 HP Kohler V-twin, 50" fabricated deck, 16" turning radius, 3-yr unlimited-hours warranty.**
**Best budget: Craftsman R110 (~$1,939) — only quality riding mower under $2,000; 30" deck, 10.5 HP, suits flat 0.5-1 acre yards.**
Note: 8 of 10 picks are dealer-only (not stocked on Amazon) — those buy buttons route to the manufacturer's official product page. [src1, src2, src4]

## Summary

The riding lawn mower market in 2026 offers strong options across gas lawn tractors, gas zero-turn mowers, and a rapidly maturing battery-electric segment. Consumer Reports' 2026 testing promoted the **John Deere X354** (~$5,100) to overall best-lawn-tractor status — it earned perfect ratings in handling, cutting evenness, bagging, mulching, and side discharge, plus the top scores in owner satisfaction and brand reliability of any mower tested [src1, src4]. The **John Deere S130** (~$2,700) remains the top value pick in the entry tier thanks to its 22 HP V-twin engine, tight 18-inch turning radius, hydrostatic transmission, and the Easy Change 30-second oil change system [src1, src2, src6]. Both Deere models earn above-average predicted reliability and stellar owner satisfaction scores [src1, src4].

For value-conscious buyers needing a wider cut, the Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB delivers a 50-inch fabricated deck with a 24 HP Kohler engine and a class-leading 16-inch turning radius for $2,999, backed by a 3-year unlimited-hours warranty [src2, src3, src7]. In the zero-turn segment, the Toro 50-inch TimeCutter MyRIDE stands out with its floating suspension seat system providing 3 inches of travel, dual hydrostatic transaxles, and a top speed of 7 mph that allows it to cover roughly 3 acres per hour [src2, src3].

Battery-electric riders have hit a practical inflection point. The EGO Power+ Z6 ZT4204L delivers the equivalent of 22 HP from four independent brushless motors, cuts up to 2 acres per charge, and recharges in approximately 2 hours — matching gas zero-turn cut quality without emissions, fuel costs, or engine maintenance [src2, src5]. Kubota remains the most reliable riding mower brand overall according to Consumer Reports, earning top scores in both predicted reliability and owner satisfaction for gas lawn tractors and zero-turn mowers [src4].

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: John Deere S130 (~$2,700) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/john-deere-s130)
The S130 combines a 22 HP V-twin engine with a hydrostatic transmission and 42-inch Edge deck, delivering smooth cutting at up to 5.5 mph. Its 18-inch turning radius handles tight spaces well, and the Easy Change 30-second oil change system eliminates messy oil draining. Assembled in Tennessee with a full welded steel frame and cast-iron front axle, it is built for longevity. Consumer Reports rates John Deere above average for reliability with stellar owner satisfaction. [src1, src2, src6]

### Best Value: Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB (~$2,999) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/cub-cadet-xt1-lt50)
At $2,999, the XT1 LT50 FAB offers a 50-inch fabricated 11-gauge steel deck — 8 inches wider than comparably priced John Deere models — with a 24 HP Kohler 7000 Series engine and a tight 16-inch turning radius that approaches zero-turn maneuverability. The 3-year unlimited-hours warranty and lifetime fabricated deck shell warranty make it an exceptional long-term value. [src2, src3, src7]

### Best Premium Lawn Tractor: Husqvarna TS 242XD (~$4,099) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/husqvarna-ts-242xd)
The TS 242XD pairs a 21.5 HP Kawasaki engine with premium comfort features including LED headlights, a high backrest padded seat, and a differential lock for improved traction on hills. One expert reviewer reported cutting mowing time from 2-3 hours to 30 minutes. Its 4-gallon fuel tank reduces refueling stops on larger properties. [src3]

### Best Gas Zero-Turn: Toro 50" TimeCutter MyRIDE (~$4,599) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/toro-timecutter-50-myride)
The TimeCutter MyRIDE features a unique floating suspension seat system with 3 inches of travel that dramatically reduces rider fatigue on bumpy terrain. Its dual hydrostatic transaxles and 10-gauge steel fabricated 50-inch deck reach speeds of 7 mph, covering approximately 3 acres per hour in testing. The 24.5 HP Toro Commercial V-twin engine delivers commercial-grade power for residential use. [src2, src3]

### Best Zero-Turn Value: Husqvarna Z254F (~$3,949) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/husqvarna-z254f)
The Z254F is one of the most popular zero-turn mowers in the US, offering a 54-inch ClearCut fabricated deck with a 23 HP Kawasaki engine at a lower price than competing 54-inch zero-turns. The patent-pending automatic park brake system activates when steering levers move outward, adding a safety feature that competitors lack. It handles 2-3 acre properties efficiently. [src1, src3]

### Best Battery Zero-Turn: EGO Power+ Z6 ZT4204L (~$6,557) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/ego-power-zt4204l)
The Z6 delivers 22 HP equivalent from four independent brushless motors with Peak Power+ technology that combines up to six 56V batteries. It cuts up to 2 acres per charge with the four included 10Ah batteries, recharging in approximately 2 hours — four times faster than competitors. Three driving modes (Control, Standard, Sport), Bluetooth connectivity, 32 LED lights, and zero emissions make it the most advanced battery rider available. Consumer Reports testing confirms battery riders now match gas models in cut quality. [src2, src5]

### Best Budget: Craftsman R110 (~$1,939) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/craftsman-r110)
The R110 is the most affordable entry point into riding mowers, with a 30-inch deck and 10.5 HP Briggs & Stratton engine. The 6-speed manual transmission and compact footprint suit small properties of 0.5 to 1 acre with simple terrain. It includes a mulching kit and weighs just 330 lbs, making it easy to maneuver in tight spaces. [src3]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### John Deere X354 vs John Deere S130
Both are John Deere lawn tractors with 42-inch decks, but they sit at very different price points. The X354 (~$5,100) is Consumer Reports' 2026 top pick with perfect testing scores, adjustable seat suspension, cruise control, and a 4-year/300-hour warranty. The S130 (~$2,700) is the value-tier entry with the same Easy Change oil system and a 2-year warranty — it handles 0.5-2 acres very capably without the premium-tier extras. [src1, src2]

**Pick John Deere X354 if:** budget reaches ~$5,000, you mow regularly enough that comfort (suspension, cruise) matters, or you want the highest-rated lawn tractor available.
**Pick John Deere S130 if:** budget is closer to $2,500-3,000 and you want Deere reliability without paying for premium ergonomic features.

### John Deere S130 vs Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB
The pricing matchup of the value tier: ~$2,700 vs ~$2,999. The S130 wins on engine refinement (22 HP V-twin Deere-spec), Easy Change oil system, and resale value. The XT1 LT50 wins on deck width (50" vs 42" — finishes open areas ~20% faster), a tighter 16" turning radius, and a 3-year unlimited-hours warranty vs Deere's 2-year. [src2, src3, src7]

**Pick John Deere S130 if:** your lawn has tight gates, mature trees, or under-2-acre flatter terrain — and brand resale matters.
**Pick Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB if:** you have 1.5-3 acres of open lawn, want a wider cut for faster mowing, and value the longer warranty.

### Toro 50" TimeCutter MyRIDE vs Husqvarna Z254F
Two popular residential gas zero-turns. The Toro MyRIDE (~$4,599) features the floating suspension seat system with 3" of travel — dramatically reducing rider fatigue on bumpy terrain — and a 24.5 HP commercial-grade V-twin. The Z254F (~$3,949) gives you a wider 54" deck (vs Toro's 50") at a lower price, with a 23 HP Kawasaki and Husqvarna's patented automatic park brake. [src1, src2, src3]

**Pick Toro 50" TimeCutter MyRIDE if:** terrain is bumpy/uneven, comfort over long sessions matters, or you want commercial-grade engine durability.
**Pick Husqvarna Z254F if:** you want the widest deck for the money, terrain is mostly flat, and a 54" cut on 2-3 acres is a priority.

### EGO Power+ Z6 ZT4204L vs Toro 50" TimeCutter MyRIDE
The electric-vs-gas zero-turn matchup. The EGO Z6 (~$6,557) delivers 22 HP equivalent from four brushless motors, cuts up to 2 acres per charge, recharges in ~2 hours, and eliminates fuel, oil changes, spark plugs, and winterization. The Toro MyRIDE (~$4,599) wins on raw range (unlimited with refueling), bigger property capability (3+ acres/hour), and proven gas-engine longevity. [src2, src5]

**Pick EGO Power+ Z6 ZT4204L if:** lawn is ≤2 acres, you want zero emissions and quiet operation, and value low long-term maintenance.
**Pick Toro 50" TimeCutter MyRIDE if:** lawn is 2.5+ acres, you don't want to manage battery charging windows, or you mow multiple times per session.

### Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB vs Husqvarna TS 242XD
Lawn tractor value matchup at different price tiers. The XT1 LT50 (~$2,999) offers a 50" deck and 24 HP for less. The TS 242XD (~$4,099) bumps to premium features — LED headlights, padded high-back seat, differential lock for hill traction, 4-gallon fuel tank — but with a smaller 42" deck. [src2, src3]

**Pick Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB if:** you want the widest deck per dollar and don't need hill-specific traction.
**Pick Husqvarna TS 242XD if:** terrain has noticeable slopes (differential lock matters), you'll mow long sessions (comfort + fuel capacity), or you want premium fit-and-finish.

## Decision Logic

### If budget < $2,000
--> The Craftsman R110 (~$1,939) is the only quality riding mower under $2,000. It suits flat lawns of 0.5-1 acre. For lawns under 0.5 acres, a walk-behind mower is more practical and cost-effective. [src3]

### If lawn is 1-2 acres with mixed terrain
--> Choose the John Deere S130 for reliability and maneuverability, or the Cub Cadet XT1 LT50 FAB for a wider cut at a similar price. The S130's 42-inch deck navigates tighter spaces, while the LT50's 50-inch deck finishes open areas faster. [src1, src2]

### If lawn is 2-4 acres
--> Prioritize a zero-turn mower over a lawn tractor. The Toro TimeCutter MyRIDE covers 3 acres per hour vs approximately 2 acres per hour for lawn tractors, saving 30-45 minutes per mow session. The comfort suspension system matters more on longer mowing sessions. [src2, src3]

### If terrain has slopes over 10 degrees
--> Choose a lawn tractor (John Deere S130, Husqvarna TS 242XD) over a zero-turn mower. Zero-turns lose traction on slopes and present rollover risk above 15 degrees. The Husqvarna TS 242XD's differential lock provides superior hill traction. [src1, src3]

### If user wants zero maintenance and quiet operation
--> The EGO Power+ Z6 ZT4204L eliminates oil changes, spark plugs, air filters, fuel stabilization, and engine winterization. Operating noise is roughly 50% lower than gas models. The higher upfront cost ($6,557) is offset by zero fuel costs and minimal maintenance over 5+ years. [src2, src5]

### Default recommendation
--> If budget reaches ~$5,000, the **John Deere X354** is the safest pick — it is Consumer Reports' 2026 overall top lawn tractor with perfect testing scores and best-in-class owner satisfaction. If budget is ~$2,500-3,000, the **John Deere S130** is the value default — 0.5-2 acres, hydrostatic transmission, Easy Change oil system, and a 42-inch deck that fits through most garden gates. [src1, src2, src4]

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Battery-electric maturity**: Consumer Reports testing shows the best battery riding mowers now perform as well as or better than gas models in cut quality, with the EGO Z6 and Ryobi 80V leading the segment. Runtime of 2+ acres per charge has crossed the practical threshold for most residential users. [src2, src5]
- **Reliability leader shift**: Kubota has emerged as the only brand earning top scores across all Consumer Reports reliability and owner satisfaction metrics for both gas lawn tractors and zero-turn mowers, displacing John Deere's longtime dominance in satisfaction rankings. [src4]
- **Zero-turn comfort innovations**: Suspension seat systems like Toro's MyRIDE and Cub Cadet's lap-bar designs have made zero-turn mowers accessible to homeowners who previously found them intimidating or uncomfortable. [src2, src3]
- **Price compression**: Entry-level riding mower prices have risen 10-15% since 2023 due to steel and component costs, pushing budget models like the Craftsman R110 near the $2,000 mark. [src3]

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US retail as of May 2026. Riding mowers are frequently purchased through authorized dealer networks where pricing may differ from online listings.
- Engine HP ratings follow SAE J1940 standards but real-world cutting performance depends on blade design, deck airflow, grass conditions, and terrain.
- Battery mower runtime varies significantly with grass height, slope, blade speed, and temperature. Manufacturer claims reflect ideal conditions.
- Zero-turn mowers are not recommended for slopes exceeding 15 degrees. Lawn tractors with locking differentials are safer for hilly terrain.
- Most riding mowers require flat-bed delivery or dealer setup; factor in $100-$300 delivery costs not included in listed prices.
- This comparison focuses on residential models. Commercial landscaping operations should evaluate commercial-grade zero-turn mowers with higher hour ratings.
- The Ryobi RYRM8034 80V zero-turn has been discontinued by Ryobi (its official product page remains live for support and parts); shoppers wanting the electric-for-large-yards option should look at Ryobi's current 80V HP 54" zero-turn or the EGO Z6. [src5]
