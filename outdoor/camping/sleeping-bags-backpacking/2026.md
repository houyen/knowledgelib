## What are the best sleeping bags for backpacking in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Feathered Friends Swallow UL 20 (~$659)** — 950+ fill goose down at 1 lb 10.6 oz, the warmth-to-weight leader after the 2026 redesign. **Best value: REI Co-op Magma 15 (~$399)** — 850-fill down, nine unisex sizes, 21F ISO comfort. **Best budget: Kelty Cosmic Down 20 (~$210)** — 550-fill down with PFAS-free DWR. ## Summary

The backpacking sleeping bag market in 2026 is defined by higher fill powers, hydrophobic down treatments, and a growing quilt segment that challenges the traditional mummy bag. The Feathered Friends Swallow UL 20 leads the pack with 950+ fill power goose down delivering exceptional warmth-to-weight at just 1 lb 10.6 oz (slightly lighter after the 2026 redesign), though its $659 price tag limits it to committed ultralight backpackers. For most hikers, the REI Co-op Magma 15 at $399 offers the best balance of performance, sizing options (nine sizes), and value with 850-fill down and a verified 21F ISO comfort rating. [src1, src2]

The 2026 update cycle brought significant changes: Therm-a-Rest's Parsec spring 2026 release upgraded the bag from 800-fill to 900-fill power Muscovy hydrophobic down with plush baffles and a simplified strap-free design, while keeping weight under 1 lb 13 oz. Feathered Friends introduced a new Pertex Quantum Pro shell (7D) with Pertex Diamond Fuse footbox lining (15D), trimming weight by ~0.7 oz. Budget-conscious hikers now have strong options in the Kelty Cosmic Down 20 ($210) and Mountain Hardwear Bishop Pass 15 ($315-335), both offering capable down insulation at accessible price points. Side sleepers should look at the Nemo Disco 15 Endless Promise (~$389) — the only spoon-shaped bag in this roundup, with a recycling commitment through Nemo's Endless Promise program. [src1, src4, src5, src8]

Across eight sources and dozens of models tested, consensus picks emerge clearly by use case. Three-season versatility with a 20F rating remains the sweet spot for most backpackers, and down insulation dominates the category — every bag in our top 8 uses goose or duck down, with fill powers ranging from 550 to 950+. [src1, src2, src3, src4]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Feathered Friends Swallow UL 20 vs Western Mountaineering UltraLite 20
Both are US-made cottage-brand benchmarks at the $610-660 price point. The Swallow UL is lighter (1 lb 10.6 oz vs 1 lb 13 oz) with higher fill power (950+ vs 850+) and a newer 7D Pertex Quantum Pro shell after the 2026 redesign. The UltraLite trades a few ounces for decades-long durability and continuous baffles you can shift to fit conditions. [src1, src2, src4]

**Pick the Swallow UL if:** weight is the deciding factor and you want the absolute lightest 20F bag with mainstream availability.
**Pick the UltraLite if:** you expect 10+ years of use, want to tune down distribution per trip, and care more about heirloom durability than 3 oz of weight.

### REI Co-op Magma 15 vs Therm-a-Rest Parsec 20 (2026)
Both target serious backpackers under $500. The Magma 15 ($399) is heavier (2 lb 3.6 oz vs 1 lb 13 oz) with 850-fill down, but offers nine unisex sizes and an ISO comfort rating. The 2026 Parsec is $70 more expensive but 6.6 oz lighter and uses 900-fill hydrophobic Muscovy down. [src1, src5, src8]

**Pick the Magma 15 if:** budget is firm at ~$400, fit matters (REI's nine sizes cover edge cases), and you value REI's satisfaction guarantee.
**Pick the Parsec 20 if:** weight savings justify $70 more, you backpack in wet conditions (hydrophobic 900-fill + recycled 20D nylon shell), and you want a 2026-spec bag at Amazon retail.

### Kelty Cosmic Down 20 vs Mountain Hardwear Bishop Pass 15
The two best sub-$400 down bags solve different problems. The Cosmic ($210) is 31F ISO comfort with 550-fill — true 3-season summer use. The Bishop Pass ($315-335) is 26F ISO comfort with 650-fill — 5F warmer and 1 oz lighter despite more fill, with a more refined construction. [src1, src3, src4]

**Pick the Cosmic Down 20 if:** budget is hard at <$250 and you camp May-September in mild conditions.
**Pick the Bishop Pass 15 if:** you'll see 30-40F shoulder-season nights and the extra $100 buys real cold-weather capability.

### Sea to Summit Spark 30 vs Nemo Disco 15 Endless Promise
Opposite philosophies in the same comparison set. The Spark 30 (1 lb 1 oz, $349) is the lightest mummy here but rated only to 39F comfort. The Disco 15 (2 lb 11 oz, ~$389) is the heaviest bag in this roundup but uses a spoon shape that fits side sleepers no mummy bag can match. [src1, src2, src4]

**Pick the Spark 30 if:** you're a back/stomach sleeper, hike May-August or shoulder-season only with warm clothing layers, and grams matter more than degrees.
**Pick the Disco 15 if:** you're a side sleeper or restless sleeper, weight is secondary to comfort, and you want a sustainability-focused brand with end-of-life recycling.

### Feathered Friends Swallow UL 20 vs Therm-a-Rest Parsec 20 (2026)
The premium-cottage vs premium-mainstream pairing. The Swallow UL is 3 oz lighter (1 lb 10.6 oz vs 1 lb 13 oz) with 950+ fill, but ships direct from Seattle ($659) with 2-6 week lead times. The Parsec is widely available at $470 with 900-fill hydrophobic Muscovy down and an Amazon supply chain. [src1, src5, src8]

**Pick the Swallow UL if:** you want the lightest 20F bag, will pay $190 more for the cottage-brand cachet, and can wait for direct shipment.
**Pick the Parsec 20 if:** you want a true 2026-spec hydrophobic bag without waiting, value Amazon return policy, and don't need to chase the last 3 oz.

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Fill power escalation**: Premium bags now routinely ship with 900-950+ fill power, up from 800-850 two years ago. The Therm-a-Rest Parsec jumped from 800 to 900 fill in its spring 2026 update. Nemo's Pulse quilt pushes to 1,000-fill power. [src1, src5, src8]
- **Hydrophobic down as standard**: Water-resistant down treatments and DWR shell fabrics are now expected in bags above $300, addressing down's historic weakness in wet conditions. PFAS-free / non-PFC alternatives (Sea to Summit Ultra-Dry Down, Kelty Cosmic) are gaining ground in response to PFAS bans in CA, NY, and the EU. [src1, src4]
- **Quilt adoption growing**: Open-back quilts like the Enlightened Equipment Enigma 20 (~$355, 1 lb 5.2 oz) and Katabatic Alsek 22 now appear on every major roundup alongside traditional mummy bags, reflecting mainstream acceptance of quilt-style sleep systems. [src1, src2, src4, src6]
- **Inclusive sizing expansion**: REI's Magma overhaul dropped gender-specific models for nine unisex sizes, and other brands are following with wider size ranges. [src1, src2]
- **Sustainability commitments**: Nemo's Endless Promise recycling program (Disco ~$389 at current Amazon retail), recycled shell fabrics (Parsec 20D recycled nylon, Kelty Cosmic), and RDS-certified down are becoming table stakes for the $300+ segment. [src1, src2, src4]
- **Mid-2026 price firming**: Through June 2026, the deep launch discounts of early 2026 have eased — the Nemo Disco 15 is back near its $389 Amazon list (it briefly dipped to ~$300 in Q1), while the Bishop Pass 15 holds at $315-335 and budget/premium tiers are otherwise stable. [src2, src4]

## Important Caveats

- Prices shown are MSRP as of April 2026 and fluctuate by retailer and season. REI members get 10% back on full-price purchases. End-of-season sales (Sep-Nov) can cut 20-40% off.
- Temperature ratings use different standards: ISO/EN ratings (comfort vs lower limit) are third-party tested, while non-ISO ratings (Feathered Friends, Western Mountaineering, Enlightened Equipment) are manufacturer-stated. Always compare the same metric.
- Individual warmth varies significantly by metabolism, sleeping pad R-value, shelter type, and clothing layers. Cold sleepers should buy 10-15F warmer than their expected use. Sleeping pad R-value contributes as much as bag rating to total warmth — see the related sleeping pads card.
- Cottage-brand and direct-only bags (Feathered Friends, Western Mountaineering Ultralite is on Amazon, REI Co-op Magma 15 is REI-exclusive, Enlightened Equipment) are typically not on Amazon and ship direct only, often with 2-6 week lead times.
- This comparison focuses on down bags. Synthetic bags weigh more but maintain insulation when wet — a critical advantage for some conditions.
