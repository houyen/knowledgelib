# Best Smartphones (2026)

## What are the best smartphones in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max (~$1,429)** — consensus best overall (Tom's Guide, TechRadar) with 8x optical-quality zoom and 17h54m battery. **Best value: OnePlus 15 (~$849)** — same Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 as the $1,139 S26 Ultra, ~3-day battery, TechRadar 5/5. **Best budget: Google Pixel 10a (~$499)** — 7yr updates, 15h16m battery, full Google AI. [src1, src2]

## Summary

By summer 2026 the market has fully settled, and **the aggressive spring Amazon discounts have largely unwound — street prices have crept back toward MSRP**: the Pixel 10 Pro XL is back to $1,180 (from a spring low near $899), the Pixel 10 Pro to $984, and the iPhone 17 (256GB) to $989. Apple's iPhone 17 Pro Max ($1,429 on Amazon) remains the consensus best phone overall on Tom's Guide and TechRadar, with its triple 48MP camera system (now with 8x optical-quality zoom), A19 Pro chip with vapor-chamber cooling, and 17 hours 54 minutes in Tom's Guide's standardized battery test. Samsung's Galaxy S26 Ultra ($1,139 Amazon, down from $1,299 MSRP) still holds the best Android title and Tom's Guide's "best camera phone" crown thanks to its 200MP f/1.4 main camera, Privacy Display technology, Horizon Lock video stabilization, and 60W charging (0-100% in 43 minutes). TechRadar still rates it 4.75/5. [src1, src2, src3, src4]

With the Pixel discounts gone, the OnePlus 15 ($849 Amazon) is now the standout value flagship — it runs the same Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 as the $1,139 Galaxy S26 Ultra for nearly $300 less, and TechRadar awarded it a perfect 5/5 for nearly three-day endurance from its 7,300mAh silicon-carbon battery (roughly 50% more capacity than the Galaxy S26 Ultra's 5,000mAh or the Pixel 10 Pro XL). Google's Pixel 10 Pro XL ($1,180 Amazon) and the more compact Pixel 10 Pro ($984 Amazon) still pair the Tensor G5 chip with the most accomplished on-device AI software experience available — Tech Advisor and TechRadar both rank the Pixel 10 Pro XL as the finest phone money can buy when price is no object — but at full price they no longer undercut Apple or Samsung. The Xiaomi 17 Ultra (~$999 global) earns NotebookCheck's highest smartphone rating for its 1-inch Light Fusion 1050L camera sensor and Leica optics, but is import-only in the US (no Amazon listing — official Xiaomi global store only). [src1, src2, src5, src6, src8]

For budget buyers, the Google Pixel 10a ($499 Amazon) is the best sub-$500 phone, with 15 hours 16 minutes battery life in Tom's Guide testing and the full Google AI suite. The Motorola Moto G (2026) ($283 Amazon) is the "best cheap phone" winner, posting 19 hours 10 minutes of battery life — the longest of any sub-$300 phone Tom's Guide has tested. The iPhone 17 ($989 for 256GB) offers 120Hz ProMotion and 256GB starting storage — features previously Pro-exclusive — and the Nothing Phone 4a Pro ($600 Amazon) and Samsung Galaxy S25 FE (currently sold out on Amazon, ~$579 when in stock) round out a mid-range that now matches flagships from 18 months ago. The OnePlus 13 (the prior Hasselblad-tuned flagship) is also currently sold out on Amazon. The iPhone Air ($1180) launched as Apple's thinnest phone ever with an A19 Pro chip, but its single 48MP camera limits its appeal for photography enthusiasts. [src1, src2, src5, src6]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max (~$1,429) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/iphone-17-pro-max-best-phones)
The iPhone 17 Pro Max is the consensus best phone of 2026 across Tom's Guide and TechRadar. Its triple 48MP camera system with 8x optical-quality zoom (upgraded from 4x) sets the benchmark for smartphone photography, and 40W wired charging reaches 50% in 20 minutes. The A19 Pro chip with new vapor-chamber cooling delivers class-leading sustained performance, the 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR display peaks at 3,000 nits, and the new two-toned aluminum unibody is a striking design departure. Battery life reached 17 hours 54 minutes in Tom's Guide's standardized web-surfing test. [src1, src2]

### Best Camera Phone: Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra (~$1,139) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-best-phones)
Tom's Guide and TechRadar both name the Galaxy S26 Ultra the best camera phone of 2026. Its 200MP main camera with f/1.4 aperture captures 47% more light than the S24 Ultra, the upgraded 50MP periscope telephoto (f/2.9, up from f/3.4) adds 37% more brightness at 5x zoom, and new Horizon Lock video stabilization keeps footage level during motion. GSMArena's lab tests confirm improved low-light performance, and Privacy Display hides on-screen content from shoulder surfers. The iPhone 17 Pro Max wins for video consistency, but for photographic versatility Samsung leads. [src1, src3, src4, src7]

### Best Android Phone: Google Pixel 10 Pro XL (~$1,180) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/pixel-10-pro-xl)

The 50+48+48MP triple camera with 5x optical zoom rivals the best, and Qi2 wireless charging is now standard. Samsung's Galaxy S26 Ultra still wins for raw camera versatility, but for software polish the Pixel leads. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### iPhone 17 Pro Max vs Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra
The iPhone 17 Pro Max ($1,429 Amazon) wins on video consistency, 8x optical-quality zoom, and 17h54m battery. The S26 Ultra ($1,139 Amazon, down from $1,299 MSRP) wins on photographic versatility (200MP f/1.4 main + Horizon Lock) and is ~$290 cheaper. [src1, src3, src4]
**Pick A if:** you live in the Apple ecosystem or prioritize video quality and zoom range.
**Pick B if:** photography versatility is your top priority and you want the better dollar-per-spec value.

### iPhone 17 Pro Max vs Google Pixel 10 Pro XL
The iPhone 17 Pro Max ($1,429) leads on camera versatility, video, and sustained chip performance. The Pixel 10 Pro XL ($1,180 Amazon, now back near MSRP after the spring discounts ended) leads on AI software (Live Translation, Camera Coach, Gemini Nano) and is ~$250 cheaper. Tech Advisor and TechRadar name the Pixel 10 Pro XL the best phone money can buy when price is no object. [src1, src2, src6]
**Pick A if:** you want the most polished hardware and Apple Intelligence ecosystem integration.
**Pick B if:** you want best-in-class on-device AI software in an Android flagship.

### OnePlus 15 vs Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra
The OnePlus 15 ($849) runs the same Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 as the S26 Ultra ($1,139) for ~$290 less, with a larger 7,300mAh battery (vs 5,000mAh), a brighter 165Hz 4,500-nit display, and TechRadar's perfect 5/5 rating. The S26 Ultra wins decisively on camera (200MP f/1.4, 5x optical, Horizon Lock) and Privacy Display. [src1, src2, src3]
**Pick A if:** you want flagship performance and class-leading battery at the lowest price.
**Pick B if:** camera versatility and the S Pen / Privacy Display matter more than saving $290.

### iPhone 17 vs OnePlus 15
The iPhone 17 ($989 for 256GB) brings iOS, 120Hz ProMotion, dual 48MP cameras, and the longest software support. The OnePlus 15 ($849) is $140 cheaper, a true flagship (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5), and far outlasts it on battery. [src1, src2, src6]
**Pick A if:** you want iOS and the longest update window.
**Pick B if:** Android is fine and you want flagship power plus multi-day battery for less.

### Google Pixel 10a vs Motorola Moto G (2026)
The Pixel 10a ($499 Amazon) brings the full Google AI suite, 7 years of updates, and 15h16m battery. The Moto G 2026 ($283) posts the longest battery in this guide (19h10m) but uses an LCD and 3yr updates. [src1, src5]
**Pick A if:** you want flagship software longevity and AI features at a sub-$500 price.
**Pick B if:** maximum battery and a sub-$300 budget are the priority.
