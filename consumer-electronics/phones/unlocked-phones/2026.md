# Best Unlocked Phones (2026)

## What are the best unlocked phones in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Apple iPhone 17 (~$799) — PCMag's best-value iPhone, A19 chip, 24h31m tested battery, carrier-free on GSM and CDMA networks.**
**Best value: Google Pixel 10a (~$499) — flagship Google AI at a midrange price, 7 years of updates, full unlocked carrier support.**
**Best budget: Samsung Galaxy A17 5G (~$200) — sub-$200 unlocked 5G with 6 years of updates and 15h45m tested battery.**
Unlocked phones let you buy outright and switch carriers (or SIMs abroad) at will. [<a href="https://tech.yahoo.com/phones/articles/best-unlocked-phones-2026-214904280.html">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.tomsguide.com/best-picks/best-unlocked-android-phones">src2</a>]

## Summary

An unlocked phone is one you buy outright — usually from Amazon, the manufacturer, or a retailer — that is not tied to a single carrier, so you can switch networks or pop in a local SIM abroad at will. PCMag's 2026 Editors' Choice unlocked phones are the **Apple iPhone 17**, **Google Pixel 10 Pro**, and **Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7**, with the **Google Pixel 10a** as the top value pick and the **Samsung Galaxy A17 5G** as the budget pick under $200. The iPhone 17 (256GB) starts at ~$799 direct from Apple, the Pixel 10 Pro at ~$999 from the Google Store, and the Galaxy A17 5G is ~$200 on Amazon. [src1, src2]

At the top, the **iPhone 17 Pro Max** (~$1,429) and **Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra** (~$1,049 on Amazon, down from $1,299 MSRP) trade the "best overall" and "best camera" crowns — the Pro Max posts a category-leading 32h11m in PCMag's battery test, while the S26 Ultra's 200MP main camera and S Pen make it the content-creator pick. The **OnePlus 15** (~$999) is the battery champion, with a 7,300mAh silicon-carbon cell delivering ~26 hours of tested endurance and TechRadar/PCMag praise for two-day life and 80W charging — and it undercuts the other flagships on price. The **Pixel 10 Pro** pairs the Tensor G5 chip with the best on-device Google AI and a 7-year update promise. [src1, src3, src4, src6, src7]

For everyone else, the unlocked mid-range and budget tiers are stronger than ever: the **Samsung Galaxy S25 FE** (~$750) delivers a near-flagship experience for less, the **Nothing Phone (4a) Pro** (~$599) brings a distinctive Glyph design and triple 50MP cameras, and the **Motorola Moto G (2026)** (~$238 on Amazon; often under $200 at retail per Tom's Guide) posted 19h+ of battery — the longest of any cheap phone Tom's Guide tested. Foldable fans get the **Galaxy Z Fold 7** (~$1,425) for the book-fold form and the **Motorola Razr Ultra 2025** (~$800) for the most powerful flip. The key caveat across all of them: unlocked only helps if the phone supports your carrier's bands — verify compatibility, especially on Verizon and MVNOs. [src1, src2, src5, src8]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Apple iPhone 17 (~$799) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/iphone-17-unlocked-phones)
PCMag names the unlocked iPhone 17 its "best value among iPhone 17 models" and an Editors' Choice, citing a pro-level 6.3-inch display, flexible dual 48MP cameras, and competitive 24-hour-31-minute tested battery life. Sold unlocked it works on GSM and CDMA networks (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, and MVNOs like Mint and Cricket) — insert a SIM, connect Wi-Fi, and activate. It includes 120Hz ProMotion and 256GB base storage that previous generations charged $200+ more for. [src1, src3]

### Best Camera / Creators: Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra (~$1,049) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-unlocked-phones)
PCMag calls the unlocked S26 Ultra the pick for "content creators and artists" with class-leading cameras, an S Pen stylus, and Galaxy AI. The 200MP main camera plus 50MP/50MP/10MP system, Privacy Display, and Super Fast Charging 3.0 make it the most versatile shooter here. On Amazon it sells unlocked for ~$1,049 (down from a $1,299.99 MSRP, a 19% saving) with a US 1-year warranty. The trade-off versus the iPhone is shorter tested battery (15h5m) and a large, heavy body. [src1, src6]

### Best AI / Photography: Google Pixel 10 Pro (~$999) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/pixel-10-pro-unlocked-phones)
A PCMag Editors' Choice for photography enthusiasts and anyone who avoids frequent upgrades, thanks to seven years of OS updates, excellent cameras, and advanced Gemini AI. The Tensor G5 chip drives 100x Pro Res Zoom, 8K video, and on-device AI, and the unlocked model works on Google Fi, Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, and other major carriers. The 512GB unlocked unit runs ~$999 direct from the Google Store. Its weakness is shorter tested battery (12h40m) than rivals. [src1, src6]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### iPhone 17 vs Google Pixel 10 Pro
PCMag makes both Editors' Choice unlocked phones, but they aim at different buyers. The iPhone 17 (~$799) wins on tested battery (24h31m), iOS ecosystem, the longest software support, and now price; the Pixel 10 Pro (~$999) wins on camera reach (100x Pro Res Zoom), on-device Gemini AI, and a 7-year update promise. [src1, src6]

**Pick iPhone 17 if:** you want iOS, the safest all-rounder, and the best balance of battery and price.
**Pick Pixel 10 Pro if:** you want the best Android AI/camera and don't mind shorter battery life.

### Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra vs OnePlus 15
The S26 Ultra (~$1,049) wins on camera versatility (200MP + S Pen) and brand support breadth across carriers; the OnePlus 15 (~$999) wins decisively on battery (7,300mAh / ~26h vs ~15h) and undercuts it on price while matching performance. [src1, src4, src6, src7]

**Pick Galaxy S26 Ultra if:** photography, the S Pen, and the widest Verizon band support matter most.
**Pick OnePlus 15 if:** you want two-day battery and flagship speed for less money.

### iPhone 17 Pro Max vs Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra
These are the two premium unlocked flagships. The iPhone 17 Pro Max (~$1,429) wins on tested battery (an exceptional 32h11m), video consistency, and 8x optical-quality zoom; the S26 Ultra (~$1,049) wins on raw camera versatility (200MP main, S Pen) and is now $380 cheaper on Amazon. [src1, src3, src6]

**Pick iPhone 17 Pro Max if:** you live in the Apple ecosystem and want maximum battery and pro video.
**Pick Galaxy S26 Ultra if:** photographic versatility and the better dollar-per-spec value win.

### Pixel 10a vs Samsung Galaxy A17 5G
Both are budget unlocked picks. The Pixel 10a (~$499) brings the full Google AI suite, a flagship-grade camera, and 7 years of updates; the Galaxy A17 5G (~$200) is less than half the price with surprisingly good battery (15h45m) and a 6-year update promise. [src1, src2]

**Pick Pixel 10a if:** you want flagship software/camera at a sub-$500 price.
**Pick Galaxy A17 5G if:** sub-$200 is the priority and you just need a reliable daily 5G phone.

### Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 vs Motorola Razr Ultra 2025
Both are PCMag-recommended unlocked foldables in different form factors. The Z Fold 7 (~$1,425) is a book-fold with an 8-inch inner display for tablet-style multitasking and a 200MP camera; the Razr Ultra 2025 (~$800) is the most powerful flip — pocketable, with a 7-inch main screen, Snapdragon 8 Elite, and far better battery (22h3m vs 13h10m) for $600 less. [src1, src5]

**Pick Galaxy Z Fold 7 if:** you want the largest screen and serious multitasking, and price is no object.
**Pick Razr Ultra 2025 if:** you want a compact flip with flagship power and long battery at a lower price.
