# Which Samsung Galaxy Phone Should You Buy? (2026)

## Which Samsung Galaxy phone should you buy in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Galaxy S26 Ultra (~$1,239)** — 200 MP camera, S Pen, 60W charging, and the year's only Galaxy with a feature set that justifies its price [src1, src2]. **Best value: Galaxy A57 5G (~$550)** — Tom's Guide's new mid-range pick, 6.7" 120Hz AMOLED + 45W charging + 6 years of updates [src1, src9]. **Best budget: Galaxy A16 5G (~$180)** — reliable 5G phone with 6 years of software support [src4, src6].

## Summary

Samsung's 2026 Galaxy lineup now spans ten actively sold models from the $200 Galaxy A16 5G to the $2,000 Galaxy Z Fold 7. The Galaxy S26 series — launched March 11, 2026 — headlines the range, powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chipset with displays reaching 2,600 nits peak brightness and, for the first time, differentiated charging speeds across all three tiers (25W/45W/60W). [src5, src8]

The Galaxy S26 Ultra remains the most complete Samsung phone available: a 200 MP quad-camera system, 6.9-inch display, S Pen support, and up to 1 TB storage with 16 GB RAM. The standard Galaxy S26 launched at $900 but has since dropped to ~$734 on Amazon, and compensates with the new Horizon Lock video stabilization feature and a refined 6.3-inch form factor that reviewers call the most practical flagship in the lineup. For foldable fans, the Galaxy Z Fold 7 (~$1,477) and Z Flip 7 remain Samsung's current-generation foldables with thinner builds and improved durability — though with Galaxy Unpacked confirmed for July 22, 2026, their NEW unlocked Amazon stock has thinned (the Z Flip 7 is now cleanest to buy direct from Samsung.com, ~$1,100). [src1, src2, src3, src11]

Mid-range and budget buyers gained a meaningful new option in April 2026: the **Galaxy A57 5G** ($549.99), Tom's Guide's "Best Value" pick for 2026, replaces the A56 as Samsung's flagship-feel mid-ranger with a 6.7" 120Hz Super AMOLED Plus display, Exynos 1680 with upgraded NPU, 45W Super Fast Charging, and six generations of OS updates. The Galaxy A56 remains in the lineup (its 256GB Amazon-exclusive variant still lists around $550, with lower-storage versions cheaper), and the Galaxy A16 5G (~$180 on Amazon) is the entry-level pick with 6 years of software support. The Galaxy S25 FE has dropped to ~$567 and still offers a cheap path into the Samsung flagship software experience, while the 2025 Galaxy S25 Edge sits around ~$574. [src1, src4, src6, src7, src9]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Galaxy S26 Ultra (~$1,239) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s26-ultra-samsung-galaxy-phones)
The Galaxy S26 Ultra is the most complete Samsung phone available in 2026 and the only S26 tier that did not receive a starting-price hike this year. Its 200 MP quad-camera system with 5x optical zoom produces the best photos and videos in the Galaxy lineup, the new 60W wired charging — a first for Samsung flagships — addresses one of the few remaining complaints about the Ultra line, and the world-first Privacy Display narrows viewing angles to block prying eyes in offices, commutes, or planes. Tradeoff: independent display testing in April 2026 found peak brightness drops by ~300 nits when Privacy mode is active, and some users report eye strain — there is no calibration option. The S Pen, 6.9-inch display, and up to 1 TB storage still make it the choice for power users who want everything. [src1, src2, src3, src10]

### Best Compact Flagship: Galaxy S26 (~$734) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s26)
The standard Galaxy S26 delivers the same Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor and 2,600-nit display as its larger siblings in a manageable 6.3-inch form factor. The new Horizon Lock video stabilization feature keeps footage level during movement, making it a standout for casual videographers. Reviewers call it the most practical choice for users who want flagship performance without a massive phone. [src1, src4, src8]

### Best Big-Screen Flagship: Galaxy S26+ (~$1,025) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s26-plus-samsung-galaxy-phones)
The Galaxy S26+ splits the difference between the S26 and S26 Ultra with a 6.7-inch display, 45W charging, and the same 50 MP triple-camera system as the standard S26. It offers more screen real estate and longer battery life than the S26 without the Ultra's higher price tag. Best for users who want a large display but do not need the Ultra's zoom capabilities or S Pen. [src5, src8]

### Best Foldable: Galaxy Z Fold 7 (~$1,477) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-z-fold-7-samsung-galaxy-phones)
The Galaxy Z Fold 7 is noticeably thinner and lighter than the Fold 6, directly addressing the biggest complaint about Samsung's book-fold phones. It adopts the 200 MP main sensor from the S25 Ultra and runs on the Snapdragon 8 Elite, making it genuinely capable as both a phone and a tablet. Best for multitaskers and professionals who benefit from a large inner screen. [src2, src3]

### Best Compact Foldable: Galaxy Z Flip 7 (~$1,100) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-z-flip-7)
The Z Flip 7 features a completely revamped 4.1-inch square cover screen (up from 3.4 inches on the Flip 6), making it far more usable without opening the phone. It remains the most affordable way to get a Samsung foldable experience with 50 MP cameras and the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor. With NEW unlocked stock thin on Amazon ahead of the July 22 Unpacked, the cleanest way to buy one today is direct from Samsung.com (~$1,100). [src1, src3, src6, src11]

### Best Value Flagship: Galaxy S25 FE (~$567) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-s25-fe)
Now around $567 on Amazon (down from its $649 launch price), the Galaxy S25 FE delivers a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display, 7 years of software support, Galaxy AI features, and a capable triple-camera system. The 4,900 mAh battery with 45W charging outperforms most phones at this price. It is the best entry point to the Samsung flagship experience without crossing $700. [src4, src6, src7]

### Best Mid-Range Value: Galaxy A57 5G (~$550) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-a57-5g)
Released April 9, 2026 at $549.99 and named Tom's Guide's "Best Value" Samsung phone of the year, the Galaxy A57 5G upgrades the A56 with a 6.7-inch 120Hz Super AMOLED Plus display with Vision Booster, the new Exynos 1680 chipset with upgraded NPU for on-device AI, a 50 MP main camera with OIS (a first at this tier), 45W Super Fast Charging in the box, and Samsung's six generations of OS upgrades. At 6.9mm thin and 179g, it is also noticeably slimmer than its predecessor. Best for buyers who want flagship-feel features without crossing $600. [src1, src9]

### Cheaper Mid-Range Alternative: Galaxy A56 5G (~$550) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-a56-samsung-galaxy-phones)
With the A57 launched, the A56 lives on as the prior-generation mid-ranger. Its 256GB Amazon-exclusive variant currently lists around $550 (roughly level with the newer A57), though lower-storage versions and carrier deals often sell for less, so it's worth a strong pick when discounted. It offers a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED display with Gorilla Glass Victus+ protection, IP67 water resistance, and Samsung's 6 years of software updates. The Exynos 1580 handles daily multitasking well, and the 50 MP camera produces solid photos in good light. Best for users who find it discounted below the A57. [src2, src4]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Galaxy S26 Ultra vs Galaxy S26
The Ultra is the only S26 tier that did not see a price hike for 2026; the base S26 launched at $900 but now street-prices around $734. The Ultra adds 200 MP main + dual telephoto (3x and 5x periscope), Privacy Display, S Pen, and 60W charging vs the S26's 50 MP triple camera, 6.3-inch screen, and 25W charging. Multiple 2026 reviews call the Ultra the only S26 with a feature set that justifies its premium this year. [src1, src2]
**Pick S26 Ultra if:** photography, S Pen workflows, or large screen matter, and you can stretch to ~$1,239.
**Pick S26 if:** you want flagship performance in a one-hand-friendly 6.3-inch body and can accept the slower 25W charging.

### Galaxy S26 vs Galaxy S25 FE
The S26 (~$734) and S25 FE (~$567) overlap more than ever in 2026: both run Galaxy AI, both offer 120Hz AMOLED displays, both get long software support. The S26 wins on processor (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 vs Exynos 2400), screen brightness (2,600 vs 1,900 nits), and the new Horizon Lock video stabilization; the S25 FE wins on battery (4,900 vs 4,000 mAh) and charging speed (45W vs 25W) — and saves you about $170. [src4, src6, src7]
**Pick S26 if:** you want the latest chipset, brightest display, and S26-only AI features.
**Pick S25 FE if:** you prioritize battery life and value, and want to spend under $600.

### Galaxy A57 5G vs Galaxy S25 FE
This is the toughest call in the mid-tier — and the gap has all but closed now that the S25 FE has dropped to ~$567. The A57 (~$550) offers 45W charging, OIS on the main camera, and a 6.9mm thin design; the S25 FE brings flagship-tier Galaxy AI features, a brighter AMOLED, and the more recent Exynos 2400 with better gaming performance for only about $20 more. [src1, src7, src9]
**Pick A57 5G if:** you want the newest mid-range Samsung with OIS camera at the lowest price.
**Pick S25 FE if:** you want full Galaxy AI features and a true flagship-tier processor.

### Galaxy Z Flip 7 vs Galaxy S26
At ~$1,100 (Samsung.com) vs ~$734, the Z Flip 7 now commands a sizable premium over the S26 — its NEW unlocked Amazon stock has dried up ahead of the July 22 Unpacked, so Samsung.com is the cleanest source and the price has settled back near its $1,099 launch. In exchange you get a compact clamshell design and a 4.1-inch cover screen that handles notifications, payments, and quick replies without unfolding, at the cost of a smaller 4,000 mAh battery. Both phones run on Snapdragon 8 Elite-tier silicon. [src1, src3, src11]
**Pick Z Flip 7 if:** pocketability and the clamshell novelty matter enough to pay a premium for a foldable without spending Z Fold money.
**Pick S26 if:** you want maximum battery life, the latest chipset, the durability of a traditional slab, and to save roughly $370.

### Galaxy Z Fold 7 vs Galaxy S26 Ultra
The Fold 7 (~$1,477) now sits about $240 above the S26 Ultra (~$1,239) and trades the Ultra's S Pen + best-in-class cameras for a 7.6-inch tablet-sized inner screen. Fold 7 borrows the Ultra's 200 MP main sensor but lacks the long telephoto and Privacy Display. [src2, src3]
**Pick Z Fold 7 if:** you want a tablet you can fold into a phone for productivity and media.
**Pick S26 Ultra if:** you want the best camera in the Galaxy lineup, S Pen support, and a more durable single-pane design.
