# Best Foldable Phones (2026)

## What are the best foldable phones in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 (~$1,547)** — best overall book-style foldable with 200MP camera, thinnest Fold ever, and 7 years of updates. **Best value: Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 (~$1020)** — Snapdragon 8 Elite + 165Hz flip display at well below its launch MSRP. **Best budget: Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 FE (~$600)** — Samsung's flip foldable now genuinely affordable. [src1, src2, src13]

## Summary

The foldable phone market in 2026 has matured significantly, with lighter builds, more durable hinges, larger cover screens, and software finally optimized for folding form factors. The category splits into two main designs: book-style foldables that unfold into tablet-sized screens (7.9-10 inches), and clamshell flip phones that fold compact for pocketability. Samsung continues to dominate market share with three distinct foldable tiers, while Google, Honor, and Motorola offer compelling alternatives. The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 (~$1,547 for 512GB at Amazon, $1,999 MSRP) is the consensus best overall book-style foldable with a 200MP camera, 8-inch inner display, and Snapdragon 8 Elite processor, while the Honor Magic V5 (~$1,500 international) challenges it as the thinnest book-style foldable at just 8.8mm folded with a massive 5,820mAh battery. [src1, src2, src5]

For flip-phone lovers, the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 (~$1,020 retail / $1,099 MSRP) leads with a new edge-to-edge 4.1-inch cover screen and the thinnest profile yet at 6.5mm unfolded, while the Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 has now dropped to ~$1020 at Amazon (from $1,299 MSRP) — making it the strongest flip-phone value of 2026 with a 165Hz inner display and Snapdragon 8 Elite. Budget seekers now have multiple real options under $700: the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 FE at ~$600, the Motorola Razr+ 2025 at ~$600, and the Motorola Razr 2025 at ~$550. The most ambitious device is the Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold (~$2,899), a tri-fold phone that unfurls from 6.5 inches to a 10-inch tablet, sold only through Samsung.com (currently sold out). The Motorola Razr Ultra 2026 ($1,499) is now shipping and wins flip battery tests, but the reviewer consensus is that the 2025 model at ~$1020 remains the better value; Motorola also launched a separate book-style Razr Fold ($1,899, US retail May 21) with the #1 DXOMARK foldable camera. [src1, src4, src6, src7, src11, src13]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 (~$1,547) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-z-fold-7)
The Galaxy Z Fold 7 earns the top spot from Tom's Guide, Android Authority, and multiple other reviewers as the best all-around foldable phone in 2026. It is the thinnest Galaxy Fold ever at 4.2mm unfolded and 8.9mm folded, with a stunning 8-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X inner display and a usable 6.5-inch cover screen. The 200MP main camera is a massive upgrade over previous Fold generations, and the Snapdragon 8 Elite processor with 12GB RAM handles multitasking with up to three split-screen windows seamlessly. It ships with Android 16 and One UI 8, with seven years of OS and security updates guaranteed. [src1, src2, src3]

### Best Battery Life: Honor Magic V5 (~$1,500) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/honor-magic-v5)
The Honor Magic V5 uses silicon-carbon battery technology to pack a massive 5,820mAh cell into a frame that measures just 8.8mm folded and 4.1mm unfolded, making it the thinnest book-style foldable on the market. Tom's Guide called it "just as good as the Galaxy Z Fold 7 and Pixel 10 Pro Fold," with a 7.95-inch BOE OLED inner display hitting 5,000 nits peak brightness. The 50MP primary camera with a 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto delivers versatile photography, and 66W wired plus 50W wireless charging ensures fast top-ups. Available globally, though US buyers may need to import. [src2, src4, src5]

### Best Camera Processing: Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold (~$1,750) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/google-pixel-10-pro-fold)
Google's second-generation foldable brings several firsts to the category: IP68 water and dust resistance (the first book-style foldable to achieve this), Qi2 magnetic wireless charging, and a 5x optical telephoto. Powered by the Tensor G5 chip with 16GB RAM, it excels at computational photography with Google's AI-driven photo processing, Night Sight, Magic Eraser, and Best Take features. The 8-inch Super Actua Flex inner display and 6.4-inch cover display both hit 3,000 nits peak brightness. At 258g it is heavier than competitors, but the IP68 durability and Google's software polish make it the most worry-free foldable to use daily. [src1, src2, src8]

### Best Tablet Experience: Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold (~$2,899) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-z-trifold)
The most innovative foldable of 2026, the Galaxy Z TriFold unfolds from a standard 6.5-inch phone into a massive 10-inch tablet with a near-4:3 aspect ratio. Powered by Snapdragon 8 Elite with 16GB RAM and a 5,600mAh battery with 45W charging (a first for Samsung foldables), it targets power users who want a true tablet replacement. The dual-hinge design folds to just 3.9mm at its thinnest point when fully open. At $2,899 for the 512GB model, it is firmly ultra-premium, and it sold out within minutes of its US launch in January 2026. Currently only available through Samsung.com. [src1, src6]

### Best Flip Phone: Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7 (~$1,020) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-z-flip-7)
The Z Flip 7 represents the biggest generational leap for Samsung's clamshell line, with a new edge-to-edge 4.1-inch cover display that makes the phone genuinely usable without opening it. At just 6.5mm unfolded and 13.7mm folded, it is the slimmest Galaxy Flip ever. The 6.9-inch inner AMOLED display runs at 120Hz with 2,600 nits peak brightness. Samsung DeX support is new for the Flip line, enabling a desktop-like experience when connected to a monitor. The 50MP main camera with Dual Pixel autofocus and OIS produces excellent results. [src1, src2, src4]

### Best Flip Value: Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 (~$800) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/motorola-razr-ultra-2025)
The Razr Ultra 2025 pushes flip phone displays to new limits with a 7.0-inch Super HD pOLED inner display running at 165Hz — the highest refresh rate on any foldable — and a 4.0-inch OLED cover screen that spans the entire front half. The Snapdragon 8 Elite processor with 16GB RAM and up to 1TB storage makes it the most powerful flip phone available. The 4,700mAh battery with 68W TurboPower charging (50% in 8 minutes) solves the perennial flip phone battery anxiety. Dual 50MP cameras (wide + ultrawide) round out a premium package. With the price now dropped to ~$800 at Amazon (from $1,299 MSRP) — and the Razr Ultra 2026 launching at $1,499 with marginal upgrades — this is the strongest flagship-flip value of 2026. [src1, src4, src7, src11]

### Best Budget Foldable: Motorola Razr (2025) (~$600) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/motorola-razr-2025)
The base Motorola Razr 2025 brings foldable technology to a sub-$700 price point without feeling like a compromise — and current Amazon retail is down to ~$600 from a $700 MSRP. It features a 6.9-inch AMOLED inner display, a 3.6-inch external OLED screen, and a 50MP main camera with a 13MP ultrawide. The MediaTek Dimensity 7300X processor handles everyday tasks smoothly, and the 4,500mAh battery provides all-day endurance. While it lacks the flagship processor and premium materials of the Ultra, it delivers the core flip phone experience — compact when closed, full-screen when open — at a price that makes foldables accessible to mainstream buyers. [src1, src2, src4]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 vs Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold
The Z Fold 7 (~$1,547) wins on camera resolution (200MP vs 48MP), thinness (8.9mm vs ~10.5mm folded), and software longevity (7 years of updates from One UI 8). The Pixel 10 Pro Fold (~$1,750, now sold on Amazon) wins on durability — it is the first book-style foldable with full IP68 — and computational photography (Tensor G5 + Night Sight, Magic Eraser, Best Take). The Pixel also adds Qi2 magnetic wireless charging and a true 5x optical telephoto. [src1, src2, src8]
**Pick Z Fold 7 if:** you want the highest-resolution camera, the thinnest book-style design, and Samsung DeX for multitasking.
**Pick Pixel 10 Pro Fold if:** you live near water/dust, value AI photo features, and prefer Google's software polish over Samsung's One UI.

### Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 vs Honor Magic V5
The Z Fold 7 (~$1,547) ships in the US through carriers with full 5G band support and the Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy. The Honor Magic V5 (~$1,500 international) wins on battery (5,820mAh vs 4,400mAh — a 32% advantage thanks to silicon-carbon tech) and pure thinness (8.8mm vs 8.9mm folded). The Honor also has a 50MP 3.5x periscope telephoto where the Fold 7 uses 10MP 3x. [src2, src5]
**Pick Z Fold 7 if:** you are in the US, want full carrier compatibility (especially Verizon), and need a 200MP main camera.
**Pick Honor Magic V5 if:** battery life is your #1 priority and you can import / use T-Mobile or AT&T (no Verizon mmWave).

### Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 vs Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 7
At current retail, the Razr Ultra 2025 (~$800) is now ~$220 cheaper than the Z Flip 7 (~$1,020) while offering a faster 165Hz inner display, a 4,700mAh battery (vs 4,300mAh), and the same Snapdragon 8 Elite chip. The Z Flip 7 wins on cover-screen integration (genuine edge-to-edge 4.1-inch panel), Samsung DeX support, and 7 years of OS updates vs Motorola's 4. [src1, src4, src7]
**Pick Razr Ultra 2025 if:** you want the best flip-phone hardware for ~$800 and value 165Hz + battery life.
**Pick Z Flip 7 if:** you prioritize cover-screen usability, longer software support, and Samsung ecosystem features.

### Motorola Razr Ultra 2025 vs Motorola Razr Ultra 2026
The 2026 model ($1,499, now shipping) is a ~$700 premium over the discounted 2025 model (~$800 at Amazon) for a larger 5,000mAh battery and a LOFIC camera sensor upgrade. Same Snapdragon 8 Elite, same displays, same form factor. The 2026 now wins flip battery tests, but reviewers (Android Central, MakeUseOf, SlashGear) still flag the value gap versus the discounted 2025. [src11]
**Pick Razr Ultra 2025 if:** you want flagship flip hardware today at half the price of the 2026 model.
