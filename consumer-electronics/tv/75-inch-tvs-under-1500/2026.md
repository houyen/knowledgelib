## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### TCL 75QM7K vs Hisense 75U8N
Both deliver ~3,000-nit Mini-LED at $999-1,300. RTINGS testing shows the TCL has cleaner local-dimming algorithms and slightly better shadow detail; the Hisense wins on HDR color volume and Dolby Vision IQ ambient adaptation. The TCL ships with Google TV + Dolby Atmos by Onkyo; the Hisense has IMAX Enhanced. [src1, src8]
**Pick TCL 75QM7K if:** you want the best all-around Mini-LED at this price with the strongest dimming algorithm.
**Pick Hisense 75U8N if:** you watch mostly Dolby Vision content in a dark room and want richer HDR colors.

### Hisense 75U8QG vs TCL 75QM8K
Both peak above 5,000 nits, both now sit comfortably under $1,500 ($1,299 vs $1,398). The U8QG runs native 165Hz with VRR 288 and 4.1.2 audio; the QM8K runs 144Hz with anti-reflective wide-angle screen and 2.1.2 audio. The U8QG is now ~$100 cheaper *and* better-specced on refresh and audio — so the QM8K's wide-angle filter is the only reason to pay more, and only if seating is spread across a wide room. [src4, src7, src8]
**Pick Hisense 75U8QG if:** you want the best value 5,000-nit TV, higher refresh, and have a roughly centered viewing position.
**Pick TCL 75QM8K if:** off-angle viewing matters and reflections in your room are heavy.

### Samsung QN75QN90D vs Hisense 75U8QG
Diverged on price: the Samsung is now ~$1,648 (above bracket) vs the Hisense at $1,299. The Samsung wins on gaming ecosystem (4× HDMI 2.1, Game Hub, FreeSync Premium Pro) but caps at ~2,000 nits and 144Hz. The Hisense U8QG hits 5,000 nits at 165Hz for $350 less, but with only 2× HDMI 2.1. [src2, src6]
**Pick Samsung QN75QN90D if:** you run multiple consoles/PC, need 4× HDMI 2.1, and can catch it on sale near $1,500.
**Pick Hisense 75U8QG if:** the room is bright, one HDMI 2.1 console is enough, and value matters — it's the cheaper, brighter pick.

### Samsung 77S90D vs LG 77B5 (77-inch OLED matchup)
Both are 77-inch OLEDs in the $1,400-1,600 sale window at Best Buy. The Samsung S90D is QD-OLED with 144Hz, 1,200-nit peak, and the NQ4 AI Gen2 processor; the LG B5 is WOLED at 120Hz with ~700-nit peak, webOS 25, and Dolby Vision. Samsung supports HDR10+ but not Dolby Vision; LG supports Dolby Vision but not HDR10+. [src9, src10]
**Pick Samsung 77S90D if:** you want maximum brightness, 144Hz gaming, and QD-OLED color.
**Pick LG 77B5 if:** you watch a lot of Dolby Vision streaming content and prefer webOS over Tizen.

### Hisense 75U75QG vs TCL 75QM7K (~$1,000 Mini-LED matchup)
At ~$1,001 (U75QG) vs ~$998-1,299 (QM7K), this is the around-$1,000 Mini-LED decision. The U75QG runs native 165Hz and Dolby Vision IQ; the QM7K runs 144Hz with stronger local dimming and Dolby Atmos by Onkyo. Tom's Guide ranked the U75QG as their go-to gaming TV; RTINGS ranked the QM7K higher for picture quality. [src7, src8]
**Pick Hisense 75U75QG if:** gaming is primary (165Hz, VRR 288).
**Pick TCL 75QM7K if:** mixed use with better local dimming and audio.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of late June 2026. Prices drop significantly during sales events (Prime Day, Black Friday) and when new model years launch. The 77" OLED prices and the Samsung QN90D in particular are sale-dependent — verify current price before purchase.
- 77-inch OLED is 3 cm (1.2 inches) wider than 75-inch LED. Confirm wall mount and cabinet dimensions before swapping panel types.
- True 75-inch OLED still does not exist under $1500 — OLEDs at this price are 77-inch. Plan for the size delta.
- Brightness figures (nits) are peak measurements in small HDR highlights. Full-screen sustained brightness is significantly lower (typically 30-50% of peak).
- Smart TV platforms collect viewing data by default. Review privacy settings during initial setup.
- Wall mounting a 75-77 inch TV requires a VESA-compatible mount rated for the TV's weight (typically 50-80 lbs).
- Samsung 77S90D: the standalone Amazon listing (B0CVS38Q4M) is live again at ~$1,578 (above $1,500) after a brief period when Amazon had reassigned the ASIN. For sub-$1,500 standalone pricing, buy at Best Buy on sale (~$1,499) or Samsung direct.
