## What are the best TVs for gaming in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: LG C6 OLED (~$1,799)** -- 4K 165Hz, 9.1ms input lag, Dolby Vision Gaming, four HDMI 2.1, now dropped below the LG G5 as Amazon discounts deepen. **Best value: LG C5 OLED (~$1,299)** -- lowest tested OLED price with 5.9ms input lag and four HDMI 2.1 ports. **Best budget: TCL QM8K (~$998)** -- 144Hz Mini-LED at 5.2ms input lag, the cheapest seriously fast gaming TV. ## Summary

The gaming TV market in late June 2026 has shifted further since the May update: continued discounting has pulled the LG C6 down to ~$1,799 on Amazon -- now cheaper than the 2025 LG G5 (~$2,299) and only ~$500 above the LG C5. The Samsung S95H OLED ($3,399 for 65") is Samsung's 2026 flagship, sold at Samsung.com, Best Buy and Abt but still NOT listed on Amazon US as of late June (its buy link points to the official Samsung product page); it brings 2,553 nits peak HDR (30% brighter than the S95F), enhanced Glare Free coating, 4K at 165Hz across four HDMI 2.1 ports, FreeSync Premium Pro plus G-Sync, and HDR10+ Advanced -- still no Dolby Vision and still awaiting independent lab input lag testing. The LG G6 OLED (now ~$2,799 at LG.com, down from $3,399 MSRP) leads tested gaming performance with a lab-measured 8.9ms input lag in Boost mode, 4K at 165Hz, G-Sync/FreeSync, Dolby Vision Gaming, and 2,471 nits peak HDR -- also still not on Amazon, sold at LG and Best Buy (its buy link points to the official LG product page). [src1, src2, src4, src9, src10]

The 2025 Samsung S95F OLED (~$2,198 on Amazon) still wins on tested gaming value at the high end -- ~5ms input lag with VRR enabled (lowest measured), 4K 165Hz, and G-Sync. The LG C5 OLED holds at ~$1,299, the cheapest tested OLED with 5.9ms input lag at 120Hz, 4K 144Hz, four HDMI 2.1 ports, and Dolby Vision Gaming. The LG C6 has now dropped to ~$1,799 on Amazon -- only a ~$500 premium over the C5 for 165Hz and brighter HDR, and the strongest mid-range OLED pick at this price. Budget-focused gamers should look at the TCL QM8K (~$998, 38% off its launch), the Hisense U8QG (~$1,200, 165Hz, 9.7ms input lag) for Mini-LED brightness, or the LG B5 OLED (~$997, back in stock on Amazon but low inventory). ## Best for Each Use Case

### Best New 2026 Flagship: Samsung S95H OLED (~$3,399 at Samsung.com) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-s95h-oled)
Samsung's 2026 flagship is sold at Samsung.com, Best Buy and Abt but is still NOT listed on Amazon US as of late June 2026 -- the buy link points to the official Samsung product page. The S95H pushes QD-OLED brightness to 2,553 nits peak HDR (30% brighter than the S95F), adds HDR10+ Advanced, enhanced Glare Free coating with improved black retention, and a new FloatLayer frame design. Gaming specs carry over from the S95F: 4K at 165Hz, four HDMI 2.1 ports, FreeSync Premium Pro, G-Sync, ALLM. No independent lab input lag measurement is available yet -- expect results similar to or better than the S95F's ~5ms. The key trade-off: no Dolby Vision, and at $3,399 it costs ~$1,200 more than the discounted S95F. Early adopters wanting the latest Samsung tech should consider it; value-seekers should grab the deeply-discounted S95F. [src9, src10]

### Best Tested Premium: LG G6 OLED (~$2,799 at LG.com) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lg-g6-oled)
The LG G6 is the tested gaming performance leader. TechRadar measures 8.9ms input lag in Boost mode -- the lowest of any OLED tested -- with 4K at 165Hz across all four HDMI 2.1 ports, full FreeSync and G-Sync certification, Dolby Vision Gaming, and HGiG HDR. The second-generation Primary RGB Tandem panel hits 2,471 nits peak HDR (Filmmaker Mode). It has now dropped to ~$2,799 at LG.com (from $3,399 MSRP) -- undercutting the S95H while adding Dolby Vision Gaming (critical for Xbox Series X) and verified lab measurements. Sold at LG and Best Buy but still not on Amazon as of late June 2026, so the buy link points to the official LG product page. [src4, src7]

### Best Tested Value Flagship: Samsung S95F OLED (~$2,198) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-s95f-oled)
Samsung's 2025 QD-OLED flagship holds at ~$2,198 on Amazon, with the S95H successor selling at Samsung/Best Buy. It still delivers the best tested gaming-vs-price ratio at the top end: 4K at 165Hz, ~5ms input lag with VRR enabled (lowest of any 65" TV tested), FreeSync Premium Pro plus G-Sync, ~2,200 nits peak HDR, and the matte Glare Free coating, it excels for both console and PC gaming. RTINGS gives it a 9.3/10 gaming score. The single trade-off remains: no Dolby Vision support (HDR10+ only). Expect steeper discounts through Prime Day in July. [src1, src2, src5]

### Best Overall Value: LG C5 OLED (~$1,299) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lg-c5-oled)
The C5 holds at ~$1,299 on Amazon, still clear of every other OLED on price-per-spec. Four HDMI 2.1 ports support 4K 144Hz, full VRR including FreeSync Premium and G-Sync, and Dolby Vision Gaming. Input lag measures ~5.9ms at 4K 120Hz in Game Optimizer mode -- still actually faster than the new C6 in like-for-like 120Hz testing. Tom's Guide and RTINGS both still rank it as the best gaming TV most people should buy. [src1, src2, src3]

### Best New Mid OLED: LG C6 OLED (~$1,799) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lg-c6-oled)
The C6 has dropped from $2,699 at launch to ~$1,799 on Amazon, narrowing its premium over the C5 to ~$500 -- and now undercutting the 2025 LG G5 (~$2,299). Lab-reviewed in April 2026: 9.1ms input lag in Boost mode, 4K at 165Hz (up from 144Hz on the C5), four HDMI 2.1 ports, full G-Sync/FreeSync, Dolby Vision Gaming, and 1,355 nits peak HDR -- a ~25% brightness jump over the C5. Tom's Guide calls it "an absolute powerhouse" and What Hi-Fi praises the brighter, richer image. At its new ~$1,799 street price the C6 is the strongest mid-range OLED pick. The 77"+ C6H sizes use LG's RGB Tandem 2.0 panel for an even bigger brightness gain. [src3, src6, src7]

### Best for PS5: Sony BRAVIA 8 II (~$2,698) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sony-bravia-8-ii)
Sony's QD-OLED flagship includes PS5-exclusive features like Auto HDR Tone Mapping and Auto Genre Picture Switch that automatically optimize the picture for PlayStation gaming. Supports 4K 120Hz, VRR, ALLM, and Dolby Vision Gaming. Input lag is ~10ms with VRR -- competitive but slightly higher than LG and Samsung rivals -- and HDMI 2.1 is limited to two ports. Now ~$2,698 on Amazon (down from $3,299 list), with low stock. [src2, src5, src8]
