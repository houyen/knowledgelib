# Best Wireless Earbuds Over $150 (2026)

## What are the best wireless earbuds over $150 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Sony WF-1000XM6 (~$378) — 88% measured ANC, 9h 41min battery, LDAC; the most balanced flagship, but now the priciest.**
**Best value: Technics EAH-AZ100 (~$148) — cheapest hi-res LDAC flagship, triple-point multipoint, 10h 40min battery.**
**Best ANC: Apple AirPods Pro 3 (~$179) — 90% measured noise reduction (highest SoundGuys has tested) at a now-bargain street price.**
[<a href="https://www.rtings.com/headphones/reviews/best/wireless-earbuds">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.soundguys.com/best-wireless-earbuds-2-14313/">src2</a>]

## Summary

The premium wireless earbuds market above $150 reshuffled hard again in June 2026 on street prices, and this time the high-value picks crashed. The Sony WF-1000XM6 (~$378, up from ~$298) remains the top-ranked earbud on RTINGS and SoundGuys — 88% average noise reduction, a 4.8/5 sound quality score, and 9h 41min measured battery with ANC on — but it is now the priciest mainstream flagship, so it is the best-overall, not the best-value, pick. The new value story is at the bottom of the price ladder: the Technics EAH-AZ100 crashed to ~$148 (from ~$248), making it the cheapest hi-res LDAC flagship in the comparison while keeping its category-leading 10h 40min battery and unmatched triple-point multipoint. The Apple AirPods Pro 3 also dropped to ~$179 (from ~$229) and still hold SoundGuys' highest-ever measured ANC at 90% — at that price they are the best-value ANC pick for iPhone owners. [src1, src2, src6, src7, src8] 

For noise-feel, the Bose QC Ultra Earbuds 2nd Gen (~$249) still deliver the most subjectively immersive silencing, though battery remains a weak point at 6h. On Android, the Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless 4 (~$150) and Sony WF-1000XM5 (~$190) are now both back in the value bracket — the Sennheiser adds aptX Lossless, the XM5 stronger ANC. JBL Tour Pro 3 fell back to ~$250 (down from ~$330), returning to MSRP and back into contention for its case-as-transmitter trick. [src1, src2, src3, src5, src9]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Sony WF-1000XM6 (~$378) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sony-wf-1000xm6)
At ~$378 (up from ~$298 — it has climbed past launch MSRP as demand outstrips supply), the XM6 are RTINGS' top-ranked wireless earbuds and SoundGuys' best overall pick. SoundGuys measured 88% average noise reduction and a 4.8/5 MDAQS sound quality score. New 8.4mm driver, dual noise sensors sampling at 50,000 Hz, and a redesigned (11% smaller) body. Tested 9h 41min battery with ANC on. Bluetooth 5.3 with LDAC, LC3, and Auracast. They remain the best earbuds you can buy on raw merit — but at $378 they are now a clear premium splurge, and the Technics AZ100 or AirPods Pro 3 deliver ~90% of the experience for under half the money. [src1, src2, src6, src7, src8]

### Best ANC / Apple: Apple AirPods Pro 3 (~$179) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/apple-airpods-pro-3)
Now ~$179 (down from $249 launch — a 28% cut). Apple's H3 chip delivers the best ANC SoundGuys has ever measured — 90% average noise reduction, ahead of Sony's XM6. Built-in heart-rate sensor for workouts and real-time live translation across iOS-supported languages. SoundGuys tested 8h 42min battery with ANC on. IP57 water/dust resistance. Seamless Apple ecosystem switching. At the new $179 street price, this is the single best value in the comparison if you live on iOS — class-leading ANC for under half the Sony XM6's price. [src2, src3, src5]

### Best ANC Feel: Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds 2nd Gen (~$249) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/bose-qc-ultra-earbuds-2)
Holding at ~$249 (down from $299 at launch — a 17% drop). Bose's second-generation QC Ultra still deliver the most immersive noise silencing experience in any earbuds, with improved ActiveSense adaptive ANC and AI-based call noise suppression. Bluetooth 5.4 with LE Audio. Immersive Audio with head tracking. The 6h ANC battery is the trade-off — bring the case if you commute long distances. [src2, src3, src5]

### Best Value (Android, aptX Lossless): Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless 4 (~$150) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sennheiser-momentum-tw4)

At $150 it ties the Technics AZ100 (~$148) at the bottom of the price ladder — pick the Sennheiser for warm tuning and aptX Lossless, the Technics for LDAC, multipoint, and longer battery. [src2, src3, src4]

LDAC and LC3 for hi-res wireless. Dolby Atmos with head tracking. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Sony WF-1000XM6 vs Apple AirPods Pro 3
At ~$378 vs ~$179, the AirPods Pro 3 are now $199 cheaper than the XM6 — less than half the price — and still measure 2 points higher on ANC (90% vs 88%, per SoundGuys). The XM6 wins on cross-platform support (LDAC for Android, multi-device), driver-tier sound quality (4.8/5 MDAQS), and 9h 41min battery vs 8h 42min. On iPhone, the AirPods Pro 3 are the obvious buy — the XM6 only makes sense on Android, for LDAC, or if the price gap doesn't matter to you. [src1, src2, src5, src7]

**Pick the XM6 if:** you're on Android, want LDAC, or use multiple devices.
**Pick AirPods Pro 3 if:** you're on iPhone, want the best raw ANC number, or want heart-rate + live translation.

### Sony WF-1000XM6 vs Bose QC Ultra Earbuds 2
At ~$378 vs ~$249, both deliver elite ANC — the XM6 measured higher (88% vs ~85%), but the Bose still wins subjectively on noise "feel" per Tom's Guide. Battery is the splitter: XM6 hits 9h 41min with ANC on, the Bose only 6h. The Bose has aptX Adaptive (LDAC-tier on supported phones) but no LDAC, and now undercuts the XM6 by ~$130. [src2, src3, src5, src7]

**Pick the XM6 if:** battery, LDAC, or measured ANC matter most and the higher price is acceptable.
**Pick Bose if:** you commute under 6h/day, want the most immersive ANC sensation, and would rather save ~$130.

### Sennheiser Momentum True Wireless 4 vs Sony WF-1000XM5
At ~$150 vs ~$190, the MTW4 is now $40 cheaper and adds aptX Lossless that the XM5 lacks. The XM5 wins on ANC (around 86-87% vs 83% measured) and slightly better real-world battery. The MTW4 wins on codecs and sound tuning per What Hi-Fi. Both have fallen back into bargain territory — pick on whether you prioritize codecs (MTW4) or ANC (XM5). [src2, src4, src6]

**Pick the MTW4 if:** budget is tight, you have an aptX-Lossless-capable Android phone, or value Sennheiser's warm tuning.
**Pick the XM5 if:** ANC is the top priority or you want Sony's mature app for ~$40 more.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of June 2026. Regional pricing and sales vary 10-25%; spring-summer 2026 saw unusually volatile shifts across this segment — several flagships (Technics, Sennheiser, XM5, Beats) have temporarily fallen below $200 on sale despite this being an "over $150" comparison.
- Hi-res codec support depends on your source device. LDAC requires Android or a compatible DAP. AirPods Pro 3 and Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 are AAC-only — fine for Apple devices, no hi-res wireless option.
- ANC and bass response vary significantly with ear-tip seal. Try multiple tip sizes.
- SoundGuys measured ANC percentages (90% AirPods Pro 3, 88% XM6, etc.) are averages across all frequencies; rankings shift by frequency band. Do not over-index on a single number.
- Sony WF-1000XM6 rose to ~$378 (from ~$298) due to constrained supply — set a price alert if you want it nearer launch MSRP, or buy the Technics AZ100 / AirPods Pro 3 instead for far less.
- Bowers & Wilkins Pi8 battery (5h 10min measured by SoundGuys) is significantly below B&W's 6.5h claim — plan for frequent case top-ups.
- Bose QC Ultra Earbuds 2 battery (6h ANC) remains the weakest in the premium tier — not ideal for long-haul travel without case access.
- The Technics AZ100 (~$148) and Sennheiser MTW4 (~$150) are temporary sale prices on flagships that launched near $300 — they may rise again; if the price has moved, re-check before buying.
