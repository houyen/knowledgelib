### Best Overall: Sony WH-1000XM6 (~$378) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sony-wh-1000xm6)
The XM6's HD NC Processor QN3 is 7x faster than the QN1 in the XM5 and processes 12 microphones in real time — SoundGuys measured 87% ambient noise reduction, the best in the category. What Hi-Fi? calls it "the most detailed, dynamic, precise and open sound heard from a wireless Sony flagship." Real-world battery measured at 37h 14min with ANC. The asymmetrical headband distributes weight evenly, and the new design folds compactly. MSRP is $459, but the Amazon street price has fallen to a new record-low ~$378 as of June 2026 — comfortably under the $400 ceiling. Tom's Guide picked it over the Bose QC Ultra 2 after a six-month head-to-head as "the clear winner" overall. [src1, src3, src5, src8, src9]

### Best Comfort + ANC: Bose QuietComfort Ultra 2nd Gen (~$379) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/bose-qc-ultra-2)
SoundGuys measured 85% ambient noise reduction — within 2 percentage points of the Sony XM6, but with notably better passive isolation thanks to plusher ear cushions. The 2nd Gen adds 30h battery (up from 24h on Gen 1), aptX Adaptive for Android lossless streaming, Bluetooth 5.4, and a Cinema Mode for immersive spatial audio. CustomTune ear profiling personalizes the sound on first wear. Amazon street has dropped back to ~$379 in June 2026 (from $449 in May) — **once again under the $400 ceiling** and only ~$1 more than the Sony XM6. The pick if you wear headphones >4 hours/day. [src2, src3, src5, src8, src9]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Sony WH-1000XM6 vs Bose QuietComfort Ultra (2nd Gen)
Tom's Guide's six-month review crowned Sony XM6 the "clear winner" — it leads on measured ANC (87% vs 85%), sound customization (10-band EQ vs 3-band), battery life (37h vs 28h real-world), and codec support (LDAC). Bose wins on passive isolation, comfort during long sessions, and spatialized audio (Cinema Mode). In June 2026 the two are nearly price-matched — Sony at ~$378 vs Bose at ~$379 — so the decision is now purely about which sound/comfort profile you prefer, not price. [src5, src8, src9]

**Pick Sony XM6 if:** you want best raw ANC, longest battery, and full sound customization.
**Pick Bose QC Ultra 2 if:** you wear headphones 6+ hours/day, prioritize passive isolation and plush comfort, or want Cinema Mode for movies.

### Sony WH-1000XM6 vs Bowers & Wilkins Px7 S3
The XM6 is the "best all-rounder" pick (ANC + sound + features + price). The PX7 S3 is a "best sound" specialist — What Hi-Fi? 5-star review and Tom's Guide "Best Sound" award. ANC is 83–84% (PX7 S3) vs 87% (XM6). At Amazon's current ~$479 (PX7 S3) vs ~$378 (XM6), the Sony is the obvious value pick; the PX7 S3 only makes sense if audiophile-grade music tuning outweighs ANC and a ~$100 premium. [src1, src3, src4, src7]

**Pick Sony XM6 if:** you want the best balance and under-$400 pricing.
**Pick PX7 S3 if:** music quality is your top priority and you find it on sale at $399 or below.

### Sony WH-1000XM5 vs Sennheiser Momentum 4
