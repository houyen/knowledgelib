# Best Soundbars Under $1000 (2026)

## What are the best soundbars under $1000 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: JBL Bar 1000MK2 (~$800, down from $1199) — true 7.1.4ch system with battery-powered detachable rears and 10" wireless sub, the most versatile surround under $1000.**
**Best value: Bose Smart Ultra (~$699, down from $999) — flagship 5.1.2ch single-bar with class-leading dialogue clarity, now $300 off.**
**Best budget: Samsung HW-Q600F (~$398) — 3.1.2ch Atmos bar with wireless sub, in stock when the Hisense surround packages are sold out.**
(Sonos Arc Ultra has returned to its $1099 MSRP and now sits above the $1000 cap.)
[src1, src2, src10, src11]

## Summary

The sub-$1000 soundbar market has shifted sharply on price since spring 2026, and the leaderboard has reordered. The JBL Bar 1000MK2 — a 7.1.4-channel system with 14 drivers, a 10-inch wireless subwoofer, detachable battery-powered rear speakers, and 480W RMS — has dropped from its $1199 list to ~$800 on Amazon, making the most versatile full-surround package the new best-overall pick under $1000. The Bose Smart Ultra Soundbar, a 5.1.2 flagship single-bar with class-leading dialogue clarity, has fallen from $999 MSRP to ~$699 — a standout value. Samsung's HW-Q800F (~$598) rounds out the in-budget premium tier with a wireless sub and Q-Symphony. The biggest change: the Sonos Arc Ultra, previously discounted to ~$899, has returned to its full $1099 MSRP and now sits above the $1000 cap entirely — it is only worth considering if the budget can stretch. [src1, src2, src3, src10, src11, src12]

The market is stratified into clear tiers: under $400 you can still get full surround Atmos packages (Samsung HW-Q600F at $398, Polk Signa S4 at $336, and — when in stock — the Hisense AX5140Q at ~$350 and AX5125H at ~$300); at $400-$700 you enter mid-range territory with better drivers, room calibration, and expandable systems (Klipsch Flexus Core 200 at $400, Sonos Beam Gen 2 at $499, Samsung HW-QS700F at ~$577, Bose Smart Ultra at $699); and at $700-$800 the in-budget premium tier is led by the JBL Bar 1000MK2 (~$800, full 7.1.4 surround system), the LG S90TR (~$769), and the Samsung HW-QS750F (~$800 when in stock). Several budget and value picks — the Hisense AX5140Q, Hisense AX5125H, Sony HT-S2000, and Samsung HW-QS750F — were out of stock on Amazon as of late June 2026; check availability before relying on their listed prices. [src2, src4, src6, src10, src11]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall / Most Versatile Surround: JBL Bar 1000MK2 (~$800, down from $1199) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/jbl-bar-1000mk2)
The JBL Bar 1000MK2 delivers a 7.1.4-channel system with a 10-inch wireless subwoofer and detachable battery-powered rear speakers (10-hour runtime) that clip magnetically onto the main bar when not in use. 480W RMS through MultiBeam 3.0 technology produces convincing surround sound. JBL remains the only manufacturer offering fully wireless, battery-powered surround speakers — no power cables needed for the rears. Supports Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, AirPlay, and Chromecast. List price $1199.95, now ~$799.95 on Amazon — a complete soundbar+sub+rears system for less than many single bars. What Hi-Fi? praised its versatility and TechRadar called it "big, bold sound in a supremely versatile package." [src10, src3]

### Best Single-Bar / Dialogue Clarity: Bose Smart Ultra Soundbar (~$699, down from $999) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/bose-smart-ultra-soundbar)
Bose's flagship all-in-one features nine speakers including three tweeters and two up-firing drivers for 5.1.2 Atmos processing. The A.I. Dialogue Mode uses machine learning to balance voice and surround sound for ultra-crisp vocal clarity. ADAPTiQ room calibration adjusts sound to your specific room. TrueSpace processing upscales non-Atmos content. Built-in Alexa and Google Assistant voice control. [src2, src3]

### Best Value with Wireless Sub: Samsung HW-Q800F (~$598) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-hw-q800f)
Samsung's 5.1.2-channel system includes a compact wireless subwoofer with 6.5-inch active driver and 8-inch passive radiator. 400W total power delivers deep, room-swallowing bass and phenomenal dialogue clarity. Q-Symphony syncs with Samsung TVs to use TV speakers as additional channels. Supports Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, and Game Mode Pro. Expandable with optional wireless rear speakers. Now ~$597.99 on Amazon (down from $797.99 list) — back to deal pricing and the best dollar-for-dollar bar-plus-sub in the lineup. [src2, src4, src6]

### If Budget Stretches — Best Single Bar: Sonos Arc Ultra (~$1099) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sonos-arc-ultra)
The Sonos Arc Ultra delivers a 9.1.4-channel configuration through 14 custom drivers powered by 15 Class D amplifiers, all in a single bar. The proprietary Sound Motion woofer uses four lightweight motors in opposing corners to shift far more air than conventional drivers, producing double the bass of the original Arc. Trueplay room calibration now works on both iOS and Android, and it supports Bluetooth — a first for a Sonos soundbar. No DTS support remains its only notable gap. It has returned to its full $1099 MSRP on Amazon, so it now sits just above this guide's $1000 ceiling — worth it only if you can stretch the budget for the cleanest single-bar Atmos. [src1, src3, src7]

### Best Complete System (when in stock): Samsung HW-QS750F (~$800, OOS) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-hw-qs750f)
Samsung's QS750F ships as a complete 5.1.2-channel system with soundbar, wireless subwoofer (10-inch cube), AND rear surround speakers included — all for $899 MSRP (~$800 street). Ten built-in speakers tuned by Samsung Audio Lab, Q-Symphony integration with Samsung TVs, and Dolby Atmos/DTS:X support. Tom's Guide called it "sounds so good, I forgot I was supposed to be reviewing it." The best dollar-for-dollar complete surround system in the $800 tier — but it was out of stock on Amazon as of late June 2026; the JBL Bar 1000MK2 at ~$800 is the in-stock alternative. [src11, src2]

### Best for LG TV Owners: LG S90TR (~$769) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lg-s90tr)
A complete 7.1.3-channel system with wireless subwoofer and rear surround speakers, now ~$769 on Amazon (down from $1,199.99 MSRP). The main bar includes front-firing, side-firing, and up-firing speakers. The 670W total RMS includes a 220W subwoofer for room-filling bass, and AI-enhanced room calibration optimizes the setup. WOW Orchestra syncs with LG TVs for enhanced spatial audio. [src1, src4, src5]

### Best for Movies Under $500: Klipsch Flexus Core 200 (~$400) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/klipsch-flexus-core-200)
Built by the Klipsch-Onkyo collaboration, this 3.1.2 bar features horn-loaded tweeters, four aluminum cone drivers, and two up-firing Atmos speakers. 185W power with frequency response down to 43Hz means no subwoofer is needed for many setups. AVS Forum Top Choice 2025 award winner. Now $399.99 on Amazon (down from $499 launch). Expandable with Flexus Surround 100 speakers ($249) and Sub 100 ($299). [src2, src6]

### Best Compact Bar: Sonos Beam Gen 2 (~$499) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sonos-beam-gen-2)
Four years after launch, the Beam Gen 2 remains the benchmark for small-space soundbars. Five-driver multidirectional array handles Dolby Atmos through psychoacoustic processing rather than physical up-firing drivers. Centre channel clarity is exceptional for dialogue. Expandable with Sonos Sub and Era 300 rear speakers for a full Atmos system. AirPlay 2 and Sonos ecosystem integration included. Now ~$499 on Amazon — back at full MSRP after the late-2025 discounts ended. [src3, src7]

### Best Budget Surround (when in stock): Hisense AX5140Q (~$350, OOS) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hisense-ax5140q)
The successor to the AX5125H upgrades from 5.1.2 to a true 5.1.4-channel configuration with four up-firing Atmos drivers across 13 speakers, a wireless 6.5-inch subwoofer, and two wireless rear surround speakers. 600W total power, Hi-Concerto audio processing, room calibration, Bluetooth 5.3, and Roku TV Ready. ~$350 ($400 MSRP) when available, but temporarily out of stock on Amazon as of late June 2026. RTINGS and Homes & Gardens praise its bass output and value, though the subwoofer timing can be slightly loose with rhythmically dense music. No Wi-Fi — Bluetooth streaming only. [src12, src9]

### Best Legacy Surround Value (when in stock): Hisense AX5125H (~$300, OOS) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hisense-ax5125h)
The AX5125H remains a strong option for buyers who want true 5.1.2 surround with wireless subwoofer and rear speakers at the absolute lowest price (~$300 when in stock — currently unavailable on Amazon). What Hi-Fi?'s "Dolby Atmos bargain of the year" in early 2026. Choose the newer AX5140Q for four up-firing drivers instead of two if you can find either in stock. [src7, src9]

### Best Compact Entry-Level (when in stock): Sony HT-S2000 (~$298, OOS) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sony-ht-s2000)

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of late June 2026. Sales, regional pricing, and retailer bundles vary significantly. Many models in this range see regular discounts of 20-40%. As of this verification, four models were out of stock on Amazon — Samsung HW-QS750F, Hisense AX5140Q, Hisense AX5125H, and Sony HT-S2000 — so check availability before relying on their listed prices. The Sonos Arc Ultra has reverted to its $1099 MSRP and now sits above this guide's $1000 cap.
- "Dolby Atmos support" varies dramatically in implementation. Bars with physical up-firing speakers (JBL Bar 1000MK2, Samsung Q800F, Klipsch Core 200, Hisense AX5140Q) deliver meaningfully different height effects compared to bars using virtual/psychoacoustic processing (Sony HT-S2000, Sonos Beam Gen 2).
- TV compatibility matters. Samsung Q-Symphony only works with Samsung TVs. LG WOW Orchestra only works with LG TVs. Sonos Trueplay room calibration requires a compatible phone. Check ecosystem compatibility before purchasing.
- Room size and acoustics significantly affect performance. Bars with physical surround speakers (JBL Bar 1000MK2, LG S90TR, Samsung QS750F, Hisense AX5140Q) or powerful subwoofers (Samsung Q800F) will outperform single-bar solutions in large, open rooms. Small rooms benefit more from compact bars like the Sonos Beam Gen 2.
- DTS:X support is not universal. Notably, Sonos soundbars do not support any DTS format. If your content library relies on DTS, verify codec support before purchasing.
- The Hisense AX5125H is a 2023 model still actively sold in 2026; its successor AX5140Q (5.1.4ch) is now available for ~$350 with four up-firing drivers. The AX5125H remains available at ~$300 for budget buyers.
- The JBL Bar 1000MK2's battery-powered rear speakers (10-hour runtime) are a unique feature but add complexity — they must be charged when depleted. Wired-power rears (LG S90TR, Samsung QS750F) avoid this tradeoff.
