# Best Wireless Headphones Under $100 (2026)

## What are the best wireless headphones under $100 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Soundcore Space One (~$100, often $80 on sale)** — adaptive ANC, LDAC, and 40h battery beat everything else under $100. **Best value: Soundcore Life Q30 (~$56)** — hybrid ANC with three modes and 50h battery for just over $50. **Best budget: 1MORE SonoFlow SE (~$40)** — ANC plus class-leading 90h battery for around $40. [src1, src2]

## Summary

The sub-$100 wireless headphone market in 2026 delivers remarkable value, with active noise cancellation, 40+ hour battery life, and hi-res codec support now standard across most models. The Soundcore Space One (~$100, frequently $80 on sale) is the best overall pick, offering LDAC, adaptive ANC with 40h battery, and Bluetooth 5.3 in a well-built package. For maximum in-budget battery life, the refreshed 1MORE SonoFlow SE (HQ31, ~$40) now delivers ANC and a class-leading 90h playtime at a price that would have been unthinkable two years ago. The Audio-Technica ATH-S300BT remains the absolute battery champion at 90h, but its 2026 street price (~$140) has climbed above $100, so it is now a stretch pick rather than a budget one — and the JBL Tune 770NC has likewise crept to ~$150 at current buy-box pricing. [src1, src2, src3]

Soundcore (Anker) dominates the budget headphone category with three models in the top 10, while Sony and JBL maintain strong positions with reliable, well-tuned options. The biggest shift in 2026 is that multipoint Bluetooth (connecting to two devices simultaneously) and app-based EQ customization are now expected even at the $50 price point. ANC quality continues to trickle down from premium tiers, though budget models still struggle with high-frequency and irregular noise compared to $300+ flagships. [src1, src4, src7]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Soundcore Space One (~$100, often $80 on sale) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/soundcore-space-one)
The Soundcore Space One is RTINGS.com's top pick for wireless headphones under $100. It combines adaptive ANC, LDAC hi-res codec support, Bluetooth 5.3, and 40 hours of battery with ANC enabled. The 2x voice reduction technology specifically targets human speech frequencies for commuters. App-based EQ with 22 presets lets you customize the sound signature. At ~$80, it offers flagship-level features at a third of the price. [src1, src7]

### Longest Battery (Stretch Pick): Audio-Technica ATH-S300BT (~$140) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/audio-technica-ath-s300bt)
An extraordinary 90 hours of battery life without ANC and 60 hours with it — more than double most competitors. Audio-Technica's pedigree shows in the sound tuning, with 40mm drivers delivering excellent fidelity across the frequency range. A 3-minute quick charge provides 2.5 hours of playback, and multipoint pairing lets you switch between two Bluetooth devices seamlessly. Note: its 2026 street price has risen to ~$140 (MSRP $149), so it now sits above the $100 ceiling — included here as a stretch option for buyers who prioritize battery above all else. For the best battery within $100, the 1MORE SonoFlow SE HQ31 (90h, ~$40) is the in-budget pick. [src2, src6]

### Best Lightweight: Sony WH-CH720N (~$88) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/sony-wh-ch720n)
At just 192g, the WH-CH720N is significantly lighter than competitors (most weigh 230-265g). Sony's DSEE audio upscaling improves compressed audio quality, and the headphones support LDAC for hi-res streaming on Android. ANC effectively dampens low-frequency noise though it falls short of Sony's flagship WH-1000XM5. A 3-minute charge yields 1 hour of playback. [src1, src3]

### Best Sound Quality: Edifier W820NB Plus Gen 2 (~$60) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/edifier-w820nb-plus)
TechHive calls the W820NB Plus line "fabulous fidelity for the price," and the 2026 Gen 2 refresh (which replaced the now-discontinued original) sharpens the value further. The 40mm dynamic drivers paired with LDAC codec support deliver detailed, warm sound that competes with headphones twice the price. Hybrid ANC now reaches -49dB (up from 38dB) across adjustable levels, battery stretches to 88 hours, and Bluetooth V6.1 with multipoint keeps two devices connected. Weighs ~230g with plush padding for all-day comfort. [src2, src5]

### Best for Commuting (Now a Stretch): JBL Tune 770NC (~$150) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/jbl-tune-770nc)
Adaptive ANC with Smart Ambient mode automatically adjusts noise cancellation based on your environment — ideal for shifting between subway, street, and office. The Ambient Aware and TalkThru modes let you hear announcements or conversations without removing the headphones. Battery lasts 44 hours with ANC on. Bluetooth 5.3 ensures stable connections in crowded areas. Foldable design fits easily in bags. Note: the 770NC's Amazon buy-box has climbed to ~$150 in mid-2026, so it now sits above the $100 ceiling; for the strongest commuter ANC that stays under $100, the Soundcore Space One (~$80–$100) is the pick. [src2, src5]

### Best In-Budget Battery / Ultra-Budget: 1MORE SonoFlow SE HQ31 (~$40) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/1more-sonoflow-se-wireless-headphones-under-100)
At ~$40, the refreshed SonoFlow SE HQ31 (which replaced the earlier 70h HQ30) is the cheapest ANC headphone worth recommending and the longest-lasting model that stays under $100. QuietMax ANC blocks the bulk of low-frequency environmental noise, and the 40mm drivers deliver punchy bass and crisp detail. Battery now stretches to roughly 55 hours with ANC on and a class-leading 90 hours off, and a wired mode lets you keep listening with the battery dead. Preset EQ via the 1MORE app adds tuning the old model lacked. The main trade-off versus pricier models is the lack of LDAC support and no carrying case. [src2, src4]

### Best ANC Under $50: Soundcore Q20i (~$42) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/soundcore-q20i)
Hybrid ANC headphones for ~$42 represent remarkable value. The Q20i inherits the Life Q30's noise cancellation architecture at nearly half the price. 40 hours of ANC-on battery life, app EQ customization with 22 presets, and Bluetooth multipoint. Sound quality is a step below pricier Soundcore models but adequate for casual listening. [src1, src5]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Soundcore Space One vs Sony WH-CH720N
The Space One wins on ANC strength (~70-80% noise reduction vs the CH720N's lighter cancellation), battery (40h ANC-on vs 35h), and adaptive 2x voice reduction. The CH720N counters with a more mature, neutral tuning and a featherweight 192g frame versus the Space One's 265g. Both support LDAC. [src1, src7]
**Pick Space One if:** ANC is your top priority or you commute in loud environments.
**Pick CH720N if:** you wear headphones all day and prioritize comfort or natural sound over noise cancelling.

### Soundcore Space One vs JBL Tune 770NC
Once near-identical in price, these have diverged in 2026: the Space One holds at ~$100 (often ~$80 on sale) while the JBL Tune 770NC's Amazon buy-box has climbed to ~$150. Both offer adaptive ANC; the Space One delivers stronger raw ANC and adds LDAC hi-res support, while the 770NC's edge is Smart Ambient auto-adjustment plus TalkThru. At current prices the Space One is the value winner by a wide margin. [src1, src2]
**Pick Space One if:** you want the strongest ANC and LDAC at an under-$100 price.
**Pick JBL Tune 770NC if:** you can stretch past $100 and specifically want Smart Ambient's automatic environment switching.

### Audio-Technica ATH-S300BT vs 1MORE SonoFlow SE HQ31
Both are battery champions, and the 2026 SonoFlow SE HQ31 refresh has closed the gap dramatically. The ATH-S300BT delivers 90h without ANC and 60h with it for ~$140 (it has crept above $100 in 2026), with Audio-Technica's signature tuning. The HQ31 now matches that 90h off (and ~55h with ANC) for just ~$40 — well under a third of the price — but loses LDAC support and some tuning refinement. With the ATH-S300BT over budget, the HQ31 is the longest-lasting model that stays under $100. [src2, src6]
**Pick ATH-S300BT if:** you want premium sound and can stretch past $100.
**Pick SonoFlow SE HQ31 if:** you want class-leading 90h battery that stays under $100, or raw battery hours per dollar matters more than sound polish.

### Soundcore Q20i vs Sony WH-CH520
The under-$50 budget pick depends on whether you want ANC. The Q20i (~$40) is the cheapest hybrid ANC headphone worth recommending at 40h ANC-on. The Sony WH-CH520 (~$38) skips ANC entirely but wins on weight (147g vs 263g) and Sony's tuning pedigree, making it the more comfortable long-wear pick. [src1, src5]
**Pick Q20i if:** you need ANC for noisy environments and budget is tight.
**Pick CH520 if:** you mostly listen in quieter rooms and prioritize comfort and a clean Sony sound signature.

### Edifier W820NB Plus Gen 2 vs Soundcore Space One
Both pack LDAC and hybrid ANC, but they optimize differently — and the Edifier is now the cheaper of the two at ~$60 vs the Space One's ~$100. The Gen 2 Edifier delivers stronger -49dB hybrid ANC, 88h battery, and warmer audiophile-leaning tuning. The Space One offers adaptive ANC that adjusts in real time and a more refined Soundcore app ecosystem with 22 EQ presets. [src2, src5]
**Pick W820NB Plus Gen 2 if:** you want the best sound quality and strongest raw ANC for the lowest price.
**Pick Space One if:** you want adaptive cancellation, a polished app, and a broader Anker support ecosystem.

## Decision Logic

### If budget < $50
--> The Sony WH-CH520 (~$38) is the safest no-ANC pick with 50h battery and 147g weight. If ANC matters, stretch to the Soundcore Q20i (~$40) for hybrid noise cancellation at a minimal premium. [src1, src2]

### If primary use is commuting
--> Prioritize adaptive ANC and ambient modes over raw battery life. Under $100, the Soundcore Space One (~$80–$100) is the pick for strong overall ANC with LDAC. The JBL Tune 770NC has the best automatic environment adaptation (Smart Ambient) but has climbed to ~$150, so it is now a stretch. [src2, src5]

### If battery life is the priority
--> The Audio-Technica ATH-S300BT at 90h without ANC is unmatched on tuning, but at ~$140 it now exceeds $100. To stay in budget, the 1MORE SonoFlow SE HQ31 (~$40) now matches it at 90h without ANC and is the longest-lasting pick under $100. [src6, src2]

### If user has an iPhone
--> Skip LDAC-capable models unless also using Android devices. The Sony WH-CH720N (~$88) sounds excellent on AAC (Apple's primary codec) with the added benefit of Sony 360 Reality Audio. [src1, src3]

### If comfort and low weight matter most
--> The Sony WH-CH720N (~$88) at 192g is 40-70g lighter than competitors. The Sony WH-CH520 (~$38) is even lighter at 147g but sacrifices ANC. [src1, src3]

### Default recommendation
--> The Soundcore Space One (~$80) is the safest all-around pick. It ranks highest on aggregate across RTINGS, Tom's Guide, and SoundGuys for the under-$100 category, balancing ANC, sound, features, and build quality. [src1, src2, src7]

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **ANC is now standard under $100**: Every model over $50 in this list includes active noise cancellation. Two years ago, reliable ANC started at ~$150. The Soundcore Q20i brings it down to $40. [src1, src5]
- **Battery wars plateau at 40-90 hours**: The Audio-Technica ATH-S300BT set a 90-hour ceiling. Most competitors cluster at 40-60 hours with ANC, which is more than adequate for weekly charging. [src2, src6]
- **Quick charge becomes a differentiator**: The Edifier W820NB Plus Gen 2 offers 7 hours from a 10-minute charge, while the 1MORE SonoFlow SE gives roughly 5 hours from a 5-minute top-up. This matters more than total battery for daily use. [src2, src4]
- **LDAC hi-res codec trickles down**: The Soundcore Space One, Edifier W820NB Plus, and Sony WH-CH720N all support LDAC under $100. Two years ago, LDAC was a $200+ feature. [src1, src3]
- **App ecosystems as competitive advantage**: Soundcore, Sony, JBL, and now 1MORE (the HQ31 refresh added Preset EQ via the 1MORE app) offer EQ customization, ANC tuning, and firmware updates. Headphones with no companion app at all feel increasingly behind. [src1, src7]
- **Multipoint Bluetooth is baseline**: Connecting to two devices simultaneously is expected even at $50. The JBL Tune 720BT is the only model in this list that lacks it. [src1, src2]
- **2026 challengers worth watching**: The OneOdio Focus A6 (~$69) introduced Bluetooth 6.0 with LDAC and 75h battery (40h ANC). The SoundPEATS Space (~$80) reaches 65h ANC-on with hybrid noise cancellation. Neither has displaced the established top picks yet, but they pressure incumbents on price-to-spec. [src5]
