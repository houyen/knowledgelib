**Top pick: Soundcore P31i (~$35)** — adaptive ANC, LDAC, 50H battery, IP55, real-time translation; covers more use cases than anything else under $50.

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Soundcore P31i vs JLab Go Pods ANC
Both have hybrid/adaptive ANC and IP55, but P31i adds LDAC, real-time translation, nearly 2x the total battery (50H vs 26H), and a more refined Soundcore app. JLab Go Pods ANC wins on price ($21 vs $35) and absolute simplicity. [src1, src4]

**Pick Soundcore P31i if:** you want one set of earbuds for everything — flights, calls, music, workouts — and can stretch to $35.
**Pick JLab Go Pods ANC if:** you just want the cheapest ANC earbud that doesn't suck, period.

### EarFun Air 2 vs Sony WF-C510
EarFun Air 2 has LDAC, larger 10mm drivers, IPX7, wireless charging, and 40H total battery. Sony WF-C510 has 22 hours on a single charge (no case top-up for a week of average use), Sony's tuning, and brand-name reliability — but no LDAC, no wireless charging, IPX4 only. Both are no-ANC sound-quality picks at $43-44. [src5, src4]

**Pick EarFun Air 2 if:** you have an Android phone (LDAC matters) and want the best raw sound under $50.
**Pick Sony WF-C510 if:** you want a name brand, refuse to charge a case mid-week, and don't need ANC or hi-res codecs.

### CMF Buds 2a vs JLab Go Pods ANC
Both are cheap ANC earbuds. CMF Buds 2a quotes deeper ANC (42dB hybrid vs JLab's unspecified rating), bigger 12.4mm drivers for more bass, and slightly longer 35H total battery. JLab Go Pods ANC is older, more proven, and cheaper at ~$21 vs CMF's ~$27. [src2, src3]

**Pick CMF Buds 2a if:** bass-heavy music is your main genre and you want stronger ANC on paper.
**Pick JLab Go Pods ANC if:** you want the absolute lowest-risk, lowest-price ANC pick and don't care about bass slam.

### Moondrop Space Travel vs EarFun Air 2
Moondrop targets audio purists with neutral, Harman-leaning tuning at ~$25. EarFun Air 2 targets mainstream listeners with a V-shaped bass-boosted profile and LDAC at ~$43. Moondrop wins on tuning accuracy and price; EarFun wins on battery (40H vs 30H), waterproofing (IPX7 vs IPX4), and codec ceiling. [src2, src5]

**Pick Moondrop Space Travel if:** you listen critically to acoustic, jazz, classical, or vocal-heavy music and want reference-style tuning.
**Pick EarFun Air 2 if:** you listen to mainstream genres, want LDAC, and need workout-grade waterproofing.

### Soundcore P31i vs QCY MeloBuds N70
Both have adaptive ANC, LDAC, and 50H total battery. QCY pushes deeper ANC on paper (56 dB) and adds Bluetooth 6.0 with 6 AI mics for best-in-class calls. P31i counters with translation features, IP55 (vs QCY's IPX5), and street price ~$15 lower. [src4, src1]

**Pick Soundcore P31i if:** you want the cheapest do-everything pick with translation and dust resistance.
**Pick QCY MeloBuds N70 if:** call quality is your #1 priority and you want the deepest ANC rating in this tier.
