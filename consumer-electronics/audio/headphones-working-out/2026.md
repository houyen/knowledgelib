# Best Headphones for Working Out and Gym (2026)

## What are the best headphones for working out and gym in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 (~$249)** — earhooks lock in for HIIT, ANC + transparency, optical heart rate, 10-hour battery.
**Best value: JBL Endurance Race 2 (~$90)** — IP68, ANC, 12 hours per bud, wing-tip secure fit under $100.
**Best budget: CMF Buds Pro 2 (~$49)** — 50dB hybrid ANC, LDAC, 43-hour total battery for under $50.
The 2026 gym category is mature: earhook in-ears for HIIT, sealed ANC buds for lifters, bone-conduction/open-ear for awareness. [<a href="https://www.rtings.com/headphones/reviews/best/by-usage/running">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/fitness/best-workout-headphones">src2</a>]

## Summary

The workout headphone market in 2026 is split between three form factors: secure-fit in-ear earbuds with earhooks or wing-tips for high-intensity training, sealed ANC earbuds for weightlifters and machine cardio, and open-ear designs for gym-goers who need spatial awareness. The Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 (~$249) is the consensus best overall pick for gym use across RTINGS, Tom's Guide, Wirecutter, and SoundGuys thanks to its earhook stability, ANC, optical heart rate monitoring, and 10-hour battery life. [src1, src2, src3, src4]

For lifters and gym-floor users who prioritize noise cancellation over portability, the Sony WF-1000XM5 (~$278, back to full street price after a spring sale) excels at muting clanging plates and treadmill motors, creating a focused training environment. Budget gym-goers get exceptional value from the JBL Endurance Race 2 (~$90), which delivers IP68 waterproofing, ANC, and 12 hours of battery at a price that makes replacement painless. The biggest ongoing shift: Jabra discontinued the Elite 8 Active Gen 2 line on Amazon US — the listing now shows "Currently unavailable" — so any 2025 best-of lists that lead with it now require alternate sourcing. [src2, src5, src6]

The key decision for gym headphones is fit stability versus sound isolation. Earhook designs (Beats Powerbeats Pro 2, Soundcore Sport X10) stay locked in during burpees, box jumps, and overhead lifts. Wing-tip earbuds (JBL Endurance Race 2) offer a more discrete fit but can shift with heavy sweat. Open-ear models (Shokz OpenRun Pro 2, Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro) let you hear gym staff and spotters. Physical button controls are strongly preferred over touch — sweaty fingers cause constant misfires on touch panels. [src1, src3, src4, src7]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 vs Apple AirPods Pro 3
Both Apple-owned brands, both include optical heart rate, both around the $230-$250 mark. Powerbeats Pro 2's earhook is the decisive factor for HIIT and overhead lifts — AirPods Pro 3 still rely on silicone-tip retention and can work loose under heavy plyometrics. AirPods Pro 3 wins on ANC strength, ecosystem features (Live Translation, Workout Buddy, hearing aid mode), and pocket form factor. [src2, src3]

**Pick Powerbeats Pro 2 if:** workouts include burpees, box jumps, overhead lifts, or any move that yanks earbuds out.
**Pick AirPods Pro 3 if:** you mostly do machine cardio, weightlifting, or yoga and want best-in-class ANC + iPhone integration.

### Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 vs Jabra Elite 8 Active Gen 2
Until May 2026 this was the canonical gym-earbud matchup. Jabra is now discontinued on Amazon US, so Powerbeats Pro 2 wins by default for any new purchase. Jabra historically offered better water resistance (IP68 vs IPX4) and lower weight (5g vs 8.7g), while Powerbeats Pro 2 has the more secure earhook and heart rate monitoring. [src4, src7]

**Pick Powerbeats Pro 2:** for any new purchase in 2026.
**Pick Jabra Elite 8 Active Gen 2:** only from a verified third-party seller below $200 — otherwise hold for the successor.

### Sony WF-1000XM5 vs Bose QC Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen)
The two premium ANC picks for lifters who prioritize a quiet training environment over fit stability. Sony WF-1000XM5 (~$278) edges out on raw ANC depth, LDAC codec support, and 8-hour battery (vs Bose's 6h); Bose QC Ultra (2nd Gen) recently dropped to ~$249, so it is now the cheaper of the two. Bose wins on Immersive Audio spatialization and ear-wing stability — silicone fins anchor better in-ear than Sony's foam tips during light cardio. [src5, src6]

**Pick Sony WF-1000XM5 if:** ANC and battery life matter more than fit stability.
**Pick Bose QC Ultra (2nd Gen) if:** you want spatial audio immersion and the most secure passive fit of any sealed earbud.

### JBL Endurance Race 2 vs CMF Buds Pro 2 (budget face-off)
At ~$90 vs ~$49, CMF Buds Pro 2 is now the budget-king specs winner — 50dB hybrid ANC, LDAC, 43h battery. But JBL Endurance Race 2 has the IP68 rating and wing-tip retention that CMF lacks (IP55, silicone tip only). For pure gym duty with heavy sweat or rinsing post-workout, JBL is worth the $40 premium. [src2, src4]

**Pick JBL Endurance Race 2 if:** you sweat heavily, rinse your buds, or need wing-tip retention.
**Pick CMF Buds Pro 2 if:** sound quality, ANC strength, and battery matter more than IP68 — and you save $40.

### Shokz OpenRun Pro 2 vs Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro (open-ear face-off)
Both target the "I need to hear my surroundings" gym-goer. Shokz uses bone conduction (no ear blockage, the most aware option but weakest bass); Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro is air-conduction open-ear with the option to switch to a semi-sealed ANC mode — best of both worlds, but bulkier earhooks. [src4, src6]

**Pick Shokz OpenRun Pro 2 if:** you teach group classes, train clients, or want zero ear coverage.
**Pick Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro if:** you want awareness during compound lifts but isolation during steady-state cardio.

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **IP68 at budget prices**: JBL Endurance Race 2 brings full submersion waterproofing to $80. Two years ago, IP68 started at $150+. This makes replacement-proof gym earbuds accessible to everyone. [src2, src5]
- **Hybrid open-ear/sealed designs**: Soundcore AeroFit 2 Pro pioneered a form factor that switches between open and sealed modes. Expect more competitors by late 2026 as gym-goers want both isolation and awareness in one product. [src4, src6]
- **Heart rate in earbuds maturing**: Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 and Apple AirPods Pro 3 offer optical heart rate, but accuracy still trails chest straps by 5-10 BPM during high-intensity intervals. Useful for zone tracking, not training precision. [src1, src2, src7]
- **Military-grade durability as differentiator**: Jabra's MIL-STD-810H testing and IP68 certification set a durability benchmark. Other brands are following — expect durability certifications to become standard marketing in the sport earbud category. [src5, src7]
- **Physical controls making a comeback**: Multiple reviewers note that touch controls fail during sweaty workouts. Brands are returning to physical buttons (Beats, Jabra) or adding external dials (CMF). [src3, src4]

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of July 2026. Sales events (Prime Day, Black Friday) regularly drop prices 20-30%.
- Jabra Elite 8 Active Gen 2 was discontinued on Amazon US in mid-2026; the Amazon listing now reads "Currently unavailable" and Jabra's US product page redirects to support only. Inventory is scarce — Best Buy and third-party sellers only. The recommendation is held in this card for users still researching the model name, not as a new-purchase suggestion.
- Fit is individual. Earbuds that stay locked for one person may shift for another. Buy from retailers with return policies and test during actual workouts.
- IPX4 handles normal sweat; heavy sweaters or outdoor boot camp users should target IPX5 or higher. Saltwater and pool chlorine degrade water resistance over time regardless of IP rating.
- ANC during gym workouts reduces awareness of falling weights, moving equipment, and verbal cues. Use transparency mode in shared spaces and during group classes.
- Heart rate monitoring in earbuds is not medical-grade and loses accuracy during high-movement exercises. Pair with a dedicated chest strap for training-zone precision.
