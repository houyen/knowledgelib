# Best Wireless Earbuds Under $100 (2026)

## What are the best wireless earbuds under $100 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: EarFun Air Pro 4+ (~$57)** — Bluetooth 6.0, dual-driver, LDAC + aptX Lossless + LC3 (SoundGuys' #1), now deep-discounted from $80.
**Best value: EarFun Air Pro 4 (~$51)** — same LDAC + aptX Lossless + LC3 codec stack, 52h battery, the cheapest hi-res Android pick.
**Best budget: JLab Go Pods ANC (~$21)** — real 40dB hybrid ANC at the lowest credible price.

## Summary

The sub-$100 wireless earbuds category in 2026 is the most competitive tier in audio — features that cost $250+ two years ago (adaptive ANC, LDAC, multipoint, 50h+ battery, IP55) are now baseline. As of June 2026, the consensus best-overall pick has shifted: SoundGuys names the **EarFun Air Pro 4+** (street price now ~$57, MSRP $99) the new #1 for its Bluetooth 6.0 chipset, dual-driver hybrid system, and aptX Lossless support — the only sub-$100 earbud with all three Snapdragon Sound flagship codecs. The June deep discount to ~$57 makes it the outright best value as well as best overall. [src2, src7] Scarbir keeps the **OnePlus Buds 4** (~$100) near the top, citing its 55dB adaptive ANC and 45h total battery. [src3] The **EarFun Air Pro 4i** (~$57) remains a consensus best ANC-for-the-money — multiple reviewers rank it within 5 dB of the $250 AirPods Pro 3. [src2, src4]

The **Anker Soundcore Liberty 5** (~$90) launched Q1 2026 with Adaptive ANC 3.0 and Dolby Audio, but reviewers position it as best-for-calls (Scarbir: "near-perfect business earbuds") rather than best-ANC — its noise cancellation actually trails the older Liberty 4 NC (~$60). [src8] The **CMF Buds 2 Plus** (~$59) still posts a SoundGuys MDAQS sound score tied with the $299 Sony WF-1000XM6, and **SoundPEATS Capsule3 Pro+** (~$80, intermittently out of stock on Amazon) remains the only sub-$100 earbud shipping with xMEMS solid-state drivers. [src2] At the bottom of the price ladder, **JLab Go Pods ANC** (~$21) and **Moondrop Space Travel 2** (~$25) are now usable daily drivers with real ANC, not disposable throwaways. [src4, src6]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: EarFun Air Pro 4+ (~$57) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/earfun-air-pro-4-plus)
SoundGuys' #1 pick (March 2026, held through June), displacing the Liberty 4 NC. Bluetooth 6.0 (one of the first sub-$100 earbuds to ship with the new spec), dual-driver hybrid system (10mm dynamic + balanced armature), and full Snapdragon Sound suite — LDAC + aptX Lossless + LC3 + Auracast. ~80% noise reduction in SoundGuys' lab tests, 54h total battery (ANC off), IP55, AI translation features. The most codec-complete and feature-complete budget earbud ever made — and at the June 2026 deep-discounted street price of ~$57 (down from $80) it is also the outright best value in the table. [src2, src7]

### Best Overall (Alternative): OnePlus Buds 4 (~$100) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/oneplus-buds-4)
Scarbir's top-ranked pick. 55dB adaptive ANC (manufacturer claim — likely measures ~30-35dB low-frequency in independent tests), IP55, 45h total battery, tri-mic AI call array, and dual-device multipoint. The half-shell/stem design fits more reliably in average-to-large ear canals than the Liberty 5's wing-tip oval. LHDC 5.0 codec works best on OnePlus and Oppo phones. Sits right at $100 in June 2026 — wait for a sale dip if budget is hard, or take the deeper-discounted EarFun Air Pro 4+ instead. [src3]

### Best ANC Value: EarFun Air Pro 4i (~$57) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/earfun-air-pro-4i)
Multiple reviewers (SoundGuys, Scarbir) rank its active noise cancellation just below the Apple AirPods Pro 3 ($249) — an extraordinary result at $53. Bluetooth 5.4, LDAC, wireless charging for the case, and 40h total battery. The sound profile is v-shaped (boosted bass/treble) which suits pop/hip-hop better than acoustic. Scarbir's pick for "strongest ANC under $100." [src2, src4]

### Best for Calls / WFH: Anker Soundcore Liberty 5 (~$90) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/soundcore-liberty-5)
Launched Q1 2026 and quickly repositioned by reviewers: Scarbir calls it "near-perfect business earbuds," noting that its ANC trails the older Liberty 4 NC but its 6-mic AI call processing is best-in-class under $100. Adaptive ANC 3.0 (algorithm adjusts every 0.3 seconds), Dolby Audio, automatic transparency mode during calls, dedicated mute button. Pick this if your primary use is video calls. Skip it if pure ANC is the priority — get the Liberty 4 NC instead. [src3, src8]

### Best Codecs / Best for Android Power Users: EarFun Air Pro 4 (~$51) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/earfun-air-pro-4)
The original codec champion: LDAC + aptX Lossless (via Qualcomm Snapdragon Sound) + LC3 for LE Audio. Bluetooth 5.4, adaptive hybrid ANC, 6 mics with AI CVC 8.0, 10-band EQ + 30 presets, IP55. Now ~$51 on Amazon — the cheapest hi-res Android pick for the money, though with the Pro 4+ now down to ~$57 the gap has narrowed to ~$6. Pick the Pro 4+ over this if you want Bluetooth 6.0 + dual drivers, or this if every dollar counts. [src2, src5]

### Best Sound Quality: CMF Buds 2 Plus (~$59) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/cmf-buds-2-plus)
SoundGuys measured an MDAQS score of 4.8/5 — **tied with the Sony WF-1000XM6** ($299). 12mm drivers, LDAC, 50dB adaptive ANC reducing outside noise by 83%, and a category-leading 10h single-charge / 61.5h total battery. The Nothing Design Studio aesthetic is a styling bonus. Shorter case life than the EarFun Air Pro 4+ but cheaper per dollar of sound quality. [src2, src4]

### Best xMEMS / Solid-State Driver: SoundPEATS Capsule3 Pro+ (~$80) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/soundpeats-capsule3-pro-plus)
The first generation of sub-$100 earbuds with xMEMS Cowell solid-state speakers — response time 150x faster than dynamic drivers, plus a SoundGuys MDAQS score of 4.9/5 (the highest in the category). 45dB AI adaptive ANC, 10-band EQ, LDAC, 43h total battery. Treble detail and instrument separation are noticeably better than dynamic-driver competitors. The xMEMS bass extension is light — pair with the in-app EQ for warmer tracks. Note: the listing is intermittently out of stock on Amazon (street price ~$80 when available) — check availability before relying on it. [src2]

### Best Battery: Anker Soundcore Space A40 (~$45) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/soundcore-space-a40)
RTINGS' reigning "best budget" pick since 2023, still their May 2026 #1 under $100. 10h per earbud with ANC on, 50h total, 12h/60h with ANC off — class-leading endurance. LDAC, adaptive ANC reducing noise by up to 98%, wireless charging. Older Bluetooth 5.2 chipset is the only dated spec but does not affect day-to-day performance. [src1, src5]

### Best for iPhone Users: Nothing Ear (a) (~$53) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/nothing-ear-a)
The transparent design and physical press-to-pinch controls are genuinely distinctive in a category of look-alikes. 45dB hybrid ANC, 11mm dynamic driver, 42.5h total battery with ANC off. Because iPhones can't use LDAC, you are not losing codec value by picking the Ear (a) over cheaper LDAC-capable rivals. ChatGPT integration via Nothing X app. The June 2026 ~$53 street price (down from $99) makes this the strongest iPhone value here. [src3, src5]

### Best for Samsung Owners: Samsung Galaxy Buds FE (~$99) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-buds-fe)
Only model here that supports Samsung's Scalable Codec and the full Galaxy Wearable ecosystem (Find My Buds, 360 Audio on supported tracks, seamless switching between Galaxy devices). ANC and sound are middle-of-pack, but the ecosystem integration is worth the trade for any Galaxy phone owner. [src5]

### Best for Small Ears: JLab JBuds Mini (~$24) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/jlab-jbuds-mini)
SoundGuys' "best for small ears" pick. 30% smaller than JLab's GO Air POP, 50% smaller case, multipoint, IP55, 3 EQ presets, "Be Aware" passive listening mode.
