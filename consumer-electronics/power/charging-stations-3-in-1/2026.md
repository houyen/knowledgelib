# Best 3-in-1 Charging Stations (2026)

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Anker Prime 3-in-1 Foldable (~$120)** -- 25W Qi2.2 with near-silent 19dB AirCool fan.
**Best value: LISEN Qi2.2 25W Foldable (~$40)** -- cheapest certified 25W station on Amazon May 2026.
**Best budget: Mous 3-in-1 Portable (~$35)** -- playing-card-sized 7.5W travel charger. [src1, src2, src11, src15]

## Summary

The 3-in-1 charging station market has been transformed by Qi2.2, the latest wireless charging standard that delivers 25W to compatible iPhones (iPhone 16 and 17 series) -- nearly doubling the 15W speeds of original Qi2/MagSafe. The best stations now charge an iPhone from 0% to 50% in around 30 minutes while simultaneously powering an Apple Watch and AirPods. As of mid-2026, the Anker Prime Foldable sits at $119.99 on Amazon (MSRP $149.99, 20% off) -- having dipped to an all-time low of $104.99 during the spring sale cycle. LISEN's Qi2.2 3-in-1 holds the sale floor at $39.99 (~33% off $59.99 reference price), and the Belkin UltraCharge Pro (Qi2.2 25W) has settled to $94.99, down from a $130 reference earlier in Q1. The market has broadly re-priced downward: every flagship 25W foldable is now around or under $120, and the cheapest certified 25W station has fallen ~60% since late 2025. [src1, src2, src3, src9, src11, src12, src13, src15]

Active cooling has become a key differentiator among premium models. The Anker Prime operates at just 19dB with AirCool technology keeping device temperatures below 98.6 degrees F, while ESR's CryoBoost reduces temperatures by up to 10.8 degrees F at 25dB. The Belkin UltraCharge Pro features a controllable ChillBoost cooling fan keeping the dock up to 10 degrees F cooler. Budget options like the UGREEN MagFlow skip active cooling but still deliver Qi2.2 speeds, though thermal throttling may reduce sustained charging rates. [src1, src3, src4, src6, src9]

Prices range from $35 for ultra-portable travel chargers (Mous) to ~$130 for premium foldables and stands (Satechi, Twelve South HiRise). ESR expanded its lineup at CES 2026 with three Qi2.2 models from $90-$120, and the foldable variant now sits at $99.99 on Amazon. For most users, the sweet spot is $60-$110, where you get 25W Qi2.2 charging, solid build quality, and a foldable design that works both on a desk and while traveling. [src1, src2, src5, src10, src15]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Anker Prime 3-in-1 Foldable (~$120) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/anker-prime-3in1-25w)
The Anker Prime delivers 25W Qi2.2 charging with AirCool fan cooling at just 19dB, keeping device temperatures below 98.6 degrees F during charging. In testing, it charged an iPhone 17 Pro Max from dead to 46% in 30 minutes (Anker rates iPhone 17 Pro to 50% in 26 minutes). Its foldable design measures 3.74 x 2.38 x 1.22 inches, weighs 8.11oz, and includes a 45W USB-C adapter. The pop-out Apple Watch charger supports Nightstand mode. MSRP is $149.99; it sits at $119.99 on Amazon at last check (20% off), having ranged as low as $104.99 (all-time low) during the spring sale cycle. The display-equipped Anker Prime Dock Stand variant has settled around $150 with a 1.65" live charging display showing real-time wattage and temperature readouts. [src1, src2, src9, src13, src15]

### Best Premium Desk Stand: Belkin UltraCharge Pro 3-in-1 (~$95) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/belkin-ultracharge-pro-3in1-charging-stations-3-in-1)
Belkin's flagship dock charges at 25W Qi2.2 with a controllable cooling fan, charging an iPhone from 0-50% in 25 minutes. The chrome-and-synthetic design feels premium, and the rotating AirPods pad can be positioned left, right, or behind the main charger. Charging speeds peaked at 24.5W in testing, regularly settling at 18-20W for sustained charging. Includes a 45W adapter. Now sits at $94.99 on Amazon (5% off a $99.99 list), down from a $130 reference price earlier in Q1. [src4, src5]

### Best Fast Charging: ESR CryoBoost Foldable 3-in-1 (~$100) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/esr-cryoboost-foldable-3in1)
ESR's CryoBoost technology reduces device temperatures by up to 10.8 degrees F during charging, and the open duct system ensures consistent 25W output. The charger folds to just 15.8mm thickness and weighs 11oz (312g). Fan noise has been reduced to 25dB (from 30dB in previous models). An iPhone 16 Pro went from 0% to 50% in just over 30 minutes during testing. Includes a 50W adapter. ESR dropped the price from $100 to $90 at CES 2026; it has since rebounded to $99.99 on Amazon. ESR also introduced a transparent stand variant at $120 with the same CryoBoost cooling in a desk-optimized form factor. [src3, src2, src10]

### Best Budget 25W: Kuxiu X55 Turbo (~$54) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/kuxiu-x55-turbo-25w)
At just ~$54 (10% off a $59.98 reference price), the Kuxiu X55 Turbo offers Qi2.2 25W charging at less than half the price of most competitors. It can lie completely flat, making it versatile for travel and desk use. An iPhone 16 charges to 50% in just over 30 minutes. The lightweight eco-friendly plastic construction keeps weight at 140g, and the package includes a 45W charging brick, cable, and carrying case. [src2, src8]

### Best Value 25W: UGREEN MagFlow 3-in-1 (~$100) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/ugreen-magflow-3in1-25w)
UGREEN's MagFlow was one of the first Qi2.2-certified 3-in-1 chargers and offers 25W charging at a competitive price. It includes a 45W USB-C charger and 3.3-foot cable. The foldable design with a 70-degree adjustable angle works well for StandBy mode. However, it lacks active cooling, so some thermal throttling may occur during extended charging sessions. [src6, src5]

### Best Travel: Mous 3-in-1 Portable Charger (~$35) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/mous-3in1-portable)
Weighing just 3oz and measuring 3.5 x 2.4 x 0.5 inches folded (about the size of a playing card), the Mous is the smallest and lightest 3-in-1 charger available. It charges at 7.5W, so it is ideal for overnight charging rather than quick top-ups. The magnetic iPhone pad, flip-up Apple Watch module, and flat AirPods surface all work simultaneously. Now at $34.99 on Amazon (down from a ~$45 reference price), it is significantly cheaper than any competitor. Mous also offers a 4-in-1 Fast Charging variant ($130) with higher speeds for users who want more power in a travel form factor. [src2, src5]

### Best Design: Twelve South HiRise 3 Deluxe (~$100) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/twelve-south-hirise-3-deluxe)
The HiRise 3 Deluxe is an Apple-blessed MagSafe charger with a vertical aluminum design wrapped in vegan leather. Its footprint (6.3 x 3.4 inches) is about the same as an iPhone Pro Max. The 35-degree adjustable iPhone angle supports StandBy mode, and the Apple Watch charger tilts up for Nightstand mode. Certified MagSafe at 15W. International plug adapters are included. Now at $99.99 on Amazon, down from the original ~$150 MSRP -- the deepest cut on this premium design pick. [src4, src8]

### Best Price 25W: LISEN Qi2.2 25W 3-in-1 Foldable (~$40 sale) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lisen-qi22-25w-3in1-foldable)
At $39.99 (~33% off a $59.99 reference price), the LISEN Qi2.2 charger is the cheapest certified 25W 3-in-1 station available in mid-2026 -- about $14 less than the Kuxiu X55 Turbo. Ultra-compact foldable design collapses to 3.94 x 2.44 x 1.23 inches, with 1200g magnetic alignment force, a 65-degree phone panel, and a 180-degree rotating AirPods pad. Charges iPhone 17 Pro Max 0-60% in 30 minutes, full charge in about 1 hour 45 minutes. Bundle includes a 45W wall adapter and 1m USB-C cable. No active cooling -- passive heat plate only, so sustained 25W will throttle. Best pick when sale is live; fall back to Kuxiu X55 Turbo (~$54) if price resets. [src11]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Anker Prime 3-in-1 Foldable vs ESR CryoBoost Foldable
At their current prices the two foldable 25W flagships are within ~$20 of each other (Anker Prime ~$120, ESR CryoBoost ~$100). Anker wins on noise (19dB AirCool vs 25dB CryoBoost) and brand polish; ESR wins on raw cooling delta (10.8 degrees F vs Anker's "below 98.6 degrees F" target).
