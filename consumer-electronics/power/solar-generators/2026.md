## What are the best solar generators in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Bluetti Elite 200 V2 (~$849)** — 2,074Wh / 2,600W LFP with 50-min fast charge, the best all-round 2kWh unit. **Best value: Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 (~$449, 44% off)** — 1,070Wh / 1,500W, the cheapest large LFP unit and a great camping pick. **Best budget: Anker SOLIX C800 X (~$379.99)** — 768Wh / 1,200W with built-in camp lights. **Home backup: EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 (~$2,599)** — 4,096Wh, 120V/240V dual voltage. ## Summary

The solar generator market in mid-2026 has matured significantly, with LiFePO4 (LFP) battery chemistry now standard across virtually all models. The aggressive May 2026 fire-sale pricing has largely normalized by late June: the best overall pick is the **Bluetti Elite 200 V2 (~$849)** — up from its May ~$449 low but still well below its $1,070 list — offering 2,074Wh capacity, 2,600W continuous output (3,900W with Power Lifting), and 0-80% recharge in 50 minutes. The best per-dollar pick is now the **Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 (~$449, 44% off $799)**, the cheapest large LFP unit on Amazon. For whole-home backup, the EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 (~$2,599) delivers 4,096Wh capacity expandable to 48kWh with 4,000W output and 120V/240V dual voltage. [src1, src2, src3]

The biggest recent market shifts are (1) the EcoFlow Delta 3 Ultra Plus (~$2,199), offering 3,072Wh capacity with 3,600W output (7,200W surge) and expandability to 11kWh, and (2) the Jackery HomePower 3000 (~$1,899) — the smallest/lightest 3kWh solar generator on the market. With the May fire-sale over, the Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2 has risen to ~$1899 (and now ships in 10-11 days), so it is no longer the sub-$500 value champion — that role passes to the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 (~$449) and, at the budget tier, the Anker SOLIX C800 X (~$379.99, 768Wh) and Bluetti AC70 (~$359, 768Wh). All models reviewed here use LiFePO4 batteries with 3,000-6,000 cycle lifespans (10-17 years of daily use). [src3, src5, src7, src8]

It charges from 0-80% in just 50 minutes via AC and accepts up to 1,000W of solar input for a full charge in about 2.4 hours. At approximately 53 lbs, it remains manageable for transport. After the May fire-sale ended it sits at ~$849 (list $1,070) — still the best-balanced 2kWh unit, though buyers chasing the lowest price per watt-hour should look at the Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 (~$449) at the 1kWh tier. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Bluetti Elite 200 V2 vs Anker SOLIX C1000 Gen 2
At ~$849 vs ~$799 the two are now close on price, but the Elite 200 V2 delivers 2× the capacity (2,074Wh vs 1,024Wh) and 30% more output (2,600W vs 2,000W) for only ~$50 more. The C1000 Gen 2 wins decisively on weight (24.9 lbs vs 53 lbs) and ultra-fast AC charging (49 min vs 50 min to 80%), but currently ships in 10-11 days. [src1, src5]
**Pick Elite 200 V2 if:** you want roughly double the capacity for a small premium — the better value at this pair's price point.
**Pick C1000 Gen 2 if:** you need to move the unit room-to-room or load it solo into a vehicle without a hand truck, and want the fastest AC recharge.

### EcoFlow DELTA Pro 3 vs Anker SOLIX F3800
Both are whole-home backup units with 120V/240V dual voltage. DELTA Pro 3: 4,096Wh / 4,000W (6,000W X-Boost), 5-year warranty, ~$2,599. F3800: 3,840Wh / 6,000W native (no boost needed), expandable to 53.8kWh vs 48kWh, ~$1,699.99. [src2, src3, src5]
**Pick DELTA Pro 3 if:** you value the longer 5-year warranty, X-Boost overhead headroom (up to 12kW combined), and EcoFlow's broader smart-home ecosystem.
**Pick F3800 if:** you want more raw native output (6,000W), higher capacity ceiling, and a lower base price.

### Jackery HomePower 3000 vs EcoFlow Delta 3 Ultra Plus
The two 3kWh-tier rivals: both ~3,072Wh LFP, 3,600W output, 7,200W surge. HomePower 3000 (~$1,899, and the listing bundles 2× 200W solar panels) leads on size/weight (47% smaller, 43% lighter than competing 3kWh units), 1.7-hour hybrid recharge, UL-certified UPS (≤20ms), and 5-year warranty. Delta 3 Ultra Plus (~$2,199) leads on expandability (to 11kWh) and Storm Guard severe-weather pre-charging. [src1, src2, src8]
**Pick HomePower 3000 if:** portability inside a 3kWh unit matters and you want the lightest/smallest option with strong UPS guarantee.
**Pick Delta 3 Ultra Plus if:** future expansion to 11kWh matters or you live in a hurricane/storm zone where automatic pre-charging adds value.
