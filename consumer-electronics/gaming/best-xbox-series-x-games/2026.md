# Best Xbox Series X Games (2026)

## What are the best Xbox Series X games in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Baldur's Gate 3 (~$145 Deluxe disc on Amazon, or buy digital on the Xbox Store) -- the highest-rated Xbox Series X game of all time (Metacritic 99) with unmatched RPG depth and 200+ hours of content.**
**Best value: Hades II ($29.99 on the Xbox Store, free on Game Pass) -- the highest-rated 2026 release on Xbox (Metacritic 95) with endless roguelike replayability.**
**Best exclusive: Forza Horizon 6 (~$65, free on Game Pass Day One) -- the newest Xbox racing flagship (Metacritic 90), set in a stylized Japan with 550+ cars.**
2026 has delivered exceptional Xbox titles including Resident Evil Requiem (93), Mixtape (92, Game Pass Day One), and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (93, Game Pass Day One).

## Summary

The Xbox Series X library in 2026 is the strongest it has ever been. Between the all-time greats and a wave of exceptional new releases, Game Pass subscribers in particular have never had it better. Baldur's Gate 3 holds the highest Metacritic score on the platform at 99, while Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Edition (96) and the Forza Horizon series define the console's strengths in RPG and racing genres. The platform's multiplatform library is outstanding, and Game Pass Day One launches have transformed the value proposition for Xbox owners. [src3, src5]

2026 has been a landmark year for Xbox releases. Hades II arrived on Xbox and Game Pass on April 14, 2026 with a 95 Metacritic score, making it the highest-rated game of 2026 on the platform. Resident Evil Requiem (Metacritic 93 on Xbox) launched February 27 as the best-reviewed new release of the year. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 from Sandfall Interactive debuted as a Game Pass Day One title with a 93 Metacritic score and the highest user score on the platform. Mixtape, the Annapurna-published coming-of-age adventure from Beethoven & Dinosaur, launched May 7 on Game Pass Day One and scored 92 on Xbox. Capcom also delivered Pragmata, a refreshingly original sci-fi action game, and Monster Hunter Wilds continued to dominate co-op play. [src2, src6, src9]

The biggest change since our last review is Forza Horizon 6. Playground Games' sequel launched May 19, 2026 as a Game Pass Day One title, scoring 90 on Xbox and debuting as the best-reviewed new game of 2026 overall. Set in a stylized Japan with over 550 cars and a new ray-traced global illumination system, it is the first Forza Horizon built exclusively for ninth-generation consoles — and it now displaces Forza Horizon 5 as the platform's flagship racing game. Notably, it is also cheaper on disc (~$65) than the increasingly scarce Forza Horizon 5 (~$93). [src8, src3]

Looking ahead, GTA 6 is confirmed for November 2026, and Fable from Playground Games is also expected this year as a major Xbox first-party exclusive. Game Pass continues to be the Xbox ecosystem's defining advantage, with Doom: The Dark Ages, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, and Hades II all available as Day One additions. [src1, src7]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Baldur's Gate 3 (~$145 Deluxe on Amazon) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/baldurs-gate-3-xbox)
The highest-rated Xbox Series X game on Metacritic at 99. Larian Studios' masterpiece offers unmatched RPG freedom with hundreds of hours of branching narrative, tactical turn-based combat, and a fully reactive world. Every decision matters, and the sheer depth of player choice sets a new standard for the genre. Whether solo or in co-op, this is the definitive RPG experience on Xbox. [src3, src5]

### Best Action RPG: Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Edition (~$61) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/elden-ring-shadow-erdtree-xbox)
Metacritic 96 on Xbox. FromSoftware's open-world masterpiece plus the acclaimed Shadow of the Erdtree DLC offers hundreds of hours of exploration, challenging combat, and discovery across the Lands Between. Tom's Guide ranks it among the best Xbox games for its unmatched sense of discovery and freedom. Note: the physical Shadow of the Erdtree Edition disc is currently unavailable at Amazon — the ~$61 figure is its last-known price, and the digital edition on the Xbox Store remains available. [src1, src3]

The highest-rated 2026 release on Xbox with a Metacritic score of 95. Supergiant Games' sequel arrived on Xbox and Game Pass April 14, 2026. As Melinoe, you battle the Titan of Time with deeper combat, new locations, and endlessly replayable runs. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Baldur's Gate 3 vs Elden Ring
Both are consensus top Xbox games but represent different RPG philosophies. Baldur's Gate 3 (Metacritic 99) offers unmatched narrative freedom with turn-based tactical combat and branching storylines. Elden Ring (96) provides real-time action combat with open-world exploration and environmental storytelling. BG3 rewards careful decision-making and dialogue; Elden Ring rewards mechanical skill and exploration. [src3, src5]

**Pick Baldur's Gate 3 if:** you want narrative depth, tactical combat, and hundreds of hours of branching choices.
**Pick Elden Ring if:** you want real-time action combat, open-world exploration, and a challenging difficulty curve.

### Hades II vs Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
Both are top-rated 2026 titles available on Game Pass. Hades II (95) is a fast-paced roguelike designed for short runs with gradual progression and Greek mythology. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (93) is a 40+ hour turn-based RPG with a linear narrative, deep combat, and spectacular art direction. Hades II offers more replayability; Clair Obscur offers a deeper single narrative experience. [src3, src2]

**Pick Hades II if:** you want fast action in short sessions with roguelike replayability.
**Pick Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 if:** you want a deep, narrative-driven turn-based RPG with stunning visuals.

### Doom: The Dark Ages vs Halo Infinite
Two premier FPS experiences on Xbox with very different approaches. Doom: The Dark Ages (87) offers ferocious single-player combat with medieval weaponry and relentless pacing. Halo Infinite (87) provides a semi-open-world campaign with the series' signature sandbox combat and a free-to-play multiplayer mode. Both are on Game Pass. [src1, src5]

**Pick Doom: The Dark Ages if:** you want pure single-player brutality with no multiplayer distractions.
**Pick Halo Infinite if:** you want both a campaign and competitive multiplayer with the iconic Halo sandbox.

### Resident Evil Requiem vs Pragmata
Both are 2026 Capcom releases on Xbox but target different audiences. Resident Evil Requiem (93) is a polished survival horror masterpiece mixing first-person terror with third-person action in a familiar franchise. Pragmata (85) is an experimental new IP with innovative hacking mechanics and sci-fi storytelling. Requiem is a safer bet; Pragmata rewards players seeking something genuinely fresh. [src2, src1]

**Pick Resident Evil Requiem if:** you want a polished horror narrative with proven franchise quality.
**Pick Pragmata if:** you want an inventive, experimental Capcom experience unlike anything else.

### Forza Horizon 6 vs Forza Horizon 5
The 2026 racing question. Forza Horizon 6 (90) is newer, set in a stylized Japan with 550+ cars and ray-traced global illumination, built exclusively for ninth-gen consoles, and launched Day One on Game Pass. Forza Horizon 5 (92) scores a point higher and its Mexico map is beloved, but it is a cross-gen 2021 game whose disc has become scarce — and therefore more expensive (~$93) than its own sequel (~$65). Both are on Game Pass, so subscribers should simply start with 6. [src8, src3]

**Pick Forza Horizon 6 if:** you want the current flagship, the newer tech, and the cheaper disc -- this is the default.
**Pick Forza Horizon 5 if:** you specifically want the Mexico map, or you already have Game Pass and want a second full Horizon at no extra cost.

### Mixtape vs Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
Two very different Game Pass Day One narrative games. Mixtape (92) is a short, stylish coming-of-age adventure -- a few hours of vignettes and needle-drop music, with no combat and no fail state. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (93) is a 40+ hour turn-based RPG with demanding reactive combat. Mixtape is a one-evening mood piece; Clair Obscur is a main-course RPG. Critics rated Mixtape highly, though its user score is notably more divided. [src9, src2]
