## What are the best RPG games on PC in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Baldur's Gate 3 (~$60) -- one of the highest-rated PC games ever (Metacritic 96), with unmatched narrative freedom and tactical depth.**
**Best new 2026 release: Esoteric Ebb (~$25) -- the first worthy successor to Disco Elysium, with a 95% positive rating on Steam.**
**Best action RPG: Elden Ring (~$60) -- FromSoftware's open-world masterpiece with Metacritic 94.**
2026 has delivered outstanding new RPGs including Mewgenics (Metacritic 88, the year's top-rated RPG), Crimson Desert, and Nioh 3, while all-time greats like BG3 and Elden Ring remain essential.

## Summary

The PC RPG landscape in 2026 spans an extraordinary range of subgenres. **Baldur's Gate 3** (Larian Studios, 2023) remains the gold standard for story-driven CRPGs with its Metacritic 96 and average 100+ hour playtime, while **Elden Ring** (FromSoftware, 2022) continues to dominate the action RPG space at Metacritic 94 with 60-80 hours of open-world exploration. Both are established masterpieces that every PC RPG player should experience. [src1, src3, src8]

2026 has already produced several critically acclaimed releases. **Esoteric Ebb** (Raw Fury, March 2026) earned Metacritic 85 and drew immediate comparisons to Disco Elysium for its dialogue-driven, skill-check-based RPG design built around a D&D campaign. **Mewgenics** (Edmund McMillen / Tyler Glaiel, February 2026) is the highest-rated RPG of 2026 at Metacritic 88, blending tactical strategy with roguelike cat-breeding mechanics. **Kingdom Come: Deliverance II** (Warhorse Studios, February 2025) has climbed to Metacritic 89 as a historically grounded medieval RPG. **Crimson Desert** (Pearl Abyss, March 2026) sold 3 million copies in its first week with a massive open world comparable to The Witcher 3. [src2, src4, src6, src8]

The JRPG genre is well represented by **Trails beyond the Horizon** (Nihon Falcom, January 2026, Metacritic 82), a 70-100 hour epic that marks a turning point in one of gaming's most ambitious ongoing narratives. On the action side, **Nioh 3** (Team Ninja, February 2026, Metacritic 85) proves the soulslike subgenre continues to evolve with hardcore combat design. For budget-conscious players, **Hermit and Pig** (Heavy Lunch Studio, February 2026, Metacritic 87) delivers a charming story-rich RPG for just $15 with 6-10 hours of gameplay. [src2, src5, src7, src8]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Baldur's Gate 3 (~$60) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/baldurs-gate-3-pc)
One of the highest-rated PC games on both Metacritic (96, from 119 critic reviews) and OpenCritic, Baldur's Gate 3 is the gold standard for story-driven RPGs. Every choice matters, every character has something to say, and every situation can go sideways in ways that feel organic. D&D 5th Edition rules power deep tactical combat, while the narrative reacts to player decisions with a level of reactivity no other RPG has matched. Average Steam playtime exceeds 100 hours. [src1, src3, src8]

### Best New 2026 RPG (Highest Rated): Mewgenics (~$30) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/mewgenics-pc)
From Edmund McMillen (The Binding of Isaac) and Tyler Glaiel (The End is Nigh), Mewgenics launched February 2026 and remains the year's highest-rated RPG at Metacritic 88 (settled from an early 90 across 54 critic reviews; it now sits behind non-RPG 2026 releases like Resident Evil Requiem at 92). A tactical RPG where you breed and battle cats, it blends strategy, roguelike progression, management sim, and RPG mechanics into something genuinely unique. It recouped development costs in 3 hours and hit 115,000+ concurrent Steam players. [src4, src7, src8]

### Best Narrative RPG: Esoteric Ebb (~$25) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/esoteric-ebb-pc)
PC Gamer called it "2026's best RPG and the first worthy successor to Disco Elysium." Built around a D&D campaign, Esoteric Ebb replaces combat with dialogue and skill checks against your inner voices representing six major attributes. Investigate crime scenes, interrogate witnesses, attend therapy sessions, and explore labyrinths. Its Metacritic score has settled at 85 as reviews matured, but player reception stays exceptional: 95% positive on Steam from 8,300+ reviews ("Overwhelmingly Positive"). Developed solo by Christoffer Bodegard and published by Raw Fury. [src6, src3, src8]

### Best Action RPG: Elden Ring (~$60) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/elden-ring-pc)
FromSoftware's collaboration with George R.R. Martin remains the definitive open-world action RPG at Metacritic 94. The Lands Between offers 60-80 hours of exploration, punishing but fair combat, and world design that rewards curiosity at every turn. The Shadow of the Erdtree DLC (2024) added substantial content. Still the benchmark against which all action RPGs are measured. [src1, src2, src8]

### Best Budget RPG: Hermit and Pig (~$15) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hermit-and-pig-pc)
The highest-rated RPG you may not have heard of in 2026 (Metacritic 87, OpenCritic 89 with 100% critics recommending). A story-rich, turn-based pixel RPG where you fend off wild assailants, forage mushrooms, survive social anxiety, and stumble into a corporate conspiracy with your trusty pig. 6-10 hours of charm for just $15. [src4, src7, src8]

### Best Immersive Historical RPG: Kingdom Come: Deliverance II (~$60) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-pc)
The highest-rated release of 2025 (Metacritic 89, having climbed two points as reviews accumulated), KCD2 doubles down on historically authentic medieval Bohemia with no magic, no monsters, and combat that demands skill and patience. Warhorse Studios crafted 60-100 hours of grounded RPG gameplay where your character starts as a nobody and must earn everything. Now frequently discounted to around $24 on Steam sales, making it the strongest value in this list at full-fat AAA scale. [src4, src5, src8]

### Best Soulslike: Nioh 3 (~$70) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/nioh-3-pc)
Team Ninja proves once again they understand hardcore action RPG design with Nioh 3 (Metacritic 85, February 2026). Deep stance-based combat, demanding boss encounters, and the most refined loot system in the soulslike genre. 40-60 hours of challenging gameplay that evolves the franchise without losing its edge. [src2, src5, src8]

### Best Co-op RPG: Divinity: Original Sin 2 (~$45) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/divinity-original-sin-2-pc)
Larian's pre-BG3 masterpiece (Metacritic 93) remains the best cooperative RPG experience on PC. Four-player co-op with full campaign support, tactical turn-based combat with environmental interactions, and a level of player freedom that set the template BG3 would later perfect. 60-100 hours, frequently on sale for under $15. [src1, src3]

The beginning of the end for Nihon Falcom's Trails saga, a franchise-defining moment in one of gaming's most ambitious ongoing narratives. Trails beyond the Horizon (January 2026, Metacritic 82) delivers 70-100 hours of turn-based JRPG gameplay with world-altering revelations. Best for series fans, though the story requires familiarity with prior Trails games. [src2, src5]

Pearl Abyss evolved Crimson Desert from an MMO spinoff into a fully single-player open-world action RPG comparable to The Witcher 3. It sold 3 million copies in week one (March 2026, Metacritic 77 across 115 critic reviews). An enormous, idiosyncratic world with a million different things to do. Clunky controls hold it back from higher scores, but the scale and ambition are undeniable. [src2, src4, src8]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Baldur's Gate 3 vs Elden Ring
The two titans of modern RPGs serve fundamentally different audiences. BG3 (Metacritic 96) is the superior narrative and tactical experience with turn-based D&D combat and unmatched story reactivity. Elden Ring (Metacritic 94) delivers tighter real-time combat in a vast open world designed for exploration. BG3 averages 100+ hours; Elden Ring runs 60-80 hours. [src1, src3, src8]

**Pick Baldur's Gate 3 if:** you value story, dialogue, character relationships, and tactical combat.
**Pick Elden Ring if:** you want challenging real-time combat, open-world exploration, and atmospheric world design.

### Esoteric Ebb vs Baldur's Gate 3
Both are CRPGs rooted in D&D, but they diverge sharply in design. BG3 offers full tactical combat, a party system, and 100+ hours of content. Esoteric Ebb strips away combat entirely in favor of dialogue and skill checks, delivering a tighter 30-50 hour experience that channels Disco Elysium. Esoteric Ebb costs $25 vs BG3's $60. [src1, src6]

**Pick Esoteric Ebb if:** you loved Disco Elysium and want narrative over combat.
**Pick Baldur's Gate 3 if:** you want the complete package -- story, combat, and co-op.

### Mewgenics vs Hermit and Pig
Both are 2026's surprise indie RPG hits, but they could not be more different. Mewgenics (Metacritic 88, ~$30) is a deep tactical roguelike with near-infinite replayability through cat breeding. Hermit and Pig (Metacritic 87, ~$15) is a focused 6-10 hour narrative RPG with pixel art and heart. The two are now separated by a single Metacritic point, so pick on format, not score. [src4, src7, src8]

**Pick Mewgenics if:** you want hundreds of hours of tactical depth and roguelike variety.
**Pick Hermit and Pig if:** you want a short, charming story you can finish in a weekend.

### Kingdom Come: Deliverance II vs Crimson Desert
Both are massive open-world RPGs released in early 2025/2026, but with opposing philosophies. KCD2 (Metacritic 89) is grounded historical realism -- no magic, no monsters, just medieval Bohemia. Crimson Desert (Metacritic 77) is epic fantasy with flashy combat and enormous scale. KCD2 has the higher critical reception and, at a frequently discounted ~$24 versus Crimson Desert's $70, the far better value; Crimson Desert has the bigger world. [src2, src4, src8]

**Pick KCD2 if:** you want historical authenticity and methodical, realistic gameplay.
**Pick Crimson Desert if:** you want a massive fantasy open world with spectacle combat.

### Nioh 3 vs Elden Ring
The top two action RPGs on PC serve different flavors of challenge. Elden Ring (Metacritic 94) is open-world with build variety and exploration as core pillars. Nioh 3 (Metacritic 85) is mission-based with deeper moment-to-moment combat through its stance system and loot complexity. Elden Ring is more accessible; Nioh 3 rewards mastery of its systems. [src1, src2, src8]

**Pick Elden Ring if:** you want open-world freedom and a broader, more accessible experience.
**Pick Nioh 3 if:** you want deeper combat mechanics and loot-driven progression.
