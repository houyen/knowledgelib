# Best PS5 Games (2026)

## What are the best PS5 games in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Elden Ring (~$49) -- the highest-rated PS5 game of all time (Metacritic 96) with hundreds of hours of open-world exploration.**
**Best value: Astro Bot (~$39) -- the definitive PS5 platformer (Metacritic 94), now well below its launch price.**
**Best new release: Resident Evil Requiem (~$63) -- the best-reviewed PS5 game of 2026 (Metacritic 89) with a 9.3 user score.**
2026 has also delivered 007 First Light (88), Pragmata (85), and PS5 exclusive Saros (87) from Housemarque, alongside Hades II (93).
[src4, src8]

## Summary

The PS5 library in 2026 is at its peak. Between established masterpieces and an exceptionally strong wave of new releases, there has never been a better time to own Sony's current-gen console. Elden Ring remains the highest-rated PS5 game on Metacritic at 96, while Baldur's Gate 3 (96) matches it in critic consensus. God of War Ragnarok, Astro Bot, and the Shadow of the Erdtree expansion continue to define what PS5 exclusives and enhanced experiences can achieve. [src4, src2]

2026 has been a landmark year for new releases. Capcom alone has published three highly rated titles: Resident Evil Requiem (Metacritic 89 on PS5, 9.3 user score across 15,000+ ratings), Monster Hunter Wilds, and the new sci-fi IP Pragmata (85). Supergiant's Hades II arrived on PS5 in April 2026 with a 93 Metacritic score, while Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 from Sandfall Interactive stunned critics with a 93 and the highest user score on the platform (9.7). Housemarque's PS5 exclusive Saros launched April 30 to an 87 Metacritic, making it the studio's highest-rated game ever, and IO Interactive's 007 First Light (88) became the best-reviewed James Bond game since GoldenEye. [src6, src8, src9]

Note that scores for the 2026 class have settled slightly as review counts grew: Resident Evil Requiem moved from 92 to 89 across 133 critic reviews, and Saros from 88 to 87 across 136. Requiem remains the highest-rated PS5 game released in 2026. [src4, src8]

Looking ahead, GTA 6 is locked to November 19, 2026 with pre-orders live since June 25, and Marvel's Wolverine from Insomniac Games is also expected this year. Forza Horizon 6 -- the highest-rated game of 2026 so far at Metacritic 90 -- is Xbox/PC-only until its PS5 port arrives in the second half of the year. The PS5 Pro Enhanced program means many of these titles look and perform better than ever on the upgraded hardware. [src1, src3, src8]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Elden Ring (~$49) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/elden-ring-ps5)
The highest-rated PS5 game on Metacritic at 96, Elden Ring delivers hundreds of hours of open-world exploration, challenging combat, and discovery across the Lands Between. The Shadow of the Erdtree DLC (Metacritic 94) adds substantial endgame content. FromSoftware's masterpiece offers complete freedom of movement -- when stuck, you can explore elsewhere and always find something incredible. GamesRadar called it one of the best open-world games of the modern era. Note that the deep discounts of early 2026 have lapsed -- it now sits near $49 rather than the ~$31 seen in spring. [src2, src4]

### Best PS5 Exclusive: Astro Bot (~$39) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/astro-bot-ps5)
Game of the Year 2024 winner with a 94 Metacritic score. GamesRadar ranks it the number one PS5 game for its finely tuned movement, carefully balanced difficulty, and celebratory showcase of PlayStation's legacy. TechRadar calls it "perhaps the most polished platformer ever made." Comes free with new PS5 consoles via the Astro Bot bundle, and now discounted to roughly $39 standalone -- the strongest quality-per-dollar pick in this table. [src2, src3]

### Best New Release 2026: Resident Evil Requiem (~$63) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/resident-evil-requiem-ps5)
The best-rated mainline Resident Evil in over 20 years and the highest-rated PS5 game released in 2026. Metacritic 89 on PS5 across 133 critic reviews, with a 9.3 user score from more than 15,000 ratings. Grace Ashcroft's first-person survival horror segments rank among the most terrifying content Capcom has produced, while Leon Kennedy's third-person action sequences carry the franchise's combat legacy. Visually stunning with excellent exploration and puzzles. [src7, src4]

### Best RPG: Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (~$49) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/clair-obscur-expedition-33-ps5)
Metacritic 93 with the highest user score on the platform at 9.7. Sandfall Interactive's debut features visually spectacular art direction, deep active turn-based combat, brilliant voice performances, and a gripping narrative. TechRadar gave it top marks for its exciting, rewarding combat system and sombre storytelling. [src3, src4]

### Best Roguelike: Hades II (~$30) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hades-ii-ps5)
Supergiant Games' sequel arrived on PS5 April 14, 2026 with a 93 Metacritic score. As Melinoe, the immortal Princess of the Underworld, you battle the Titan of Time with deeper combat, new locations, and an endlessly replayable structure. Combat is faster, deeper, and more varied than the original, with distinct tactical choices that reward dozens of runs. Note: Hades II is a digital-only release on PS5 (no physical disc); purchase via the PlayStation Store. [src4, src5]

### Best Action/Adventure: God of War Ragnarok (~$33) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/god-of-war-ragnarok-ps5)
TechRadar's number one PS5 game. The critically acclaimed sequel follows Kratos and Atreus through the Nine Realms with emotive storytelling, clever combat evolutions, and massive scope. A single-player epic that is "often as brutal as it is poignant." PS5 Pro Enhanced for improved visuals. [src3, src1]

### Best PS5 Exclusive 2026: Saros (~$69) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/saros-ps5)
Housemarque's latest PS5 exclusive launched April 30, 2026 to an 87 Metacritic score across 136 critic reviews -- the studio's highest-rated game ever, surpassing Returnal (86). A lean fusion of roguelike sci-fi action and eldritch horror with Housemarque's signature bullet-ballet combat. Push Square called it "Housemarque at the peak of its powers." Still at full price, so patient buyers should wait for a PS Store sale. [src6, src4]

### Best 2026 New IP: 007 First Light (~$70) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/007-first-light-ps5)
IO Interactive's James Bond origin story launched May 27, 2026 to a Metacritic 88 on PS5 -- the best-reviewed Bond game since GoldenEye and IO's highest-rated release ever, beating its own Hitman 3 (87). The Hitman studio's sandbox-stealth design fits Bond naturally: improvised gadgets, multi-route infiltration, and set-piece chases. 97% of critics recommend it, placing it in OpenCritic's "Mighty" tier and Metacritic's 2026 midyear top ten. Note that the Standard Edition is currently unavailable on Amazon -- the linked Specialist Edition is the in-stock physical option at ~$70. [src9, src8]

### Best JRPG: Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (~$47) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth-ps5)
Metacritic 92 with exceptional narrative design, fluid combat, and captivating character chemistry. A much larger and more substantial slice of action and open-world exploration than Remake. TechRadar praised its gorgeous world and excellent combat system. PS5 console exclusive (timed). [src3, src4]

### Best Co-op: Monster Hunter Wilds (~$30) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/monster-hunter-wilds-ps5)
Capcom's most accessible Monster Hunter yet with a seamless open world, breathtaking seasonal changes, and the new Focus Mode system. Push Square gave it 9/10. Playable solo with AI companions but arguably best in online co-op. TechRadar praised its "all killer, no filler" campaign. [src3, src1]

### Best Multiplayer: Helldivers 2 (~$31) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/helldivers-2-ps5)
The breakout co-op phenomenon of the PS5 generation. Four-player squad-based combat against alien hordes with friendly fire, strategic loadouts, and a community-driven galactic war metagame. Still actively supported with new content in 2026, and now one of the cheapest live-service entries on the console at roughly $31. PS5 console exclusive (timed). [src1, src2]

### Best Value Exclusive: Returnal (~$36) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/returnal-ps5)
Housemarque's PS5 exclusive roguelike shooter that defined the console's early generation. Metacritic 86 with lightning-fast combat, DualSense haptic feedback showcase, and a haunting sci-fi narrative. The spiritual predecessor to Saros, and roughly half its price -- the cheaper way to sample Housemarque's arcade DNA. [src1, src3]

### Best Sci-Fi New IP: Pragmata (~$60) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/pragmata-ps5)
Capcom's original sci-fi action-adventure launched April 17, 2026 to a Metacritic 85 across 104 critic reviews and an 8.8 user score. Critics praised its unusual blend of shooting and real-time hacking, the RE Engine's lunar visuals, and the character dynamic between engineer Hugh and android Diana. Compact and tightly designed rather than sprawling -- a good pick when the 100-hour open worlds elsewhere in this table feel like too much. [src4, src1]

### Best Superhero Game: Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (~$44) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/spider-man-2-ps5)
PS5 exclusive from Insomniac Games with two playable Spider-Men, the symbiote storyline, and a massive open-world New York City. Metacritic 90. Near-instant load times and stunning visuals showcase PS5 hardware. [src2, src1]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Elden Ring vs God of War Ragnarok
Both are consensus top PS5 games but offer fundamentally different experiences. Elden Ring (Metacritic 96) provides unmatched exploration freedom and challenging combat across hundreds of hours, while God of War Ragnarok (94) delivers a tightly directed, emotive narrative experience in a 30-40 hour campaign. Elden Ring has more replay value; Ragnarok has a more accessible difficulty curve. [src3, src2]

**Pick Elden Ring if:** you want open-world freedom, challenging combat, and 200+ hours of content.
**Pick God of War Ragnarok if:** you prefer a directed narrative, accessible difficulty, and a cinematic experience.

### Resident Evil Requiem vs Saros
Both are standout 2026 releases but target different audiences. Resident Evil Requiem (Metacritic 89, ~$63) is a carefully crafted survival horror experience mixing first-person terror with third-person action. Saros (87, ~$69) is a fast-paced roguelike shooter from Housemarque with arcade-style replayability. Requiem is a one-playthrough narrative; Saros is designed for dozens of runs -- and Requiem is currently the cheaper of the two. [src7, src6]

**Pick Resident Evil Requiem if:** you want a cinematic horror narrative with exploration and puzzles.
**Pick Saros if:** you want replayable arcade combat with roguelike progression.

### Astro Bot vs Returnal
Both are PS5 exclusives from Sony's first-party studios but couldn't be more different. Astro Bot (94, ~$39) is a joyful, family-friendly platformer celebrating PlayStation history. Returnal (86, ~$36) is an intense, punishing roguelike shooter with a dark sci-fi atmosphere. Astro Bot is 8-12 hours; Returnal is 20-30+ hours with roguelike loops. At near-identical prices this is a pure taste decision, not a budget one. [src2, src3]

**Pick Astro Bot if:** you want a polished, accessible platformer suitable for all ages.
**Pick Returnal if:** you want challenging, high-intensity combat with a mysterious narrative.

### Hades II vs Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
Both scored 93 on Metacritic but represent different RPG traditions. Hades II is a fast-paced roguelike with Greek mythology, designed for short runs and gradual progression. Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is a 40+ hour turn-based RPG with a linear narrative, deep combat systems, and spectacular art direction. [src4, src3]

**Pick Hades II if:** you want fast action in short sessions with roguelike replayability.
**Pick Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 if:** you want a deep, narrative-driven turn-based RPG.

### Final Fantasy VII Rebirth vs Clair Obscur: Expedition 33
Two exceptional RPGs competing for the same audience in 2026. FF7 Rebirth (92) offers an action-RPG hybrid system with open-world exploration and beloved characters. Clair Obscur (93) is a new IP with active turn-based combat and a darker, more melancholic tone. FF7 Rebirth has more content; Clair Obscur has a tighter, more focused experience. [src3, src4]

**Pick Final Fantasy VII Rebirth if:** you love JRPG traditions, open-world exploration, and the FF7 universe.
**Pick Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 if:** you want a fresh RPG with interactive turn-based combat and stunning art.
