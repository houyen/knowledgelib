# Best Toaster Ovens and Countertop Ovens (2026)

## What are the best toaster ovens and countertop ovens in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Breville Smart Oven Pro BOV845BSS (~$270)** — top marks across every review site for broiling, toasting, and Element IQ heat control.
**Best value: Ninja French Door Premier FO101 (~$180)** — Tom's Guide's best overall, with 20.3 QT capacity and PFAS-free surface, now discounted ~$100 off list.
**Best budget: Hamilton Beach 2-in-1 31156 (~$66)** — the only oven to ace every Consumer Reports toasting test. [src1, src2, src3]

## Summary

The toaster oven market in 2026 has expanded well beyond simple toast-making. Today's countertop ovens serve as full-featured cooking appliances with air frying, convection baking, dehydrating, smart wireless probes, and even dual-zone cooking capabilities. Prices range from under $50 for basic models to over $400 for premium smart ovens. The best overall toaster oven is the Breville Smart Oven Pro BOV845BSS (~$270), which earns top marks from Consumer Reports for broiling, toasting consistency, and ease of use. Its Element IQ technology distributes heat intelligently across 5 quartz elements, and it offers 10 cooking functions with 1800W of power. [src1, src3, src4]

For air-fryer enthusiasts, the Cuisinart Digital AirFryer Toaster Oven TOA-65 (~$368) remains the consensus pick, combining 1800W convection power with a dedicated air-fry basket that produces crispy results rivaling standalone air fryers, though its price has risen notably in mid-2026. The Ninja Double Oven DCT401 (~$380) stands out for dual-zone cooking, offering two independent cooking chambers with FlexDoor technology. The Typhur Sync Oven (~$199, sharply discounted from a $300 list) introduced a built-in wireless meat probe with five sensors and AI-powered recipe generation, earning RTINGS' top rating for smart cooking precision — and is now one of the best smart-oven values available. New for April 2026, the Ninja French Door Premier FO101 (~$180, down ~$100 from list) has replaced the Ninja Prestige Smart XL as Tom's Guide's top overall pick — its dual swing-out doors, 20.3 QT capacity, 10-in-1 functions, and PFAS-free cooking surface deliver oven-replacement versatility in a countertop footprint, and its current price makes it the strongest overall value of 2026. The Ninja Prestige Smart XL DT551 (~$230, down sharply from a $430 list) still brings 10x convection power and a 90-second preheat to the premium segment. [src1, src2, src3, src5]

Budget shoppers should consider the Hamilton Beach 2-in-1 Toaster Oven 31156 (~$66, recently discounted from ~$100), the only model in Consumer Reports' ratings to earn top scores in every toasting test while also performing solidly in baking evaluations. For compact kitchens, the Panasonic FlashXpress NB-G110P (~$119) heats up instantly with double infrared technology, eliminating preheat time entirely. At the premium end, the Breville Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro BOV950BSS (~$500) adds Wi-Fi connectivity and 13 smart cooking functions with the Joule app for guided recipes. [src1, src2, src4, src6]
