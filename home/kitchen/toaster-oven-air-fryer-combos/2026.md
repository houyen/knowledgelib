# Best Toaster Oven Air Fryer Combos (2026)

## What are the best toaster oven air fryer combos in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro BOV900BSS (~$400) — Element IQ 5-element system gives the most even toast/roast of any combo, 13 functions, 1 cu ft.**
**Best value: Ninja Foodi 10-in-1 XL Pro DT201 (~$250) — XL two-level capacity, 90-second preheat, frequently 30%+ off list.**
**Best budget: BLACK+DECKER Crisp 'N Bake 6-Slice (~$73) — 5-in-1, fits a 12" pizza, the cheapest competent combo.**
Combos trade some peak crispness for versatility — basket air fryers still crisp faster.
[<a href="https://www.rtings.com/toaster-oven/reviews/best/air-fryer">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.rtings.com/toaster-oven/learn/research/versus-air-fryers">src2</a>]

## Summary

A toaster oven air fryer combo replaces both a toaster oven and a basket air fryer with one countertop appliance that bakes, broils, toasts, roasts, and air-fries in a single wide cavity. The **Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro BOV900BSS** (~$400) is the consensus best overall across RTINGS, Food Network, and Taste of Home — its Element IQ system uses 5 independent quartz elements and PID temperature control to produce the most even toasting and roasting of any combo tested, with 13 functions and a 1 cubic foot interior that fits a 14-lb turkey or nine slices of toast. [src1, src3, src7, src8]

For most households the better buy is the **Ninja Foodi 10-in-1 XL Pro DT201** (~$250, frequently 30-34% off its $380 list), which offers XL two-level cooking, a 90-second preheat, and up to 30% faster cooking than a traditional convection oven. Budget shoppers can get a competent combo for **~$73** in the BLACK+DECKER Crisp 'N Bake 6-Slice. The most important caveat: RTINGS' head-to-head testing of 11 air-fry toaster ovens found that dedicated basket air fryers still crisp faster and more evenly — combos win on versatility and capacity, not peak crispness. [src1, src2, src3]

The 2026 field clusters into three tiers: premium even-heating ovens ($300-400, Breville), mid-range XL workhorses ($150-250, Ninja, Cuisinart, Emeril, Hamilton Beach), and budget 5-in-1 units (under $100, BLACK+DECKER). Nearly every model runs 1700-1800W; the real differentiators are element layout, convection fan design, capacity, and preset count. [src1, src4, src6]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro vs Ninja Foodi 10-in-1 XL Pro DT201
The Breville wins decisively on heating evenness (Element IQ, 5 quartz elements) and build, and is the better baker/toaster. The Ninja DT201 wins on price (~$250 vs ~$400), preheat speed (90 seconds), and two-level capacity. For pure cooking quality choose the Breville; for value and throughput choose the Ninja. [src1, src3, src7]

**Pick Breville BOV900BSS if:** even toasting/baking quality and longevity matter most and budget allows ~$400.
**Pick Ninja DT201 if:** you want XL family capacity and fast preheat at a far lower price.

### Cuisinart TOA-70 vs Ninja Foodi DT202BK
Both are ~$158-170 mid-range workhorses. The Cuisinart TOA-70 adds a grill plate and is more compact (0.6 cu ft, fits a 12" pizza); the Ninja DT202 has a much larger two-level XL cavity (12-lb turkey) and a faster 90-second preheat. Cuisinart for a smaller kitchen and grilling; Ninja for maximum capacity. [src1, src6]

**Pick Cuisinart TOA-70 if:** you want a grill plate and a more compact footprint.
**Pick Ninja DT202 if:** you cook for a crowd and want the largest cavity at the price.

### Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro vs Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer (BOV860)
Both use Breville's Element IQ even-heating. The Pro (BOV900) is larger (1 cu ft vs ~0.8), has 13 functions vs 11, adds a Proof setting and dehydrate-4-trays, and costs ~$50 more. The BOV860 keeps the heating quality in a smaller body. [src1, src5, src8]

**Pick BOV900BSS if:** you want maximum capacity and the full function set.
**Pick BOV860BSS if:** you want Breville heating quality but less counter space.

### Emeril French Door 360 vs COSORI 13-in-1 32QT
Both are extra-large, preset-heavy ovens for families. The Emeril (26 QT, French door, 24 presets, ~$160) has a reliably in-stock listing and the convenience of a single-pull door; the COSORI (32 QT, 13 presets, app/voice control) is larger and adds smart control but its Amazon listing has been intermittently unavailable in 2026. [src6]

**Pick Emeril French Door 360 if:** you want a confirmed in-stock XL oven with rotisserie and a French door.
**Pick COSORI 13-in-1 if:** you want the largest cavity plus app/voice control and the listing is in stock at a good price.

- **French-door designs trickling down**: the single-pull French door (Emeril, BLACK+DECKER 12-in-1) is now available under $150, a convenience feature previously limited to $200+ ovens. [src5, src6]
- **Smart/preset inflation**: high-end COSORI and Emeril units now ship with 13-24 presets plus app/voice control; reviewers caution that more presets rarely beat good fundamentals (even heating, accurate temps). [src4, src6]
- **Probe and sous-vide features appearing mid-range**: integrated temperature probes (Hamilton Beach 31243) and sous-vide modes (COSORI CS130-AO) are migrating below $200. [src5, src6]

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of July 2026 and fluctuate; Ninja in particular discounts heavily (the DT201 was recently ~34% off its ~$380 list). Confirm current price at the buy link before purchasing.
- The COSORI CS130-AO Amazon listing has been intermittently "currently unavailable" during 2026 (and is again as of July 2026) — its buy link now points to the official COSORI product page (cosori.com) rather than an out-of-stock Amazon listing.
- Combo air-fryer crispness depends on not overcrowding the rack; single-layer batches crisp far better than piled food. Basket air fryers remain superior for small, crispy portions. [src2]
- Large XL and French-door units (Emeril 26QT, COSORI 32QT, Ninja DT-series) need substantial counter depth and clearance; exteriors get hot during use.
- Manufacturer "function" counts mix true cooking modes with simple presets — a 24-preset oven is not necessarily more capable than a 13-function one. Judge by heating quality and capacity, not the headline number. [src4, src6]
