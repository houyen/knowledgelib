## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### SIHOO M18 vs Mimoglad
SIHOO M18 (~$140) is the only chair under $200 with BIFMA certification and offers a 330 lbs capacity vs Mimoglad's 300 lbs. Mimoglad (~$98) wins on price by ~$40 and includes a 5-year warranty vs SIHOO's 3-year. SIHOO has 15,000+ Amazon reviews vs Mimoglad's smaller review base. [src1, src3, src5]
**Pick SIHOO M18 if:** BIFMA certification matters, you weigh 220+ lbs, you want the deepest review history, or you sit 8+ hours/day.
**Pick Mimoglad if:** Budget under $100 is hard limit, you want maximum warranty length, or you need flip-up armrests for under-desk clearance.

### FlexiSpot OC3B vs Gabrylly with Footrest
FlexiSpot OC3B (~$140) wins on price by ~$42 and comes from a recognized ergonomic brand. Gabrylly (~$182) adds 3D armrests (vs 2D), 135° recline (vs lockable), and a retractable footrest. FlexiSpot has 2D headrest; Gabrylly's headrest is fully adjustable. [src1, src2, src5]
**Pick FlexiSpot OC3B if:** Budget under $150 is firm, you don't need a footrest, or you prefer a known brand for warranty support.
**Pick Gabrylly w/Footrest if:** You want post-lunch reclining with a footrest, you need 3D armrests, or you're under 6'0" tall.

### Ticova vs Gabrylly w/Footrest
Both sit at the top of this price range ($182-200). Ticova (~$200) has higher weight capacity (350 vs 300 lbs) and a thick foam W-shaped seat. Gabrylly (~$182) is all-mesh (more breathable) with a footrest and 135° recline, and now undercuts Ticova by ~$18. Ticova was listed under $130 in early-2026 review-site guides but has climbed to the $200 ceiling in Amazon street price (Q3 2026). [src7, src5]
**Pick Ticova if:** You weigh 300-350 lbs, you prefer foam over mesh seat, or you need a curved-foam headrest for neck support.
**Pick Gabrylly w/Footrest if:** You run hot (mesh is more breathable), you want a footrest, or you recline frequently during the day.

### SIHOO M18 vs Monhey Ergonomic
Both are foam-seat + mesh-back chairs at similar price ($140 vs $130). SIHOO M18 wins on BIFMA certification, 15,000+ reviews, and S-curve spine support. Monhey wins on 350 lbs cap (vs 330), dual-zone neck + lumbar adjustment (separate zones), and 3D headrest (vs 2D). [src1, src2, src4]
**Pick SIHOO M18 if:** BIFMA certification matters, you want the largest review base, or you prefer S-curve back over dual-zone.
**Pick Monhey if:** You have chronic neck/upper-back pain (dual zones address both), you weigh 300+ lbs, or you want a 3D adjustable headrest.

### Hbada J3 vs Mimoglad
Both are budget picks under $120 with flip-up armrests. Hbada J3 (~$116) has a thicker 12cm W-cushion vs Mimoglad's thinner foam, and PU silent wheels (better for hard floors). Mimoglad (~$98) is $18 cheaper, has 300 lbs cap (vs 250), and offers a 5-year warranty (vs Hbada's 3-year). [src5, src7]
**Pick Hbada J3 if:** You want the thickest cushion under $120, you have hardwood floors (silent wheels matter), or compact under-desk fit is critical.
**Pick Mimoglad if:** Budget under $100 is hard limit, you weigh 250+ lbs, or maximum warranty length matters.
