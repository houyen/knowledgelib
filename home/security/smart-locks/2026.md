# Best Smart Locks (2026)

## What are the best smart locks in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Yale Assure Lock 2 Wi-Fi (~$170)** — broadest platform support (Alexa, Google, HomeKit, SmartThings, Matter) and ~1-year AA battery life, now discounted to ~$170.
**Best value: Schlage Arrive (~$199)** — ANSI Grade 1 / BHMA AAA commercial-grade security with built-in Wi-Fi.
**Best budget: Wyze Lock Bolt v2 (~$80)** — adds Wi-Fi remote access to fingerprint + keypad entry.
UWB hands-free unlocking, Aliro 1.0 cross-platform Home Key, and palm-vein biometrics arrived as shipping products in early 2026. ## Summary

The smart lock market in 2026 has undergone a generational shift. Ultra-Wideband (UWB) hands-free unlocking, Matter-over-Thread as a baseline connectivity standard, and advanced biometrics — 3D facial recognition and palm-vein scanning — have moved from CES demos to shipping products. After surveying recommendations from nine major review outlets, the **Yale Assure Lock 2** remains the best overall smart lock for most people, thanks to its unmatched platform support (Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit, SmartThings, and Matter), modular configurations, and approximately one-year battery life on four AA batteries — and mid-2026 street pricing has fallen to about $170 (35% off the $260 MSRP) [src1, src5]. For top-tier physical security, the **Schlage Encode Plus** (now ~$319) retains its crown with BHMA Grade 1 / ANSI Grade 1 certification and Apple Home Key, while the **Schlage Arrive** ($199) offers the same BHMA AAA / ANSI Grade 1 security at a lower price point with 250 access codes and built-in Wi-Fi [src7, src6].

The biggest 2026 milestone is the **Aqara Smart Lock U400** ($270), the first widely available UWB-enabled smart lock in the US — and in April 2026 the first lock to pass Aliro 1.0 certification. It detects your iPhone or Apple Watch as you approach and unlocks hands-free — no tapping, no codes, no biometrics required — while also supporting Matter-over-Thread, fingerprint, PIN, NFC, and Apple Home Key [src8, src4]. PCMag awarded the **Ultraloq Bolt Fingerprint** (the 2026 "Upgrade" model, ~$140) their Editors' Choice as best overall smart lock, citing its 360-degree fingerprint scanner, built-in Wi-Fi, BHMA AAA certification, IP65 weatherproofing, and eight distinct unlock methods at a mid-range price [src3]. Meanwhile, the **Level Lock Pro** ($349) replaces the Level Lock+ as the ultimate invisible smart lock, hiding all components inside the deadbolt while adding native Matter-over-Thread, Apple Home Key, and built-in door-state detection [src2].

Budget shoppers now have a compelling upgrade: the **Wyze Lock Bolt v2** ($80) adds built-in Wi-Fi to the original's fingerprint and keypad entry, enabling remote access from anywhere — a feature that previously required spending twice as much. For those willing to invest in cutting-edge biometrics, the **eufy FamiLock E34** (~$230) brings palm-vein recognition — a 99.9%-accurate, touchless biometric that cannot be spoofed by photos — while the **Lockly Visage Zeno** (~$339) uses dual infrared cameras for 3D face unlock that works in total darkness [src3, src9].

Mid-2026 street price has fallen to around $170 (35% off the $260 MSRP), making it the clear value leader among premium locks. [src1, src5]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Yale Assure Lock 2 vs Schlage Encode Plus
Both are premium full-replacement deadbolts with broad platform support. The Yale wins on flexibility — modular configurations (keypad, touchscreen, fingerprint, Home Key module) and ~1-year AA battery life — while the Encode Plus wins on raw physical security with BHMA Grade 1 / ANSI Grade 1 certification (Yale is Grade 2). With Yale now ~$170 and Encode Plus ~$319 — a ~$150 gap — Yale is the better default; Encode Plus is the right pick when door-kick resistance matters more than configurability. [src1, src6]

**Pick Yale if:** you want broad platform compatibility, multiple form-factor options, and ~1-year battery life.
**Pick Encode Plus if:** you want the highest residential security grade and Apple Home Key on a single SKU.

### Aqara U400 vs Schlage Encode Plus
The Aqara U400 leads on technology — first-to-market UWB hands-free unlock, native Matter-over-Thread, Apple Home Key, fingerprint, NFC, and Aliro 1.0 cross-platform support — while the Encode Plus leads on physical security (BHMA Grade 1) and the comfort of a US-incumbent brand with lifetime mechanical warranty. The U400 is rated IP65 only (not BHMA Grade 1). For Apple-first households that want hands-free unlock as a daily quality-of-life upgrade, U400 wins. [src8, src1]

**Pick U400 if:** UWB hands-free unlock, Matter-over-Thread, and Aliro future-proofing matter more than maximum impact-rating.
**Pick Encode Plus if:** BHMA Grade 1 physical security and a US-brand warranty matter more than cutting-edge unlock methods.

### Schlage Arrive vs Schlage Encode Plus
Both are Schlage WiFi deadbolts with ANSI Grade 1 commercial-grade security. The Arrive is ~$120 cheaper, supports 250 access codes (more than Encode Plus), has a rubberized backlit push-button keypad, and lasts ~1 year on AA batteries. The trade-off: no Apple Home Key and no Matter support — ecosystem is limited to Alexa and Google Home. [src7, src6]

**Pick Arrive if:** you don't use Apple Home and want the strongest physical security at $199.
**Pick Encode Plus if:** you want Apple Home Key tap-to-unlock and the matte-black flagship aesthetic.

### Lockly Visage Zeno vs eufy FamiLock E34
Both are biometric-first smart locks, but with very different biometrics. Visage Zeno uses 3D facial recognition (dual infrared, works in darkness, 1-second unlock, 99 fingerprint prints, Apple Home Key) at ~$339. FamiLock E34 uses palm-vein recognition (99.9% claimed accuracy, sub-1-second match, cannot be spoofed by photos or lifted prints, ANSI/BHMA Grade 1) at ~$230. Palm-vein is the more secure biometric in principle (vein patterns are subdermal); face recognition is the faster user experience (no hand-raise gesture required). [src9, src3]

**Pick Visage Zeno if:** you want hands-free face unlock (walk up, glance, in) and Apple Home Key.
**Pick FamiLock E34 if:** you want the highest-security biometric at ~$110 less, with Matter support.

### Aqara U400 vs Level Lock Pro
Both are Apple-first Matter-over-Thread retrofit-friendly locks with Apple Home Key. The U400 adds UWB hands-free unlock, a touchscreen keypad, fingerprint, NFC, and Aliro 1.0 — and costs $80 less ($270 vs $349). The Level Lock Pro's killer feature is invisible installation: all smart components are hidden inside a deadbolt that looks like a traditional lock. [src8, src2]

**Pick U400 if:** UWB hands-free and on-lock biometrics (fingerprint, keypad) matter more than aesthetics.
**Pick Level Lock Pro if:** you want a smart lock that looks like a traditional deadbolt — period.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US retail as of July 2026 and may vary by retailer, finish, and configuration. Mid-2026 has seen meaningful street-price erosion on the Yale Assure Lock 2 (~$170 vs $260 MSRP), Ultraloq Bolt Fingerprint (~$140 vs $180 MSRP), and Kwikset Halo Select (~$246 vs $279 MSRP), while the Schlage Encode Plus has risen to ~$319. Many models are available in multiple variants (with/without Wi-Fi, with/without fingerprint) at different price points.
- Battery life estimates assume typical use (~8-10 lock/unlock cycles per day). Heavy usage, extreme temperatures, or always-on Wi-Fi connections can significantly reduce battery life.
- Smart locks either retrofit an existing deadbolt (Aqara, Level) or replace the entire lock (Yale, Schlage, Kwikset, Ultraloq, eufy). Retrofit locks preserve your existing keys but depend on your current deadbolt's physical security. Full replacements typically offer higher security ratings.
- UWB hands-free unlock on the Aqara U400 requires an iPhone with U1 or U2 chip (iPhone 11 and later) or Apple Watch Series 9/Ultra 2 and later. Older Apple devices fall back to NFC-based Home Key. Samsung Galaxy users get tap-to-unlock today and hands-free UWB rolling out in Q2 2026; Google Pixel and other Android devices will follow.
- Wi-Fi connected locks enable remote access but consume more battery. Bluetooth-only locks require proximity to your phone. Matter-over-Thread locks offer a balance of responsiveness and efficiency but require a Thread border router (Apple TV 4K, HomePod Mini, or equivalent) in the home.
- Compatibility with your specific door type, thickness, and existing hardware should be verified before purchase. Most US-market smart locks fit standard doors with a 2-1/8" borehole, but not all doors qualify.
