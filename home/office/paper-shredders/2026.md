## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Royal 14MC vs Aurora AU120MA
The Royal 14MC is the value champion on paper: 14-sheet manual feed, P-4 micro-cut, ~$60-75 and quiet -- but it is now Currently Unavailable on Amazon and under a Wirecutter defect probe. The Aurora AU120MA costs ~$168, is reliably in stock, and adds 120-sheet auto-feed -- you load a stack and walk away. With the Royal's Amazon supply gone, the Aurora is the safer default for most buyers right now. [src1, src3]

**Pick Royal 14MC if:** you can find it in stock at Costco/Walmart, want the cheapest capable micro-cut shredder, and shred small batches by hand.
**Pick Aurora AU120MA if:** you want an in-stock all-rounder, have stacks to clear, and value hands-free auto-feed.

### Aurora AU1060MA vs Aurora AU120MA
Both are Aurora micro-cut workhorses with 60-min (AU1060MA) / 30-min (AU120MA) run times, but they solve different problems. The AU1060MA is P-5 (highest security, 2,592 particles/page) at ~$185; the AU120MA is P-4 with 120-sheet auto-feed at ~$168. Security vs throughput at nearly the same price. [src2, src5]

**Pick AU1060MA if:** maximum (P-5) document security matters more than speed.
**Pick AU120MA if:** you need to shred large volumes and P-4 micro-cut is enough.

### Bonsaii C234-A vs Amazon Basics 8-Sheet Micro-Cut
Both are budget P-4 micro-cut units, but the price gap has widened back out. The Bonsaii C234-A (~$42) is now clearly the cheaper of the two after the Amazon Basics 8-Sheet climbed to ~$75. The Amazon Basics adds 2 sheets of capacity (8 vs 6) and a larger 4-gal bin, but you pay ~$33 more for it. [src2, src3, src6]

**Pick Bonsaii C234-A if:** you want the lowest price for occasional shredding and a compact footprint.
**Pick Amazon Basics 8-Sheet if:** you'll pay ~$33 more for slightly higher capacity and a bigger bin.

### Royal 14MC vs Fellowes LX220
The Royal 14MC (~$60-75) is the budget pick for individuals; the Fellowes LX220 (~$170-300) is built for shared offices with 100% jam-proof tech, SafeSense hand detection, and a larger 8-gal bin. The LX220 costs 3-4x more but is far harder to jam or misuse. [src3, src4]

**Pick Royal 14MC if:** it's a personal home-office shredder and budget matters.
**Pick Fellowes LX220 if:** multiple people share it and you want jam-proof reliability and hand-safety.

- Security levels follow the DIN 66399 standard: P-3 (cross-cut, readable with effort), P-4 (cross-cut or micro-cut, very difficult to reconstruct), P-5 (micro-cut, virtually impossible to reconstruct). For most home-office needs, P-4 is sufficient; P-5 is recommended for sensitive financial or medical documents.
