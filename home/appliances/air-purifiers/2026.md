## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Coway AP-1512HH Mighty vs Levoit Vital 200S-P
The Mighty (~$220) has a 10+ year Wirecutter track record with proven reliability and quiet 24 dB operation, but HouseFresh now considers it dated. The Vital 200S-P (~$190) delivers higher PM1 CADR (249 vs ~232), pelleted carbon for superior odor removal, and no ionizer. [src1, src3]
**Pick the Mighty if:** you want the longest-proven, quietest pick and don't mind the ionizer (CARB-certified <50 ppb).
**Pick the Vital 200S-P if:** you have pets, want zero ozone risk, or value odor-removal performance per dollar.

### Blueair Blue Signature vs Coway Airmega 350
The Blue Signature (~$294) leads on raw CADR (445/434/450) and covers 700 sq ft with a furniture-grade table-top design and 23 dB low setting. The Airmega 350 (~$339) hits 352 CFM PM1 CADR for 528 sq ft with pelleted carbon and portable styling. With the Signature's price now below the 350, it has become the stronger all-round value. [src3, src5, src8]
**Pick the Blue Signature if:** living room is large or open-plan, design matters, noise sensitivity is the top constraint, or you want the most CADR per dollar.
**Pick the Airmega 350 if:** you specifically want pelleted carbon for cooking odors/VOCs in a medium room.

### Coway Airmega ProX vs IQAir HealthPro Plus
The ProX (~$814) delivers 462 CFM PM1 / 586 CFM dust CADR and is HouseFresh's top large-room pick — it outperformed the $1,400 IQAir Atem X in testing. The HealthPro Plus (~$1,200) has lower CADR (300 combined) but HyperHEPA filters to 0.003 microns with a 4-year filter life. [src3]
**Pick the ProX if:** raw CFM-per-dollar for smoke or large open rooms is the priority.
**Pick the HealthPro Plus if:** medical-grade ultra-fine filtration matters more than CFM (asthma, severe allergies, MCS).

### AirFanta 3Pro vs Levoit Core 600S
The 3Pro (~$165) is HouseFresh's value champion: 353 CFM PM1 CADR for 529 sq ft, collapsible, 7 lbs — but no app, no smart features. The Core 600S-P (~$296) delivers 410 CFM combined CADR for 635 sq ft with WiFi, VeSync app, and smart auto-mode. [src3, src6]
**Pick the 3Pro if:** budget rules, portability matters, or you prefer no electronics/sensors.
**Pick the Core 600S-P if:** you want smart features and a quieter polished package for a large room around $300.

### Levoit Core 300S-P vs Levoit Core 400S
The 300S-P (~$127) is the cheapest True HEPA + smart purifier with 143/153/167 CADR for 222 sq ft. The 400S (~$182) more than doubles CADR (231/240/259) and covers 403 sq ft with the same VeSync ecosystem — and the price gap has narrowed to ~$55. [src2, src6]
**Pick the 300S-P if:** small bedroom/dorm under 250 sq ft and budget is the priority.
**Pick the 400S if:** living room or main-floor coverage, or you cook frequently and want responsive smart auto-mode.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate U.S. street prices as of June 2026 (live Amazon at last verification). Sales, promotions, and regional pricing vary significantly. Models like the Coway AP-1512HH regularly swing 20-40% across Amazon sales events.
- CADR ratings are measured by AHAM using standardized tests with smoke, dust, and pollen particles. HouseFresh uses its own PM1 CADR methodology with incense testing in a controlled 728 sq ft room — their numbers may not directly compare to AHAM-certified CADR.
- Room size ratings from manufacturers typically use 1 air change per hour (ACH). The EPA and AHAM recommend 4-5 ACH for effective purification. Divide the manufacturer's room size by 4-5 to estimate effective coverage.
- Annual filter replacement costs range from $30 (Levoit Core 300S) to $1200 (IQAir HealthPro Plus) and should be factored into total cost of ownership. Running a purifier 24/7 on medium adds $20-50/year in electricity.
- HouseFresh dropped the Blueair Blue Pure 311i Max from its recommended list because the ionizer function cannot be disabled. Users concerned about trace ozone should verify ionizer disable capabilities before purchasing any model with ionizer technology.
