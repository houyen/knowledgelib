## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Dyson V15 Detect vs Shark PowerDetect Speed Clean & Empty
The Dyson V15 Detect wins on outright cleaning power (240 AW, 99.4% debris removal, laser-illuminated head) and 24% lighter weight. The Shark PowerDetect counters with a self-emptying 2L dock (45 days of debris), a longer 70-min runtime, and a ~$95 lower street price (~$400 vs the V15's ~$495). For a single primary vacuum that handles all floors, the V15 is the more capable cleaner; for households tired of emptying bins, the PowerDetect wins on convenience. [src1, src2, src5]
**Pick the V15 if:** you want the best raw cleaning performance, lower weight, and a brand with the deepest review-test history.
**Pick the PowerDetect if:** you want set-and-forget emptying, longer runtime per charge, and ~$95 saved.

### Dyson V15 Detect vs Dyson Gen5detect
Both share Dyson's Fluffy Optic head and HEPA filtration; the Gen5 adds a more powerful Hyperdymium motor (280 AW vs 240 AW), 10 extra minutes of runtime (70 vs 60), and higher-grade 0.1-micron filtration vs the V15's 0.3-micron. The V15 weighs ~0.8 lb less and, after its mid-2026 price drop, costs ~$300 less (~$495 vs ~$799). The Gen5detect is the absolute performance king; the V15 is now a standout value pick. [src1, src2, src3]
**Pick the V15 if:** the ~$300 saving matters and 240 AW is enough for your floors.
**Pick the Gen5detect if:** you have heavy carpets, pets, or want the highest-rated cordless cleaner ever tested.

### Samsung Bespoke AI Jet Ultra vs Dyson Gen5detect
Two premium flagships approaching the same problem differently. The Samsung wins on suction (400 AW vs 280 AW), runtime (100 vs 70 min), and includes an All-in-One Clean Station for auto-emptying — but Vacuum Wars' airflow tests still put the Gen5detect ahead on real-world carpet removal (69 CFM, 40/40 deep-clean score). The Samsung now carries a ~$300 premium (~$1,100 MSRP via Samsung.com vs the Gen5detect's ~$799 on Amazon) as of June 2026. [src1, src2, src4, src7]
**Pick the Samsung if:** you want the longest runtime, self-empty dock, and SmartThings integration in a single buy.
**Pick the Gen5detect if:** you prioritize raw deep-clean test scores and the Dyson ecosystem of cleaning heads.

### Tineco Pure ONE A90S vs Dyson V15 Detect
At ~$749 the Tineco A90S now sits ~$254 above the price-dropped V15 (~$495) while offering 30 AW more suction, 45 more minutes of runtime, and ~1.8 lbs less weight. The V15 wins on tested debris-removal score (99.4% vs ~96% for the A90S), brand-support depth, and -- after its mid-2026 discount -- price. For gadget-friendly buyers in larger homes who want maximum runtime and the lightest build, the A90S remains a credible flagship alternative. [src4, src7]
**Pick the A90S if:** you want maximum runtime, lighter weight, and smart-sensor features, and the price premium is acceptable.
**Pick the V15 if:** you value Dyson's longer service network, proven debris-pickup numbers, and the lower ~$495 price.

### Shark Stratos Cordless vs Tineco Pure ONE S70
Two strong mid-range picks at ~$350 vs ~$399. The Stratos wins on raw cleaning power (300W HyperVelocity Plus, Vacuum Wars double-winner) and Odor Neutralizer. The S70 wins on runtime (95 min vs 60 min), lighter weight, and ZeroTangle hair management. For pet households on a budget, the Stratos delivers more suction per dollar; for whole-home cleaning sessions, the S70's battery lasts longer. [src1, src2, src4]
**Pick the Stratos if:** you want the most powerful Shark cordless and best-in-class carpet/pet-hair pickup at a budget price.
**Pick the S70 if:** you need 90+ minutes of runtime and prefer a lighter, lower-noise build.

- Prices shown are approximate U.S. street prices as of June 2026. Cordless vacuum prices fluctuate significantly; Dyson, Shark, and Samsung frequently run 20-40% off sales on Amazon, direct sites, and during holiday events. The Dyson Gen5detect's 70-minute claim translates to roughly 40 minutes in Auto mode and 15 minutes on Boost. Levoit quotes Watts. Direct comparisons across brands require independent third-party testing results rather than manufacturer specs alone.
- Even the most powerful cordless stick vacuums cannot fully match the deep-cleaning performance of a quality corded upright or canister vacuum on thick carpets. For homes with predominantly thick carpet flooring, a cordless stick vacuum works best as a supplement.
- Dustbin capacities range from 0.35L (Dyson V12 Detect Slim) to 0.8L (Tineco A90S). Homes with pets or heavy debris should prioritize larger bins or self-emptying systems to avoid frequent interruptions.
