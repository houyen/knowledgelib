# Best Portable Dishwashers (2026)

## What are the best portable dishwashers in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: GE GPT225SSLSS (~$849) — cleaning equal to a built-in, 12 place settings, but now discontinued and dealer-stock only.**
**Best rolling you can actually buy: SPT SD-9263W (~$584) — 8 place settings, adjustable upper rack, in stock.**

## Summary

The portable dishwasher market in 2026 divides into two distinct product categories. **Full-size rolling portable dishwashers** like the GE GPT series connect to your kitchen faucet, roll on casters, and clean as well as built-in dishwashers with 8-12 place settings. Wirecutter has recommended the GE GPT series for five consecutive years, praising its cleaning performance, ease of setup, and functional countertop surface. However, these units cost $800-1,050 and are essentially full-size appliances on wheels. [src1, src2]

**Availability has become the deciding factor in the rolling category.** GE now lists the 24-inch GPT225SSLSS as no longer manufactured, and both GE portables read "Currently Unavailable" at Reviewed and on Amazon; remaining stock sits with appliance dealers (Abt, Plesser's, Sears, regional GE dealers) at roughly $794-$850. The Danby DDW1805EWP is also off Amazon and has climbed to about $741 at Home Depot — no longer the budget alternative it was. The **SPT SD-9263W** (~$584) is the only rolling portable in this comparison currently in stock on Amazon, which makes it the practical rolling pick even though the GE still cleans better. [src2, src5, src8]

**Compact countertop dishwashers** are smaller units (typically 17-22 inches wide) that sit on your counter and hold 2-6 place settings. The **Danby DDW631SDB** leads this category, with Reviewed finding it cleared about 96% of test stains in just 100 minutes — performance comparable to full-size dishwashers; it now runs about $425 at Home Depot and is off Amazon. Many countertop models like the Euhomy and Farberware include built-in 5L water tanks, meaning they require no faucet hookup at all — ideal for dorms, RVs, and kitchens with incompatible faucets. Note that the Farberware has repriced sharply upward (about $310) and Reviewed now rates it best for *cleaning* (99%+ stain removal) rather than best for budget — that role passes to the **Euhomy** (~$249), which also holds twice as many place settings. [src3, src4]

An important note: Danby, RCA, and SPT rolling portable dishwashers are all manufactured by Midea on the same platform, so cleaning and drying performance is nearly identical across brands. The GE GPT models may also use Midea internals but with different racks and hose assemblies. The main differentiator is build quality and feature set (GE's Piranha hard food disposer and Sanitize cycle stand out). [src1, src2]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### GE GPT225SSLSS vs Danby DDW631SDB
The GE is a full-size rolling dishwasher (12 place settings, ~$849) while the Danby is a countertop unit (6 place settings, ~$425). They are fundamentally different products. The GE delivers built-in-quality cleaning with a Piranha disposer and Sanitize cycle. The Danby is compact and affordable but limited to smaller loads. Both clear 96%+ of stains in testing. Both are currently unavailable on Amazon; the GE is additionally discontinued, while the Danby is still in production at Home Depot and Walmart. [src1, src3, src8]

**Pick GE GPT225SSLSS if:** you have floor space and want performance equal to a built-in dishwasher.
**Pick Danby DDW631SDB if:** you need a compact, affordable countertop solution and wash smaller loads.

### GE GPT225SSLSS vs GE GPT145SSLSS
Both GE models share the same excellent cleaning technology, Piranha disposer, and Sanitize cycle. The 24-inch model holds 12 place settings vs 8 for the 18-inch. The 18-inch model is slightly quieter (53 vs 54 dBA) and fits through narrower doorways. Pricing is similar ($849-939). [src1, src2]

**Pick GE GPT225SSLSS if:** you regularly wash dishes for 3+ people and have space for a 24-inch unit.
**Pick GE GPT145SSLSS if:** you need a narrower unit or wash dishes for 1-2 people.

### GE GPT145SSLSS vs Danby DDW1805EWP
Both are 18-inch rolling portable dishwashers with 8 place settings. The GE (~$849-939) includes the Piranha hard food disposer, Sanitize cycle, and a more durable stainless steel interior. The Danby has only 4 cycles vs GE's 3 cycles plus multiple options. Both connect to a faucet and roll on casters, and both are currently unavailable on Amazon. The Danby's price advantage has narrowed to roughly $100 now that it sells for ~$741, which makes the GE much easier to justify if you can find one in dealer stock. [src1, src2]

**Pick GE GPT145SSLSS if:** you want the best cleaning performance and can source it from Best Buy or a GE dealer.
**Pick Danby DDW1805EWP if:** you find it discounted well below $741 — at list price the GE out-cleans it for about $100 more.

### SPT SD-9263W vs Danby DDW1805EWP
The two mainstream non-GE rolling portables, both 18-inch/8-place-setting Midea-platform units. The SPT is ~$584 and in stock on Amazon; the Danby is ~$741 and unavailable there. The SPT adds an adjustable upper rack and a shallower cabinet for galley kitchens; the Danby is marginally quieter at 52 dBA. On current pricing and availability the SPT wins this matchup decisively. [src2, src5]

**Pick SPT SD-9263W if:** you want a rolling portable you can order today, or you need the shallower depth or adjustable rack.
**Pick Danby DDW1805EWP if:** you specifically want the quietest rolling unit and find it back in stock near its old ~$500-570 street price.

### Euhomy vs Farberware (No-Hookup Countertop)
Both feature 5L built-in water tanks and require no faucet connection. The Euhomy holds 4 place settings vs the Farberware's 2 settings and has 6 programs vs 5. Both include Baby Care and Fruit Wash modes. The Euhomy accommodates plates up to 9.5 inches. The price relationship has inverted since the last review: the Euhomy is now ~$249 and in stock, while the Farberware has climbed to ~$310 and is unavailable on Amazon. The Farberware's remaining claim is wash quality — Reviewed measured 99%+ stain removal against the field's typical 96%. [src3, src4, src5]

**Pick Euhomy if:** you want the better all-round value — cheaper, double the capacity, more programs, and actually in stock.
**Pick Farberware if:** you wash for one person and want the best-cleaning countertop unit regardless of the price premium.
