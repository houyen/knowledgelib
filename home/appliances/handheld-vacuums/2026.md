# Best Handheld Vacuums (2026)

## What are the best handheld vacuums in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: [Shark UltraCyclone Pet Pro+ CH951](https://knowledgelib.io/go/shark-ultracyclone-pet-pro-plus) (~$70)** — strongest all-round cyclonic suction with versatile attachments.

## Summary

The handheld vacuum market in 2026 is dominated by cordless lithium-ion models ranging from $25 to $300, with most top performers falling in the $45-$100 range. The Shark UltraCyclone Pet Pro+ CH951 (~$70) is the best overall pick for its powerful cyclonic suction, versatile attachments, and strong performance across bare floors, upholstery, and pet hair. For budget buyers, the Black+Decker Dustbuster Blast HNVD220J00 (~$45) delivers reliable cleaning at Wirecutter's recommended price point. The Bissell Pet Hair Eraser 2390A (~$80) achieved the highest dirt pickup rate (94%) in Reviewed.com's lab tests, making it the top choice for pet owners. [src1, src3, src4]

The biggest differentiators among modern handheld vacuums are suction power (measured in air watts), battery runtime (9-50 minutes for cordless models), dust bin capacity (2.5-25 oz), and weight (1.3-3.8 lbs). Cordless models now dominate the category, though corded options like the Dirt Devil Scorpion still offer unlimited runtime for dedicated tasks. As of mid-2026 the **Dyson Car+Boat (~$229)** is the premium pick to buy: it packs the same 115 AW Hyperdymium motor and 50-minute low-power runtime as the now-discontinued Dyson Humdinger, in a pistol-grip form factor, and Consumer Reports names it the single longest-runtime handheld on high power (~24 minutes). The Dyson Humdinger has been retired from Dyson's lineup and is out of stock on Amazon — it is no longer a current option, and the Car+Boat is its direct successor. [src2, src4, src6, src8, src9]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Dyson Car+Boat vs Dyson Humdinger
Both used the same 115 AW Hyperdymium motor and HEPA filtration. The Humdinger was lighter (2.2 lbs vs 4.0 lbs) and slightly more versatile around the home; the Car+Boat is purpose-built for vehicle interiors with a pistol grip and is the only handheld Consumer Reports measured at ~24 minutes on high power. This matchup is now decided by availability: the Humdinger has been discontinued and is out of stock, while the Car+Boat (~$229) is Dyson's current premium handheld — so the Car+Boat is the only one you can reliably buy today. [src2, src9]

**Pick the Car+Boat if:** you want a current-generation Dyson handheld with the longest high-power runtime — this is the buyable option.
**Consider the Humdinger only if:** you specifically want the lighter 2.2 lb form factor and can find leftover third-party stock (Dyson no longer sells it).

### Shark UltraCyclone Pet Pro+ vs Bissell Pet Hair Eraser 2390A
The Shark (~$70) is the better all-rounder — strong cyclonic suction across bare floors, upholstery, and pet hair with versatile attachments. The Bissell (~$80) wins specifically on pet hair, with the highest 94% dirt pickup in Reviewed.com's lab tests and a category-leading 24 oz dust bin, but it is less impressive on bare floors. [src3, src4, src7]

**Pick the Shark if:** you need one handheld that does everything well.
**Pick the Bissell if:** pet hair on upholstery and furniture is your dominant use case.

### Bissell Pet Hair Eraser 2390A vs Black+Decker Furbuster AdvancedClean+
Both are dedicated pet-hair handhelds; the Furbuster (~$67) now undercuts the Bissell (~$80).
