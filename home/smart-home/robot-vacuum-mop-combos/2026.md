## Summary

The robot vacuum-mop combo market keeps moving fast into mid-2026. The **Dreame X60 Max Ultra Complete** (~$1360 street, down from $1,699 MSRP) is still Vacuum Wars' Best Overall for June 2026 with a 4.20 score — the highest ever recorded — and its ~$1360 street price now undercuts the Roborock Saros 20. Its 35,000 Pa suction, 3.13-inch ultra-slim profile with retractable LiDAR (VersaLift), 100% flattened pet-hair pickup, and 212°F boiling-water mop wash make it the most powerful robot vacuum-mop tested to date. On the value side, the **Eufy E25 Omni** (~$650) — which is now consistently listed on Amazon US, no longer DTC-only — remains the strongest sub-$700 all-rounder: 20,000 Pa Turbo suction, the HydroJet continuous-rinse roller mop that scored 218 in combined mopping tests (above category average), 88% carpet deep-clean, 93% flattened-pet-hair pickup, and 0% tangle in 7-inch hair tests. [src1, src2, src5, src9]

The **Roborock Saros 20** (~$1,600) remains the navigation flagship: 36,000 Pa suction, StarSight 2.0 3D ToF, AdaptiLift Chassis 3.0 crossing 3.46-inch double-layer thresholds, 212°F hot-water mop washing, and the only model in the lineup with confirmed native Matter support for Apple HomeKit and Samsung SmartThings. The **Dreame L60 Pro Ultra** (~$1,400) brings the 35,000 Pa flagship suction down a tier, with ProLeap legs crossing 3.47-inch thresholds and the same VersaLift retractable LiDAR — a strong middle-bracket option for buyers who want X60 power without the X60 price. The **Narwal Flow 2** (~$1500) still leads on mopping and is now available on Amazon US at ~$1500 (previously US-direct / Best Buy only). [src2, src3, src6]

Budget remains aggressive: the **MOVA P10 Pro Ultra** (~$359) holds strong with LiDAR navigation, 140°F hot-water mop washing, and a 198-minute battery covering ~2,157 sq ft. The **Dreame L50 Ultra** (~$950) is the highest-scoring dual-spin-mop sub-$1,000 robot at 4.07 on Vacuum Wars and a sensible pick for buyers who prefer dual-spinning mops over rollers. The **Ecovacs Deebot X11 OmniCyclone** (~$739) and **Yeedi S20 Infinity** (~$700) round out the mid-range with bagless OmniCyclone stations and self-washing roller mops. In June 2026 Vacuum Wars named two newer models category winners — the **Ecovacs Deebot T90 Pro Omni** (~$900, 30,000 Pa, OZMO Roller 3.0) as Best Value and the **Eufy Omni C28** (~$530) as Best Budget — both driven by the same continuous-rinse roller-mop trend. [src1, src2, src7]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Dreame X60 Max Ultra Complete (~$1360) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dreame-x60-max-ultra-complete)
The X60 Max Ultra Complete holds the #1 overall position on Vacuum Wars with a 4.20 score — the highest ever recorded, and it retained Best Overall for June 2026. Its 35,000 Pa suction paired with a 3.13-inch ultra-slim body (VersaLift retractable LiDAR) cleans under furniture other robots cannot reach. It achieved an 89% carpet deep-clean score, 100% flattened pet-hair pickup, and elite obstacle avoidance. The dock washes mops at 212°F and auto-dispenses detergent. Main drawback: software can be finicky out of the box — but the street price has fallen to ~$1360 (from $1,699 MSRP), now undercutting the Roborock Saros 20. [src1, src2, src5]

### Best Value: Eufy E25 Omni (~$650) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/eufy-e25-omni)
The strongest sub-$700 all-rounder, and now consistently listed on Amazon US (it launched DTC via eufy.com but its Amazon ASIN is live and in stock). The headline feature is the HydroJet roller mop: clean water is continuously fed to the roller while dirty water is squeegeed into a separate onboard tank, eliminating the dirty-mop-cross-contamination problem of dual-spinning pads. It scored 218 in combined mopping tests (well above category average), 115 on dried stains, 88% carpet deep-clean, 93% flattened pet-hair pickup, and 0% tangle in 7-inch hair tests. (In June 2026 Vacuum Wars rotated its formal "Best Value" title to the newer Ecovacs Deebot T90 Pro Omni, but the E25 remains the best-balance pick at this price.) [src1, src9]

### Best Value (Vacuum Wars, June 2026): Ecovacs Deebot T90 Pro Omni (~$900) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/ecovacs-deebot-t90-pro-omni)
Vacuum Wars named the T90 Pro Omni its Best Value pick in June 2026 with a 4.09 score. It pairs 30,000 Pa suction with the OZMO Roller 3.0 continuous-self-cleaning mop, which scored 157 points in stain tests — nearly 50% above the category average. TruEdge deep-edge cleaning, AIVI 3D obstacle avoidance, hot-water mop washing, and 145°F air drying give it a near-flagship dock at $900. It steps above the Eufy E25 Omni on raw suction and edge coverage while staying well under the flagship tier. [src1, src2]

### Best Budget: MOVA P10 Pro Ultra (~$359) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/mova-p10-pro-ultra)
At ~$359 (list $399), the MOVA P10 Pro Ultra offers LiDAR navigation with multi-level mapping, 13,000 Pa suction, 140°F hot-water mop washing with hot-air drying, auto-emptying, and a 198-minute battery covering ~2,157 sq ft per charge. Its 4.51 battery efficiency score is significantly above average, and the dock handles dust, mop washing, and drying for near hands-free operation. (June 2026: Vacuum Wars moved its formal budget crown to the newer Eufy Omni C28 (~$530), but the P10 remains the cheapest full-dock LiDAR combo here.) [src1, src2]

### Best Step-Down Flagship: Dreame L60 Pro Ultra (~$1400) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dreame-l60-pro-ultra)
