### Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 vs MOVA P10 Pro Ultra
The best-overall vs best-value matchup, both now firmly under $500. The Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 (~$400) wins on raw suction (25,000Pa vs 13,000Pa), extendable side brush and mop arm, and RoboRatings' #1 ranking (9.0/10). [src1, src3, src4]

**Pick Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 if:** you want the highest suction and best carpet deep-cleaning under $500 and don't mind paying ~$40 more.
**Pick MOVA P10 Pro Ultra if:** you want the lowest price with predictable street pricing and strong navigation for the money.

### Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 vs MOVA P10 Pro Ultra Gen 2
The two highest-suction robots under $500, both now sub-$500 after July price drops. The Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 (~$400) leads on 25,000Pa suction, extendable side brush and mop arm, 3DAdapt obstacle avoidance, and RoboRatings' #1 under-$500 ranking. The MOVA P10 Pro Ultra Gen 2 (~$419) leads with 26,000Pa suction, a 212°F hot-water dock (vs Dreame's lower temp), and 1-hour hot-air mop drying. [src1, src3, src4]

**Pick Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 if:** you want the top-rated all-rounder and best edge-cleaning thanks to the extendable side brush and mop arm.
**Pick MOVA P10 Pro Ultra Gen 2 if:** you have pets and want the hottest mop-washing dock (212°F) in this tier for hygiene.

### Yeedi M14 Plus vs Ecovacs Deebot T80 Omni
Both are sibling Ecovacs-family OZMO Roller mop robots (Yeedi is owned by Ecovacs). The M14 Plus (~$450) edges out the T80 Omni (~$420) on mop spin rate (200 RPM with 4,000Pa pressure vs T80's 3,700Pa) and battery life (241 min vs 200 min). The T80 Omni now wins on price (~$420 vs ~$450) plus TruEdge deep edge cleaning and ZeroTangle 3.0; both share 18,000Pa suction. Both use the same OZMO Roller architecture and 167°F hot water dock. [src6, src3]

**Pick Yeedi M14 Plus if:** mopping is your top priority and you want the best mop performance under $500.
**Pick Ecovacs Deebot T80 Omni if:** you want the lower price plus the ZeroTangle 3.0 brush system, TruEdge cleaning, and broader retailer support.

### MOVA P10 Pro Ultra vs Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2
The mid-price value matchup. MOVA P10 Pro Ultra (~$359) leads on suction (13,000Pa vs 10,000Pa), RGB camera + 3D-light obstacle avoidance (vs Dreame's 3D structured light only), extendable mop arm for edge cleaning, and now a lower price. Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2 (~$400) wins on battery life (240 min vs 198 min), MopExtend RoboSwing edge tech, and Dreame's longer-established software ecosystem. [src1, src2, src4]

**Pick MOVA P10 Pro Ultra if:** you want stronger suction and RGB-camera object recognition for pet/cord avoidance.
**Pick Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2 if:** you want longer runtime and prefer the more mature Dreamehome app for whole-home maps.

### Roborock Q7 M5+ vs Ecovacs Deebot N20 Plus
The under-$300 LiDAR matchup. Roborock Q7 M5+ (~$300) wins on dock capacity (7-9 weeks vs 45 days), PreciSense LiDAR (slightly more mature mapping), and JawScrapers anti-tangle.
