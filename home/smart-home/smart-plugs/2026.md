# Best Smart Plugs (2026)

## What are the best smart plugs in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Kasa KP125M (~$10/plug)** — Matter + energy monitoring in a slim non-blocking design, the cross-site consensus winner. **Best value: Kasa EP25 4-pack (~$9/plug)** — accurate energy monitoring at the lowest per-plug price. **Best budget: Tapo P125M (~$10)** — cheapest single Matter plug. ## Summary

The smart plug market in 2026 has matured significantly, with Matter protocol support now standard across most top picks, enabling cross-platform compatibility between Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Samsung SmartThings. Prices range from ~$10 to ~$35 per plug, with the best options falling in the $10-$25 range. The TP-Link Kasa KP125M (~$10/plug in 2-packs) is the best overall smart plug — the consensus pick across Engadget, Wirecutter, Reviewed, and CNN Underscored — combining Matter certification, energy monitoring, and a compact form factor that does not block adjacent outlets. The Leviton Decora D215P 2nd Gen, which added Matter via firmware update, has dropped to ~$23 (from ~$36) and is now an affordable choice for HomeKit-first households that also want cross-platform flexibility. [src1, src2, src3, src4, src5]

For budget buyers, the TP-Link Tapo P125M (~$10) delivers Matter compatibility in one of the smallest form factors available, though it lacks energy monitoring. The Eve Energy (~$33) stands out as the best option for Apple HomeKit users, offering Matter over Thread connectivity for faster response times and detailed energy tracking without any cloud dependency. Energy monitoring has moved from a premium feature to an expected one, with the Emporia Smart Plug (~$12) offering the most detailed per-device power analytics at the lowest price point, while the Kasa EP25 4-pack (~$9/plug) is Wirecutter's value pick for accurate energy tracking. For outdoor use, Tom's Guide's March 2026 update confirms the TP-Link Tapo P400M (~$20) remains the best overall outdoor pick, with IP65, dual-outlet independent control, and 300-foot Wi-Fi range; the Kasa EP40A outdoor plug has fallen to ~$20, matching it on price. [src2, src3, src4, src8]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: TP-Link Kasa KP125M (~$10/plug) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/kasa-kp125m)
The Kasa KP125M is the first "no compromise" smart plug, combining Matter certification with energy monitoring in a slim design that does not block adjacent outlets. It works instantly with Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and Samsung SmartThings. The Kasa app provides real-time and historical power consumption data, including estimated electricity costs based on your utility rate. Sold in 2-packs (~$21) and 4-packs (~$40), it offers excellent per-unit value. Note that energy monitoring data is only available through the Kasa app, not through Matter-connected platforms. [src1, src2, src4]

### Best Budget: TP-Link Tapo P125M (~$10) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/tapo-p125m)
One of the smallest Matter-certified smart plugs on the market, the Tapo P125M is compact enough to stack two in a single duplex outlet without blocking either socket. At ~$10 per plug (or roughly $6.50/plug in the 3-pack), it is one of the most affordable ways into the Matter ecosystem. Setup is hub-free via Bluetooth and a Matter QR code. It handles up to 15A/1800W loads, suitable for lamps, coffee makers, fans, and other household appliances. It lacks energy monitoring, which is its main trade-off at this price. [src2, src3, src6]

### Best for Energy Monitoring: Emporia Smart Plug (~$12) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/emporia-smart-plug)
The Emporia Smart Plug offers the most detailed per-device energy monitoring at the lowest price point. It tracks real-time power draw, daily/weekly/monthly consumption, and estimated costs through the Emporia app. Ideal for identifying energy-hungry appliances like space heaters, dehumidifiers, and old refrigerators. It integrates with the Emporia Vue whole-home energy monitor for comprehensive tracking. The 10A continuous load limit (15A peak) is lower than most competitors, making it unsuitable for high-draw appliances above 1,200W. [src3, src4]

### Best for Apple HomeKit: Eve Energy (Matter) (~$33) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/eve-energy-matter)
The Eve Energy was the first smart plug to adopt Thread technology, providing lightning-fast response times and reliable operation without relying on Wi-Fi or cloud services. With Matter certification, it now works across all major platforms, though it remains the best choice for Apple-centric homes where Thread border routers (HomePod, Apple TV 4K) are already present. Its energy tracking provides detailed cost projections, and Eve's 100% privacy commitment means no cloud registration, no tracking, and all communication stays local. Requires a Thread border router (HomePod mini, Apple TV 4K, or equivalent). [src2, src4, src5]

### Best Outdoor: TP-Link Tapo P400M (~$20) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/tapo-p400m)
Tom's Guide's March 2026 refresh confirms the Tapo P400M as the best overall outdoor smart plug. It offers Matter support, two independently controllable 15A outlets, and IP65 weather resistance rated for -4 to 122 degrees Fahrenheit. Its dual-antenna Wi-Fi delivers a 300-foot range, ensuring reliable connectivity for garden lights, holiday displays, and patio equipment placed far from the router. Comprehensive scheduling (including sunset/sunrise automations) is handled natively in the Tapo app. It undercuts most outdoor smart plugs while offering Matter compatibility that most competitors lack. [src3, src7, src8]

### Best Multi-Pack Value: TP-Link Kasa EP25 (~$9/plug in 4-packs) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/kasa-ep25)
The Kasa EP25 delivers fuss-free, reliable performance at ~$9 per plug in 4-packs (~$35). It supports Apple HomeKit, Alexa, Google Home, and IFTTT, with energy monitoring through the Kasa app. Its compact design does not block adjacent outlets. While it lacks Matter certification (unlike the newer KP125M), it remains one of the most reliable and widely recommended smart plugs, with Wirecutter naming it its overall top pick (99.8% schedule accuracy across a 14-month test) and the best sub-$20 energy-monitoring plug for bulk deployments. [src1, src4]

### Best for Matter/Thread: Eve Energy (Matter) (~$33) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/eve-energy-matter)
For homes already invested in Thread mesh networking, the Eve Energy operates over Matter via Thread rather than Wi-Fi, reducing network congestion and improving responsiveness. Each Eve Energy plug also extends your Thread mesh network's range. Combined with its energy monitoring, local-only processing, and TUV/UL certification, it represents the most future-proof smart plug available, though at a premium price. [src2, src4, src5]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Kasa KP125M vs Tapo P125M
Both are TP-Link Matter plugs, but the KP125M (~$10/plug) adds energy monitoring and a slightly larger footprint, while the Tapo P125M (~$10 single, ~$6.50/plug in 3-packs) is smaller and cheaper per plug in bulk but drops energy tracking. The KP125M is the better all-rounder; the P125M wins purely on size and bulk price. [src1, src2]
**Pick KP125M if:** you want energy monitoring and the consensus best-overall pick. **Pick Tapo P125M if:** you are buying several plugs and do not need power tracking.

### Kasa KP125M vs Leviton D215P (2nd Gen)
The Leviton D215P dropped to ~$23 in 2026 (from ~$36), narrowing its gap with the KP125M (~$10/plug). Both are Matter plugs that work with Apple Home, Alexa, and Google; the KP125M adds energy monitoring, while the Leviton offers Wire-Free Anywhere Switch companions and a beginner-friendly My Leviton app. [src1, src3]
**Pick KP125M if:** energy monitoring and lowest cost matter most. **Pick Leviton D215P if:** you want companion switch accessories or prefer Leviton's ecosystem.

### Eve Energy vs Kasa KP125M (for Apple HomeKit)
The Eve Energy (~$33) runs Matter over Thread for sub-second, local-only response and extends your Thread mesh, but costs roughly 3x the KP125M (~$10/plug), which reaches HomeKit over Wi-Fi via Matter. [src2, src4, src5]
**Pick Eve Energy if:** you have a Thread border router and want the fastest, fully-local Apple experience. **Pick KP125M if:** you want HomeKit compatibility at a fraction of the price and do not need Thread.

### Tapo P400M vs Kasa EP40A (outdoor)
Both are dual-outlet outdoor plugs at essentially the same price now (~$20 each). The Tapo P400M adds Matter certification and IP65; the Kasa EP40A is IP64 and lacks Matter but supports Apple HomeKit/Siri natively. [src3, src7, src8]
**Pick Tapo P400M if:** you want Matter cross-platform and higher IP65 weather sealing. **Pick Kasa EP40A if:** you are HomeKit-first and want to save a few dollars.

### Emporia Smart Plug vs Kasa KP125M (for energy monitoring)
Both are sub-$15 with energy tracking. The Emporia (~$12) gives the most granular per-device analytics and AI energy insights but is limited to 10A continuous (1,200W) and lacks Matter. The KP125M (~$10/plug) handles 15A/1800W and adds Matter cross-platform support. [src3, src4]
**Pick Emporia if:** you want the deepest energy analytics for low-draw devices. **Pick KP125M if:** you need higher wattage headroom plus Matter.

--> Any Matter-certified plug delivers this out of the box. The Kasa KP125M (~$10/plug) is the best overall value with full four-platform support plus energy monitoring. Avoid the Emporia Smart Plug (10A continuous / 1,200W) for heaters and dehumidifiers. Never use any of these plugs on motor-driven compressors (window ACs, refrigerators) — startup surges exceed rated amperage. [src2, src3]

### Default recommendation
--> If requirements are unknown or general-purpose, recommend the TP-Link Kasa KP125M (~$10/plug in 2-packs). It covers every major voice platform via Matter, includes energy monitoring, has a compact non-blocking design, and is the consensus top pick across Wirecutter, Reviewed, Engadget, and CNN Underscored. [src1, src2, src4, src5]

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Matter protocol adoption**: Matter has become the dominant smart home interoperability standard. Most new smart plugs now ship with Matter certification, enabling seamless cross-platform control between Apple Home, Alexa, Google Home, and SmartThings without brand-specific apps. [src2, src3, src4]
