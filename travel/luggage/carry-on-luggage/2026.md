# Best Carry-On Luggage (2026)

## What is the best carry-on luggage in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: [July Carry On With CaseSafe](https://knowledgelib.io/go/july-carry-on-with-casesafe) (~$260)** — Men's Journal's Best Carry-On of 2026, the only bag with native Apple Find My + Google Find Hub tracking built into the TSA lock, now under $260. **Best value: [Samsonite Freeform 21"](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsonite-freeform-21-carry-on) (~$84)** — Outdoor Gear Lab's "Best Buy" hardshell, a 6.6 lb spinner with a 10-year warranty at a deep discount. **Best budget: [Rockland Melbourne 20"](https://knowledgelib.io/go/rockland-melbourne-20-carry-on) (~$43)** — spinner wheels and expansion at the lowest price. ## Summary

The carry-on luggage market in 2026 is defined by a clear divide between softside bags favored by frequent business travelers and hardshell options preferred for durability and protection — plus an emerging third category: smart-trackable bags. After synthesizing hands-on testing from Wirecutter (200+ hours of research), GearJunkie (20 bags tested across 10+ flights), Outdoor Gear Lab (retested Nov 2025), and CNN Underscored (750,000+ miles of testing), the Travelpro Platinum Elite 21" Expandable Spinner remains the best overall softside pick for most travelers, combining premium organization, self-aligning MagnaTrac wheels, and a lifetime warranty at ~$295 [src1, src2, src3]. For hardshell enthusiasts, the Away Bigger Carry-On (~$250) delivers exceptional capacity at 47.9 liters with a polycarbonate shell and built-in compression system [src4, src5].

The defining product launch of early 2026 is the **July Carry On With CaseSafe** — the world's first suitcase with an integrated, trackable TSA lock that works with both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub natively (no AirTag required). Named Best Overall Carry-On of 2026 by Men's Journal, it combines July's aerospace-grade German polycarbonate shell, silent spinner wheels, and an ejectable USB-C battery with built-in tracking that survives battery separation, all in a 7.4 lb, 20" shell. Its Amazon street price has now fallen to ~$260 (list $325, down from the ~$495 launch pricing), making it markedly cheaper than every premium softside pick and the strongest default for 2026 [src9, src10]. The CaseSafe lock is being positioned as a new industry standard — Victorinox's Spectra 3.0 has since adopted the same dual-platform tracking — with Outdoor Gear Lab's November 2025 retest confirming July's thesis that connected luggage is the next durable platform shift [src2].

Budget-conscious travelers have exceptionally strong options in 2026, with the Rockland Melbourne 20" available for ~$43, the Samsonite Freeform 21" discounted to ~$84, and the Travelpro Maxlite Air V2 at ~$124, all delivering core features like spinner wheels and TSA locks without the premium price tag [src3, src7]. At the luxury end, the TUMI Alpha International Dual Access Carry-On (~$950) and Briggs & Riley Baseline Essential (~$729) justify their prices with ballistic nylon construction, lifetime warranties, and business-focused organization features [src1, src2, src5, src6]. The ultralight trend also continues to mature — the July Carry-On Light Expandable at just 4.9 lbs remains the lightest hardside spinner tested and is now available on Amazon (~$275), solving the perennial weight-vs-capacity trade-off [src7, src8]. Front-access laptop compartments (Monos Carry-On Pro, Delsey Chatelet Air 2.0), expansion zippers adding 2-8 liters of bonus capacity, and USB-C charging have become table stakes across nearly every model [src1, src2, src3, src5].

The aerospace-grade German polycarbonate shell, anodized aluminum inner bumpers, and silent spinner wheels match the build quality of competitors at twice the weight, in a 7.4 lb, 20" frame. With its Amazon price now down to ~$260 (list $325, from ~$495 at launch), it has shifted from a premium novelty to outright the best value among premium bags — undercutting every softside flagship. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### July Carry On With CaseSafe vs Travelpro Platinum Elite 21"
The CaseSafe (~$260, hardshell, 7.4 lbs) is the trackable, protect-your-contents choice; the Platinum Elite (~$295, softside, 7.8 lbs) is the pack-more, organize-better classic. With the CaseSafe now ~$35 cheaper than the Travelpro, the decision is no longer about price — it is shell preference and whether built-in Apple Find My + Google Find Hub tracking matters to you. [src1, src2, src9]
**Pick the CaseSafe if:** you have ever had a bag lost or gate-checked, want native tracking with no AirTag, and prefer a hardshell. **Pick the Platinum Elite if:** you want maximum packing organization, a softside that flexes into tight bins, garment-friendly interior, and Wirecutter's longest-running top pick.

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Trackable luggage becomes the new default conversation**: July's CaseSafe (launched March 2026) is the first carry-on with a built-in TSA lock that speaks natively to both Apple Find My and Google Find Hub, with the tracker powered independently of the ejectable USB-C battery. By mid-2026 its Amazon street price had fallen from ~$495 to ~$260 (list $325) as stock stabilized, and Victorinox's Spectra 3.0 became the first competitor to adopt the same dual-platform tracking — confirming The Points Guy's and Men's Journal's read of this as a platform shift rather than a one-off feature. Expect Away, Monos, and Samsonite to respond within 12-18 months. [src2, src9, src10]
- **Ultralight carry-ons gaining momentum**: The July Carry-On Light Expandable at 4.9 lbs represents a wave of sub-5-lb hard-shell spinners that eliminate the weight penalty traditionally associated with polycarbonate luggage. This matters increasingly as international airlines enforce 7-10 kg carry-on weight limits. [src7, src8]
- **Front-access laptop pockets becoming standard**: Multiple brands including Monos, Delsey, and Calpak now offer built-in front-access compartments for laptops and tablets, reducing the need to open the full suitcase at security checkpoints. [src3, src5]
- **Expansion capacity as a core feature**: Nearly every carry-on tested in 2026 includes an expansion zipper adding 2-8 liters of bonus capacity, reflecting rising airline fees for checked bags that push travelers to maximize their carry-on. [src1, src2]
- **Sustainability materials on the rise**: Brands like Calpak, Samsonite, and July are increasingly using recycled polycarbonate, recycled polyester from plastic bottles, and vegan leather trims, though premium sustainability options still command a price premium. [src5, src7]
- **Ejectable USB-C battery as a safety requirement**: FAA and most international carriers now require lithium-ion batteries to be removable before gate-check. The new generation (July CaseSafe, TUMI Alpha, Travelpro Platinum Elite) uses tool-free eject systems; older integrated-battery designs are being quietly phased out. [src1, src3, src9]
- **Self-aligning and noise-reducing wheels**: Travelpro's MagnaTrac magnet-aligned wheels, July's SilentMove Series 2, and Monos's "whisper-quiet" wheel technology represent a broader push toward smoother, quieter rolling performance. [src2, src6, src7]
