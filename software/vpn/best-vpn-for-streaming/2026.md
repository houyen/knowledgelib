# Best VPNs for Streaming (2026)

## What are the best VPNs for streaming in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: NordVPN (~$3.09/mo on 2-year)** — unblocks 15+ Netflix regions plus BBC iPlayer/Hulu/Disney+ at 1,256 Mbps with a native Apple TV app.
**Best value: Surfshark (~$1.78/mo on 2-year)** — fastest tested (1,615 Mbps), unlimited devices, 20+ Netflix libraries.
**Best for restricted regions: ExpressVPN (~$2.79/mo on 2-year)** — Lightway obfuscation works in China/UAE; MediaStreamer Smart DNS supports older Apple TVs and Samsung/LG smart TVs.
[src1, src2, src4, src9]

## Summary

The streaming-VPN landscape in 2026 is a cat-and-mouse game. Netflix, BBC iPlayer, and Disney+ invest heavily in proxy detection, rotating IP blocklists weekly — a provider that unblocks five regions today may be burned next week. Four providers have demonstrated consistent success across multi-month testing cycles: NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN, and Proton VPN. [src1, src2, src4, src9] Private Internet Access is now contested: Security.org unblocked all five of its Netflix test regions in May 2026, while CyberInsider's May 2026 round found PIA blocked on both Netflix and BBC iPlayer — treat it as a gamble for streaming rather than a safe default. [src4, src9] Atlas VPN shut down in April 2024 and migrated its users to NordVPN, reducing meaningful streaming-VPN competition. [src8]

Peak WireGuard speeds now routinely exceed 1,200 Mbps on flagship providers, making 4K streaming trivial on any modern connection. Surfshark led speed tests at 1,615 Mbps, with ExpressVPN (1,479 Mbps, Lightway) and Proton VPN (1,521 Mbps) close behind. [src1, src2] For pure Netflix catalog coverage, Windscribe unblocks 32 libraries — more than any other tested provider — while NordVPN reliably unblocks 15+ regions and Surfshark now clears 20–30 depending on the test methodology. [src2, src4] Proton VPN has also grown into a genuine catalog contender: its Plus-tier streaming servers now cover 10+ dedicated Netflix libraries and 15+ further regions, up from the 5-region footprint tested earlier in 2026. [src9] For devices without native VPN support (Samsung, LG smart TVs, older Apple TVs), Smart DNS services like ExpressVPN's MediaStreamer bypass geo-restrictions without encryption and do not count against the device limit. [src7]

The recommended default is NordVPN (~$3.09/mo on 2-year, verified 2026-07-16) for consistent unblocking plus 10 devices and a native Apple TV app; Surfshark (~$1.78/mo) is the best value with unlimited simultaneous connections. Avoid Mullvad and CyberGhost for Netflix/iPlayer in 2026 — both are frequently blocked. [src4, src6]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: NordVPN (~$3.09/mo on 2-year)
Consensus #1 across Tom's Guide, PCWorld, CyberInsider, and Security.org (9.7/10). Unblocks 15+ Netflix regions (US, UK, Canada, Australia, France, Germany, Japan, India, Korea, Brazil, and more), plus BBC iPlayer, Hulu, Disney+, and Prime Video. 1,256 Mbps average on WireGuard (NordLynx), 10 simultaneous connections, native Apple TV and Fire TV apps, included Smart DNS (SmartPlay), and 400+ UK servers for iPlayer. Independently audited no-logs policy (six Deloitte audits). [src1, src2, src4, src9]

### Best Netflix Catalog Coverage: Windscribe (~$5.75/mo) or Surfshark (~$1.78/mo)
Windscribe unblocks 32 Netflix libraries — more than any other tested provider — but has slower peak speeds (~500 Mbps) and no Apple TV app. [src4] Surfshark is a better all-rounder at lower cost, unblocking 20–30 Netflix regions depending on the outlet's methodology (16+ core regions confirmed across every test, including Turkey, Singapore, and Taiwan). [src2, src9]

### Best for BBC iPlayer Abroad: NordVPN or ExpressVPN
Both consistently unblock iPlayer via UK servers. ExpressVPN's Lightway protocol delivers 1,374–1,479 Mbps on UK–EU routes, eliminating buffering on 1080p streams. [src1] Mullvad does not work with iPlayer as of March 2026 tests and should be avoided if iPlayer is the priority. [src6]

### Best for 4K Streaming Speed: Surfshark (~$1.78/mo)
1,615 Mbps peak in WireGuard tests — the fastest in the category — plus unlimited simultaneous connections let you stream on every device in the household at once. QR-code login simplifies setup on streaming sticks. [src1, src2]

### Best for Fire TV / Apple TV Native App: NordVPN or ExpressVPN
Both offer native tvOS and Fire OS apps (ExpressVPN first to ship a tvOS 17+ app). For older Apple TVs or Samsung/LG smart TVs that cannot run a VPN app, ExpressVPN's MediaStreamer Smart DNS works without eating into the device limit. [src1, src7]

### Best Budget: Surfshark (~$1.78/mo on 2-year)
Lowest effective price among providers with reliable Netflix unblocking. Unlimited device connections replace the per-seat math entirely. Private Internet Access is nominally cheaper on its 3-year plan (~$1.98/mo) but its streaming record is now contested — CyberInsider found it blocked on Netflix and BBC iPlayer in May 2026, and it struggles with Hulu. [src2, src4, src9]

### Best for Privacy-Maximum Streamers: Proton VPN (~$2.99/mo Plus tier)
Open-source apps, Swiss jurisdiction, and a no-logs policy that passed its fifth consecutive annual Securitum audit in June 2026 — the deepest audit cadence of any provider in this card. [src10] Streaming unblocking is only available on the Plus tier (not Free), where dedicated streaming servers now reach 10+ Netflix libraries plus 15+ further regions, making Proton a catalog contender rather than just a privacy pick. Encountered minor issues with 9Now in testing but solid across Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Hulu, and Disney+. [src1, src9]

### Best for Hotstar / India: CyberGhost or ExpressVPN
CyberGhost operates 37 virtual Indian servers (Mumbai) explicitly optimized for Disney+ Hotstar — the only major provider that publicly lists Hotstar-specific servers. ExpressVPN also reliably unblocks Hotstar from the US, UK, and Australia. [src5]

### Best for China / Restricted Regions: ExpressVPN or NordVPN
Both offer obfuscated ("stealth") protocols — ExpressVPN's Lightway and NordVPN's NordWhisper/obfuscated servers — that disguise VPN traffic as regular HTTPS. Mullvad, Proton, and most other providers are blocked at the Great Firewall. Verify within the 30-day refund window. [src1, src4]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### NordVPN vs Surfshark
Both unblock the major libraries (Netflix US/UK/JP, BBC iPlayer, Hulu, Disney+) with near-100% success rates in 2026 testing. Surfshark wins on price (~$1.78/mo vs ~$3.09/mo), peak speed (1,615 Mbps vs 1,256 Mbps), and unlimited device connections. NordVPN wins on reliability of automatic streaming detection (SmartPlay) — Surfshark sometimes requires manual server switching when a specific service is blocked. [src1, src2, src4]

**Pick NordVPN if:** you want hands-off streaming with native Apple TV app and the best automatic unblocking, and the higher price is acceptable.
**Pick Surfshark if:** you want unlimited devices for the household and the lowest effective monthly cost.

### NordVPN vs ExpressVPN
Both are top-tier with native Apple TV/Fire TV apps and consistent unblocking. NordVPN has more Netflix regions (15+ vs 12+), while ExpressVPN is slightly cheaper (~$2.79/mo vs ~$3.09/mo). ExpressVPN edges ahead for restricted regions thanks to Lightway obfuscation that survives the Great Firewall and for Smart TV users via MediaStreamer Smart DNS, which does not consume a device slot. [src1, src2, src7]

**Pick NordVPN if:** you stream from a stable region and want the widest Netflix library access on the largest device limit (10).
**Pick ExpressVPN if:** you travel to China/UAE/Iran or stream on a Samsung/LG TV or pre-tvOS-17 Apple TV.

### Surfshark vs ExpressVPN
Surfshark is well under half the price (~$1.78 vs ~$2.79/mo) with faster peaks and unlimited connections. ExpressVPN is more beginner-friendly with simpler apps and ships obfuscation by default — useful in restricted countries. ExpressVPN's MediaStreamer Smart DNS gives it the edge for older streaming hardware. [src1, src2, src7]

**Pick Surfshark if:** budget and speed are top priorities and you stream from non-restricted regions.
**Pick ExpressVPN if:** you need beginner-friendly apps, obfuscated protocols in restricted regions, or Smart DNS for legacy TVs.

### NordVPN vs Proton VPN
Both deliver consistent unblocking across Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Hulu, and Disney+ at 1,200+ Mbps peak speeds. Proton VPN's Plus tier costs slightly less (~$2.99/mo vs ~$3.09/mo on 2-year), runs from Swiss jurisdiction, ships open-source apps, and passed its fifth consecutive annual Securitum no-logs audit in June 2026 (against NordVPN's six Deloitte audits). [src10] The Netflix gap has narrowed sharply: Proton's Plus streaming servers now reach 10+ dedicated libraries plus 15+ further regions, versus NordVPN's 15+. NordVPN still wins on Smart DNS (SmartPlay) and a more polished Apple TV app. [src1, src4, src9]

**Pick NordVPN if:** you want the widest confirmed Netflix coverage and Smart DNS on devices that cannot run a VPN app.
**Pick Proton VPN if:** Swiss jurisdiction, open-source apps, and the deepest audit cadence are non-negotiable alongside streaming.

### Surfshark vs Private Internet Access
Both offer unlimited simultaneous connections at the cheapest price tier (~$1.78 on 2-year vs ~$1.98 on 3-year). Surfshark wins decisively on streaming: 20–30 Netflix regions against a PIA record that testers no longer agree on (Security.org unblocked all five test regions in May 2026; CyberInsider found PIA blocked on Netflix and BBC iPlayer the same month), plus 1,615 Mbps vs 621 Mbps peak and full Hulu unblocking vs partial. PIA's main appeal is open-source apps and a longer track record on privacy. [src1, src2, src4, src9]

**Pick Surfshark if:** streaming is the primary use case — Netflix region coverage, speed, and test consistency are not close.
**Pick Private Internet Access if:** you prioritize open-source code and privacy track record over streaming reliability, and can test inside the 30-day refund window.

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Proprietary streaming protocols dominate**: WireGuard forks (NordLynx) and custom protocols (ExpressVPN's Lightway, Proton's Stealth) deliver 1,200+ Mbps while handling Netflix's deep-packet inspection better than vanilla WireGuard/OpenVPN. [src1]
- **Streaming-optimized server clusters**: CyberGhost, Surfshark, and NordVPN now group servers by streaming service (Netflix US, iPlayer UK, Hotstar IN) rather than just by geography. This shifts the load when a subset of IPs is banned. [src2, src5]
- **Smart DNS resurgence**: MediaStreamer (ExpressVPN), SmartPlay (NordVPN), and Surfshark's Smart DNS enable streaming on devices that cannot run VPN apps (Samsung, LG, older Apple TVs, some consoles). Faster than VPN because there is no encryption, but no privacy benefit. [src7]
- **Market consolidation**: Atlas VPN shut down April 2024; Nord Security now owns NordVPN, Surfshark, and Atlas. Kape owns ExpressVPN, CyberGhost, PIA, and ZenMate. Two companies control most of the top-tier streaming-VPN market. [src8]
- **Obfuscation is table stakes for restricted regions**: China, UAE, Iran, and Russia have escalated VPN blocking. Only providers with active obfuscation (ExpressVPN Lightway, NordVPN NordWhisper, Proton Stealth, Astrill) reliably connect. [src1]
- **Netflix blocks rotate weekly**: The 2024–2026 trend is automated residential-IP detection that burns proxy IPs within days of discovery. Even top providers now see 1–2 day "dark windows" after a block wave, after which IPs are cycled. [src4]
- **Tester verdicts are diverging on the second tier**: The top three (NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN) pass every outlet's tests, but mid-tier providers now get opposite verdicts from equally rigorous testers in the same month — Security.org unblocked all five Netflix test regions on PIA in May 2026 while CyberInsider recorded PIA as blocked on Netflix and BBC iPlayer. Treat any single "it works" claim about a non-top-three provider as a point-in-time sample, not a property. [src4, src9]
- **Audit cadence as a purchase signal**: Annual re-audits are replacing one-off audits as the privacy differentiator. Proton VPN passed its fifth consecutive yearly Securitum no-logs audit in June 2026 and denied all 47 legally binding data requests it received in H1 2026; NordVPN is at six Deloitte ISAE 3000 audits. Streaming-first buyers increasingly get audited privacy as a side effect. [src10]

## Important Caveats

- Streaming service unblocking changes week-to-week; any data in this card may be stale within 30 days. Unblocking columns reflect published test rounds as of 2026-07-16. Always verify within a provider's money-back window (typically 30 days).
- Private Internet Access is listed as "Contested" for Netflix because two high-reliability testers disagreed in the same month (Security.org: all five test regions unblocked; CyberInsider: blocked on Netflix and BBC iPlayer). Do not treat PIA as a safe streaming pick without testing it yourself inside the refund window. [src4, src9]
- Prices are promotional USD rates on 2-year (or noted longer) prepaid plans and differ by outlet and season — NordVPN was quoted between $3.09 and $3.49/mo and Surfshark between $1.78 and $2.19/mo across testers in mid-2026. Provider checkout pages are IP-geolocated and will show your local currency and a different headline number; confirm the price at signup.
- Netflix's proxy detection targets datacenter IP ranges; residential-IP providers (some Smartproxy-style services) are more durable but more expensive and not the focus of this card.
- Using a VPN to access content outside your region may violate the streaming service's Terms of Use. It is rarely illegal, but accounts can be restricted or banned. This is a legal gray zone that users accept at their own risk.
- Price figures are 2-year introductory rates. Renewal prices often jump 2–3x. Budget for either switching providers or accepting the higher renewal at year three.
- Free VPNs (including free tiers of Proton, Windscribe, and TunnelBear) rarely unblock streaming services reliably; the paid tiers are a different product.
- In China, UAE, Iran, Russia, and a growing list of jurisdictions, unauthorized VPN use can carry legal penalties. Verify local law before subscribing.
- Native Apple TV apps require tvOS 17 or newer. Older Apple TV 4K models may need MediaStreamer Smart DNS instead.
- Peak-speed figures are lab benchmarks on gigabit connections; real-world throughput depends on your ISP, server load, and distance. Any of the top five providers comfortably exceed the ~25 Mbps needed for 4K streaming.
