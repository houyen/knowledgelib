# Best Smart Fitness Mirrors (2026)

## What are the best smart fitness mirrors in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Tempo Studio (~$2,495–$3,995; $39/mo) — best AI form correction; 3D motion capture coaches your reps and posture in real time.**
**Best value: Echelon Reflect (~$1,999.99 direct; $39.99/mo) — cheapest real touchscreen mirror with a deep live-class library.**
**Best subscription-free: AEKE K1 (~$3,398) — ~220 lb of digital resistance and AI form coaching with no monthly fee.**
The category has consolidated hard: lululemon's Mirror, Fiture, NordicTrack's Vault and now the ProForm Vue are all discontinued — check availability before you buy. [<a href="https://www.reviewed.com/health/best-right-now/best-workout-mirrors">src1</a>, <a href="https://www.garagegymreviews.com/proform-vue-mirror-review">src2</a>, <a href="https://echelonfit.com/products/echelon-reflect-50-se-fitness-mirror">src9</a>, <a href="https://shop.tempo.fit/">src10</a>]

## Summary

The smart fitness mirror — a wall-mountable or freestanding panel that looks like a normal full-length mirror until its hidden screen turns on and streams workout classes — peaked around 2021–2022 and has contracted sharply since. lululemon, which bought Mirror for ~$500M in 2020, took ~$443M in impairment charges and discontinued Mirror/Studio Mirror hardware sales in fall 2023, migrating remaining owners to Peloton-branded content; lululemon Studio's own site now states plainly that "the lululemon Studio Mirror Hardware is no longer available for purchase." Fiture shut down US operations in 2023, the iFIT-powered NordicTrack Vault has been discontinued, and as of July 2026 its sibling **ProForm Vue is discontinued too** — ProForm's product page 404s and the Amazon listing reads "Currently unavailable," leaving it resale-only. The practical takeaway: this is a "verify before you buy" market — several heavily-reviewed names are zombie products. [src1, src2, src3, src4, src5, src8, src11]

Among devices you can actually buy with active support in 2026, **Tempo Studio** is Reviewed's top connected pick because its 3D Motion Capture camera and AI provide real-time form feedback and rep counting that pure streaming mirrors (Echelon Reflect, ProForm Vue) lack; it ships loadable physical weights and now runs $2,495 (Starter) to $3,995 (Pro) on Tempo's own storefront, with an all-inclusive $39/mo membership. **Tonal 2** (~$4,295 hardware, $59.95/mo) and **FORME Studio Lift** (~$5,995, $49/mo) push into "digital weights" territory — wall-mounted arms with adjustable electromagnetic resistance up to ~200–250 lb — and are the answer for serious strength training, at a serious price. The **AEKE K1** is the standout no-subscription option and has become materially cheaper — ~$3,398 on Amazon at this verification, down from ~$4,039 in June — for a 43" 4K touchscreen with AI coaching and ~220 lb of dial-adjustable digital resistance that folds to roughly a doormat's footprint. For people who just want classes, **Echelon's Reflect line** remains the value play, though it has moved upmarket: the current-production Reflect 50" SE lists at $1,999.99 direct (down from $2,599.99), the older Reflect Touch 50" turns up around ~$1,640 at resellers and as a Renewed unit on Amazon, and Echelon's Premier membership has risen to $39.99/mo. [src1, src2, src6, src7, src9, src10]

Across the board, plan for: a recurring subscription (only AEKE K1 avoids it), a clear ~6–7 ft viewing distance plus a yoga-mat workout zone, and — for Tonal and FORME — professional or structural wall mounting. Build quality and software polish are the real differentiators now that the hardware concept is mature; the cheaper iFIT-based mirrors (Vue, Vault) consistently underwhelmed testers on both, and both are now discontinued. With the sub-$1,500 tier effectively gone, the realistic entry point into a new, supported smart mirror is now ~$2,000. [src1, src2, src3, src9]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall / Best AI Coaching: Tempo Studio (~$2,495–$3,995; $39/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/tempo-studio)
Reviewed's top connected workout-mirror pick. Tempo's 3D Motion Capture camera tracks your body, counts reps and gives form corrections during class — capability the streaming-only mirrors don't have. It ships with loadable physical weights (plates + handles), so it doubles as a real strength tool, and it freestands (no structural mount). Tempo sells it in three packages that differ by how much weight is bundled: Starter $2,495, Plus $3,245, Pro $3,995, on a single all-inclusive $39/mo membership. Trade-offs: testers note you must stand ~6 ft back to stay in frame, the visual-only form cues are easy to miss, and you have to load/unload the dumbbell handles to change weight. [src1, src10]

### Best Value: Echelon Reflect (~$1,999.99 direct; $39.99/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/echelon-reflect-touch)
The cheapest way into a genuine touchscreen fitness mirror with a large live-class library. It looks like The Mirror (slim, plain, wall-mountable), shows heart-rate zone and calories on screen, and supports up to five user profiles per subscription. Echelon's current-production model is the Reflect 50" SE at $1,999.99 direct (reduced from $2,599.99); the older Reflect Touch 50" this card links to now shows up around ~$1,640 at third-party fitness retailers and as a Renewed unit on Amazon, so confirm whether you are buying new or refurbished. Reviewed's main complaints: glitchy software, an unstable freestanding stand (fixed by wall-mounting), and classes that are less customizable than Tonal's or Tempo's — you can't track the exact weight you lifted, change the music, or skip movements. [src1, src9]

### Best for Serious Strength: Tonal 2 (~$4,295; $59.95/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/tonal-2)
Reviewed's "best upgrade." Tonal isn't a class-streaming mirror so much as a wall-mounted digital cable machine with a screen: electromagnetic resistance up to ~250 lb per arm, adjustable in 1 lb increments, with AI that auto-adjusts the load and tracks every rep. It needs a structural wall mount and professional install (typically $295–$495), and the 12-month subscription commitment plus $59.95/mo membership make it the most expensive option here over time — but nothing else in this list builds strength as effectively. [src1, src7]

### Best Premium / Best 1:1 Training: FORME Studio Lift (~$5,995; $49/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/forme-studio-lift)
The "Rolls-Royce" of the category: a 43" 4K touchscreen with multiple HD cameras for nuanced live 1:1 personal training, plus the Lift's wall-mounted arms delivering up to ~200 lb of auto-adjusting digital resistance. The base FORME Studio (~$2,495) is the same gorgeous mirror without the strength arms. Caveats: it's the priciest mirror to buy, the subscription tops out higher than Peloton/Hydrow once you add a dedicated trainer, and it requires wall mounting. [src1]

### Best Subscription-Free: AEKE K1 Smart Home Gym (~$3,398; list ~$3,498–$4,598) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/aeke-k1)
The rare smart mirror with no mandatory monthly fee, and it has got cheaper — ~$3,398 on Amazon at this verification, down from ~$4,039 in June, which now undercuts Tonal 2's hardware price outright. A 43" 4K touchscreen runs an AI personal trainer that does skeletal motion tracking and real-time form correction across 280+ movements, with ~220 lb of dial-adjustable digital resistance and bundled grips, bench and armband. It freestands (no structural mount, unlike Tonal) and folds to roughly a doormat's footprint. Downsides: the software still has early-product quirks, and stock is thin. [src6]

### Formerly Best Budget — now discontinued: ProForm Vue Mirror (was ~$999–$1,499; iFIT $39/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/proform-vue)
Until mid-2026 this was the cheapest "mirror plus equipment" bundle: a pivoting 24"×60" mirror with a 22" HD touchscreen streaming iFIT, and a loadable barbell plus dumbbells stored behind it. **ProForm has now discontinued it** — the proform.com product page 404s and the Amazon listing reads "Currently unavailable" — so it survives only as resale stock at QVC, Costco and eBay, without a first-party warranty path. Garage Gym Reviews' verdict was already "there's better for the price": subpar build quality and included weights, a glitchy display, and no rep/weight/form tracking. Not recommended in 2026; buy it only as cheap used stock with eyes open. [src2]

### Best for Streaming Classes on a Budget Mirror: Echelon Reflect 32" Connect ($1,799.99 list; currently unavailable on Amazon) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/echelon-reflect-40-connect)
A 32" Full HD 1080p non-touch mirror that streams Echelon's live and on-demand classes; you control it from your phone and supply your own dumbbells. No camera, no AI, no equipment — but it's a simple way to put a content mirror on the wall. Caveat as of this verification: the Amazon listing reads "Currently unavailable," and Echelon's own site now leads with the Reflect 50" SE instead, so treat the 32" Connect as end-of-life inventory rather than a current product. Good for yoga/cardio/bodyweight followers who already own weights — if you can still find one. [src1, src2, src9]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Tempo Studio vs Tonal 2
Different machines wearing the same "smart mirror" label. Tempo is a freestanding, camera-driven coach with physical loadable weights — best for guided strength + cardio classes with form feedback, from $2,495 (Starter). Tonal 2 is a wall-mounted digital cable machine: up to ~250 lb of adjustable electromagnetic resistance with AI auto-loading, the strongest strength tool here, but ~$4,295 + $59.95/mo + a structural install. [src1, src7, src10]

**Pick Tempo Studio if:** you want AI form coaching, classes and real strength work, can't (or won't) wall-mount, and want to keep total cost under ~$3K (stay on the Starter package).
**Pick Tonal 2 if:** progressive strength training is the whole point, you have a stud wall, and budget isn't the constraint.

### Tonal 2 vs FORME Studio Lift
Both are wall-mounted "digital weights" mirrors with AI. FORME Lift has the bigger, prettier display (43" 4K vs ~24" touchscreen) and multiple cameras for premium 1:1 trainer sessions, but costs ~$5,995. Tonal 2 is cheaper (~$4,295), has the deeper, more refined strength-programming ecosystem, and slightly higher peak resistance (~250 lb/arm vs ~200 lb). [src1, src7]

**Pick Tonal 2 if:** you want the most proven strength platform and the lower price of the two.
**Pick FORME Studio Lift if:** screen quality, camera-based 1:1 coaching, and a showpiece aesthetic matter as much as the lifting.

### AEKE K1 vs Tonal 2
The subscription question decides it, and AEKE's price cut sharpened the case. AEKE K1 (~$3,398, down from ~$4,039) gives you a 43" 4K screen, AI form correction and ~220 lb of digital resistance with **no monthly fee** and no structural mount — it freestands and folds. Tonal 2 now costs *more* up front (~$4,295) and adds $59.95/mo indefinitely plus a wall install; in exchange you get a more mature content library and ecosystem. Over three years the gap is roughly $2,100 in AEKE's favour. [src1, src6, src7]

**Pick AEKE K1 if:** you hate subscriptions, can't wall-mount, want a foldable footprint, or want the lower lifetime cost.
**Pick Tonal 2 if:** you value a polished, well-populated workout library and trust an established platform more than a newer brand's software.

### Echelon Reflect vs ProForm Vue
Formerly the two budget options, and neither does form tracking — but only one is still made. Echelon Reflect is a slimmer, wall-mountable touchscreen mirror with a larger screen and a bigger live-class catalog, now $1,999.99 direct as the Reflect 50" SE. ProForm Vue bundled a barbell and dumbbells behind a pivoting frame (~$999–$1,499) running iFIT, but ProForm discontinued it in 2026 and Amazon lists it "Currently unavailable." Reviewed and Garage Gym Reviews both rated the Echelon experience above the iFIT mirrors anyway. [src1, src2, src9]

**Pick Echelon Reflect if:** you want a current, supported product — it is now the only real choice of the two.
**Pick ProForm Vue if:** you find cheap used stock, already accept no warranty and no tracking, and need bundled weights above all else.

### Tempo Studio vs Echelon Reflect
Tempo (from $2,495) adds the things Echelon lacks: a 3D-motion-capture camera that coaches form and counts reps, and loadable physical weights in the box. Echelon Reflect ($1,999.99 direct) is ~$500 cheaper and has a comparable class library, but it's "just" a streaming mirror — no AI feedback, no equipment, plus a wobbly stand unless wall-mounted. The two have converged: Echelon's price rose while Tempo's entry package held, so the premium for AI coaching plus weights is now only ~$500. [src1, src9, src10]

**Pick Tempo Studio if:** form correction and an all-in-one strength setup justify the extra ~$500 — at this gap, most buyers should.
**Pick Echelon Reflect if:** you want the lowest-cost real touchscreen mirror, already own weights, and don't need AI coaching.

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **Consolidation is still running, not finished**: lululemon discontinued Mirror/Studio Mirror hardware sales (fall 2023, after a ~$443M impairment), Fiture shut its US operations, NordicTrack's Vault was discontinued — and in 2026 the ProForm Vue joined them, removing the last sub-$1,500 name. The category that had ~half a dozen big consumer names is down to roughly five viable products. Verify availability before recommending. [src1, src2, src4, src5, src8, src11]
- **The budget tier has disappeared**: with the Vue discontinued and the Reflect 32" Connect unavailable, Echelon's $1,999.99 Reflect 50" SE is now the cheapest new, supported smart mirror — roughly double the ~$999 entry price of 2025. Smart mirrors are consolidating into a premium-only category. [src2, src9]
- **"Smart mirror" now spans two product types**: thin streaming mirrors that just play classes (Echelon Reflect, ex-Mirror, ex-Vue) and "digital weights" machines that happen to have a mirror-style screen (Tonal 2, FORME Studio Lift, AEKE K1). The price gap between them has narrowed to ~$2,000 vs ~$3,400–$6,000 as the streaming tier moved upmarket and AEKE cut its price. [src1, src6, src7, src9]
- **AI form correction has become the key differentiator**: Tempo, FORME and AEKE use cameras for real-time rep counting and posture cues; the cheaper iFIT/Echelon mirrors still only stream video. Reviewers now treat camera-based feedback as the dividing line for "smart." [src1, src6]
- **Subscription fatigue is driving subscription-free entrants, and subscriptions keep rising**: AEKE markets "no monthly fee" as a headline feature against Tonal's $59.95/mo and FORME's $49/mo, while Echelon raised Premier from $34.99 to $39.99/mo. With AEKE now undercutting Tonal on hardware too, the no-subscription pitch has teeth. [src6, src7, src9]
- **iFIT-based mirrors underdelivered — and are now gone**: both the ProForm Vue and the NordicTrack Vault drew criticism for build quality and lack of tracking; Garage Gym Reviews' verdict on the Vue was "there's better for the price." Both are now discontinued, and iFIT has effectively exited the mirror form factor. [src2, src3]
- **Existing-owner support is fragile**: discontinued devices keep working only as long as the content deal holds — Mirror owners were shifted to Peloton-branded classes, Fiture made content free-but-frozen. A discontinued mirror is a bet on someone else's content roadmap. [src4, src5, src8]

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of July 2026 and move a lot: Echelon runs frequent promos, AEKE and Tonal run periodic ones. As of this verification the AEKE K1 was ~$3,398 on Amazon (down from ~$4,039 in June, with only ~12 units in stock), the Echelon Reflect 50" SE was $1,999.99 direct (reduced from $2,599.99), Tempo Studio ran $2,495–$3,995 across its three packages, and both the ProForm Vue and Echelon Reflect 32" Connect read "Currently unavailable" on Amazon. Subscription costs are separate from hardware and recur indefinitely — Echelon Premier rose to $39.99/mo this cycle.
- Availability is the biggest risk in this category. The lululemon Studio Mirror, Fiture mirror, NordicTrack Vault and (as of 2026) the ProForm Vue are discontinued — they appear in older "best of" lists but cannot be bought new with active warranty/support. Treat any listing for them with caution: our own Amazon links for the Vault and Vue exist for reference and price history, not as a recommendation to buy.
- "Strength training support" means very different things per model: built-in adjustable digital resistance (Tonal 2, FORME Studio Lift, AEKE K1), loadable physical weights bundled in (Tempo Studio, ex-Vue, ex-Vault), or none at all — bring your own dumbbells (Echelon Reflect, base FORME Studio, ex-Mirror).
- Tempo, Tonal and FORME are direct-to-consumer brands with no Amazon listings, so their "Check price" links go to the manufacturer's own product page rather than a retailer. The lululemon Studio Mirror has no live listing or product page at all — its link is a reference search only.
- Install requirements differ: Tonal 2 and FORME need professional or structural wall mounting (extra cost); the others freestand. Confirm wall type and ceiling height before buying a wall-mounted model.
- Camera-equipped mirrors (Tempo, FORME, AEKE, ex-Mirror) raise privacy considerations — verify physical camera covers and data policies if that matters to the user.
- This unit covers the *interactive/screened* mirror category. A plain wall mirror for checking lifting form is far cheaper and needs no subscription — see the related "Home Gym Mirrors (non-smart)" unit.
