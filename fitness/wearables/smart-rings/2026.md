## What are the best smart rings in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Oura Ring 5 (~$399 + $5.99/mo) — new flagship, 40% smaller, adds Blood Pressure Signals.**
**Best value: RingConn Gen 2 (~$299, no subscription) — 12-day battery, sleep apnea detection.**
**Best budget: Amazfit Helio Ring (~$150, no subscription) — solid sleep tracking, no recurring fees.**

## Summary

The 2026 smart ring market is shaped by three events: Oura's ITC patent victory removed Ultrahuman from the US through Q1 2026 (Ring PRO cleared Customs but ships only via Ultrahuman's store), RingConn Gen 2 became the first ring with FDA-cleared sleep apnea detection, and the **Oura Ring 5** landed on June 4, 2026 as the new flagship. The Ring 5 (~$399 + $5.99/mo) is 40% smaller than the Ring 4, runs 6-9 days, and adds Blood Pressure Signals and Nighttime Breathing. The **Oura Ring 4** (~$349 + $5.99/mo) stays on sale and remains the accuracy-validated pick — a Wright-Patterson Air Force Base study across 500+ nights placed it first on every cardiovascular metric with 94% sleep detection accuracy, and its HRV correlates with ECG at r²=0.980. **RingConn Gen 2** (~$299, no subscription) ships a 12-day battery, sleep apnea monitoring, and HSA/FSA approval. **Samsung Galaxy Ring** (~$399) is the only ring with deep Galaxy ecosystem integration and a charging case that displays battery on the case itself. [src2, src3, src4, src5, src9]

The subscription-free tier is now the dominant value proposition: RingConn, Samsung, Ultrahuman, Amazfit, Evie, and Circular all offer the full feature set with one upfront payment. Oura's $5.99/month adds ~$216 over 3 years on top of the ring; without it, the Oura app is reduced to three daily scores. The **RingConn Gen 3** (~$349, no subscription) is now stocked on Amazon with up to 14-day battery, vibration alerts, and Vascular Trends. The Evie Ring (~$269) is the only ring purpose-built around women's cycle tracking, and the Circular Ring 2 (~$349) is the only ring with FDA-cleared on-finger ECG. [src1, src2, src6, src7, src10]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Oura Ring 5 (~$399 + $5.99/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/oura-ring-5)
The newest Oura flagship, announced May 28 and shipping since June 4, 2026. Oura calls it the world's smallest smart ring: ~40% smaller than the Ring 4, roughly 2mm narrower and ~30% thinner, with battery up to 6-9 days (vs the Ring 4's 5-8). Adds Blood Pressure Signals, Nighttime Breathing, live activity tracking with real-time metrics, and GLP-1 medication insights. $399 in Silver/Black, $499 for Gold, Stealth, Brushed Silver and Deep Rose; the optional charging case is $99. Still requires the $5.99/month membership. [src9]

### Best Validated Accuracy Per Dollar: Oura Ring 4 (~$349 + $5.99/mo) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/oura-ring-4)
Most-validated smart ring for sleep and recovery, and still on sale $50 below the Ring 5. A 2025 Wright-Patterson Air Force Base study across 500+ nights placed Oura first on every cardiovascular metric with 94% sleep detection accuracy. HRV correlates with gold-standard ECG at r²=0.980 (Nature). Fully titanium build, 8-day battery (4-5 days for sizes 4-7 due to physically smaller battery), 100m water resistance. Subscription unlocks 50+ metrics, AI Advisor, cycle tracking. The Ring 5's new sensors are not yet independently validated, so the Ring 4 remains the evidence-backed choice. [src3, src4, src5, src9]

### Best Value (No Subscription): RingConn Gen 2 (~$299) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/ringconn-gen-2)
Wareable, TechRadar, and multiple reviewers call it the best Oura alternative. World's first smart ring with FDA-cleared sleep apnea monitoring, 10-12 day battery (longest non-Pro), 2mm thick titanium, IP68 + 100m water resistance, HSA/FSA approved. Tracks activity, sleep, stress, HR, HRV, SpO2 with no recurring fees. [src1, src2]

### Best for Samsung Users: Samsung Galaxy Ring (~$399) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-ring-smart-rings)
Deep Samsung Health integration with the Energy Score combining activity + sleeping HR + sleep duration. Charging case displays battery level without app. 10 ATM water resistance, 7-day battery (degrades to ~5 days after 6 months per long-term reviews). Currently back at its $399.99 MSRP on Amazon after a long spell at $299 — wait for a sale if you can, as it has hit $299 repeatedly and $108 in deep open-box deals. No subscription. [src1, src3]

### Longest Battery + Vibration Alerts: RingConn Gen 3 (~$349) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/ringconn-gen-3)
The CES 2026 flagship, now stocked on Amazon. Up to 14 days of battery with vibration off, 10-13 days with it on — the longest in the category. Adds smart vibration alerts (used for health alerts and reminders, not general phone notifications) and Vascular Trends, which reports long-term cardiovascular patterns rather than single blood-pressure readings. 10 sizes, no subscription. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Oura Ring 5 vs Oura Ring 4
The Ring 5 (~$399) is ~40% smaller than the Ring 4 (~$349), runs 6-9 days vs 5-8, and adds Blood Pressure Signals, Nighttime Breathing, live activity metrics and GLP-1 insights. The Ring 4 keeps the independent validation record (Wright-Patterson, Nature) that the Ring 5's new sensors do not yet have, and costs $50 less. Both need the same $5.99/month membership, so the only real question is whether the smaller body and new sensors are worth the premium. [src4, src5, src9]

**Pick Oura Ring 5 if:** you want the smallest ring, the longest Oura battery, and the newest sensors.
**Pick Oura Ring 4 if:** you want the peer-validated Oura at $50 less and don't need blood-pressure signals.

### Oura Ring 5 vs Samsung Galaxy Ring
Now the same price (~$399 each). Oura wins on cross-platform accuracy, sensor breadth and a mature app; Samsung wins on zero subscription and Galaxy ecosystem depth (Energy Score, charging-case battery display). Over 3 years the Oura membership adds ~$216 the Galaxy Ring never charges — but the Galaxy Ring is a first-gen product whose battery degrades noticeably. [src1, src3, src9]

**Pick Oura Ring 5 if:** you want the best data and accept $5.99/month.
**Pick Samsung Galaxy Ring if:** you own a Samsung phone and want zero recurring fees.

### Oura Ring 4 vs RingConn Gen 2
RingConn delivers ~80-90% of Oura's tracking with no subscription, longer battery (10-12 days vs 8), and FDA-cleared sleep apnea detection that Oura lacks. Oura still wins on accuracy validation, AI Advisor coaching, and cycle prediction depth. Over 3 years RingConn saves ~$216 in subscription fees on top of its $50 lower sticker. [src1, src2]

**Pick Oura Ring 4 if:** you want the most-validated sleep/HRV scores and AI coaching.
**Pick RingConn Gen 2 if:** you want sleep apnea detection, longer battery, and no subscription.

### RingConn Gen 3 vs RingConn Gen 2
The Gen 3 (~$349) adds vibration alerts, Vascular Trends and up to 14-day battery; the Gen 2 (~$299) is $50 cheaper, lighter, and keeps the FDA-cleared sleep apnea monitoring that remains RingConn's headline medical feature. Gen 3's vascular reporting is trend-based, not a blood-pressure reading, so it is a wellness signal rather than a diagnostic. [src2, src3, src10]

**Pick RingConn Gen 3 if:** you want the longest battery in the category plus on-finger alerts.
**Pick RingConn Gen 2 if:** you want sleep apnea screening at the lowest price.

### Samsung Galaxy Ring vs RingConn Gen 2
Both are subscription-free. Galaxy Ring wins on iOS-Samsung hybrid users (case display, ecosystem) and brand support; RingConn wins on battery (12 vs 7 days, both degrade), thinness (2mm), and sleep apnea detection. RingConn is now $100 cheaper ($299 vs $399). [src1, src2, src3]

**Pick Samsung Galaxy Ring if:** you live in the Samsung ecosystem.
**Pick RingConn Gen 2 if:** you're cross-platform and want longest battery + sleep apnea.

### Oura Ring 4 vs Ultrahuman Ring AIR
Pre-ban, Ultrahuman was the strongest no-subscription Oura alternative — circadian/metabolic engine instead of recovery scores, lightest titanium, 100m water resistance. Post-ban (US-only), the Ring AIR is "Currently unavailable" on Amazon and sells only through Ultrahuman's own store (~$349); the cleared **Ultrahuman Ring PRO** ($479, no subscription) started shipping June 20, 2026 through that same store. Either way, Ultrahuman now means buying direct rather than on Amazon. EU/UK/global buyers can still pick Ring AIR for its circadian focus. [src2, src6]

**Pick Oura Ring 4 if:** you're in the US, want validated sleep accuracy, and accept the subscription.
**Pick Ultrahuman (Ring PRO in US / Ring AIR elsewhere) if:** you prioritize circadian/metabolic guidance with no subscription.

### Evie Ring vs Oura Ring 4 (women's health)
Evie's open-design adapts to finger swelling (better for pregnancy / menstrual cycles), no subscription, $80 cheaper. Oura's cycle-prediction algorithms and historical data depth are more sophisticated, and its temperature accuracy is better-validated. Evie is the data-collection choice; Oura is the insight choice. [src5, src7]

**Pick Evie Ring if:** you want a women-first design with no recurring cost.
**Pick Oura Ring 4 if:** you want best-validated cycle and temperature insights.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate street prices as of July 2026. Smart rings drop heavily during holiday and Prime Day events; Samsung Galaxy Ring is back at its $399.99 MSRP after months at $299 and has hit $108 in open-box deals, and Amazfit Helio Ring sells at ~$150 vs $299 launch. Expect the Oura Ring 4 to discount further now that the Ring 5 has replaced it at the top of the line.
- Sizing is permanent — every brand requires a free sizing kit (3-7 day wait) before shipping the ring. Finger size varies 5-10% with temperature, hydration, and activity; a poor fit can cut sensor contact and reading accuracy by 15-30%.
- Battery life claims are best-case (typically with continuous SpO2 disabled). Real-world battery is 60-80% of marketing figures, and degrades 30-50% after 12-18 months of daily charging — Galaxy Ring users report dropping from 7 days to 2-5 days after 6-12 months.
- Ultrahuman Ring AIR is **unavailable on Amazon** as of 2026 due to the ITC import ban and shows "Currently unavailable" there; its buy link goes to Ultrahuman's official store instead. Existing owners get continued software support; US buyers can buy the cleared Ultrahuman Ring PRO ($479, shipping since June 20, 2026) — also direct from Ultrahuman, not Amazon — or pick an Amazon alternative.
- The Evie Ring and Circular Ring 2 are direct-to-consumer only — neither has an Amazon listing, so their buy links go to the brands' own stores. Evie was showing **out of stock** at Movano's store in July 2026; Movano has paused and relaunched the product before, so confirm availability before committing to it.
- The Oura Ring 5's new sensors (Blood Pressure Signals, Nighttime Breathing) are **not yet independently validated**. The accuracy record cited for Oura — the Wright-Patterson study and the Nature ECG correlation — was established on earlier generations, so treat the Ring 5's new metrics as promising rather than proven.
- Smart rings have no display — every alert, setting, or stat requires a paired phone. They are companion devices, not standalone wearables. Buyers expecting on-wrist notifications, GPS pace, or workout displays should buy a smartwatch.
- HRV, SpO2, and temperature accuracy depend on sensor contact and finger size. Lab-grade comparisons (Wright-Patterson, Nature) reflect ideal fit; real-world variance is higher.
