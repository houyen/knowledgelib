# Best Double Strollers (2026)

## What are the best double strollers in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: UPPAbaby Vista V3 (~$1,000)** — convertible single-to-double tandem with 25.7" width, 30+ configurations, and best all-around scores. **Best value: Mockingbird Single-to-Double 3.0 ($549)** — Vista-level versatility at half the cost. **Best budget side-by-side: UPPAbaby Minu Duo (~$750)** — Babylist's 2026 "Best Overall" pick, 27.8" wide. [src1, src2, src7]

## Summary

The double stroller market in 2026 saw three meaningful shake-ups. **Bugaboo launched the Donkey 6** — replacing the Donkey 5 — with a 50% larger storage basket, expanded breezy panels, and a still-narrow 29.1-inch double width. **UPPAbaby released the Minu Duo** (~$750), a dedicated 27.4-inch-wide side-by-side that Babylist now ranks "Best Overall" — closing the brand's gap against Bugaboo for families who want side-by-side without paying $1,700. And **Thule launched the Urban Glide 3 Double**, replacing the 2.0 as the leading premium jogger. [src7, src8]

For tandem buyers, the **UPPAbaby Vista V3** (~$1,000) remains the gold standard for convertible single-to-double use, with a 25.7-inch width that fits standard doorways and 30+ configurations including bassinet, infant car seat, and toddler seat combos. The **Mockingbird Single-to-Double 3.0** ($549) is the value champion — a 2026 refresh added a fold-out UPF 50+ LegShade and easier magnetic buckles, and the price still undercuts the Vista by roughly half. For travel, the **Silver Cross Jet Double** (~$700) at under 20 lbs with a suitcase-style fold remains uncontested for portability, with the lighter **Zoe Twin V2** (~$529, 23 lbs) as a budget alternative that lacks car-seat support. Joggers should look at the **Thule Urban Glide 3 Double** ($1,000 MSRP, sale price gone as of June 2026) or the durable, now-cheaper **BOB Revolution Flex 3.0 Duallie** (~$1000) — which is currently the jogger value pick. [src1, src2, src7, src8]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall Tandem: UPPAbaby Vista V3 (~$1,000) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/uppababy-vista-v3)
The Vista V3 remains the convertible stroller of choice for 2026, earning top marks across BabyGearLab, Mommyhood101, and Babylist. It features improved all-wheel suspension, a magnetic buckle system for easier fastening, and a massive 30-lb storage basket. At 25.7 inches wide, it fits through standard doorways with ease. Over 30 seating configurations accommodate bassinet, infant car seat, and toddler seat combos, and the rear seat sits raised so neither child feels "stacked." [src1, src2, src7]

### Best Overall Side-by-Side (NEW 2026): UPPAbaby Minu Duo (~$750) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/uppababy-minu-duo)
Babylist's new "Best Overall" double stroller pick for 2026. UPPAbaby's first dedicated side-by-side double pairs Minu compactness with full newborn compatibility (with bassinets/adapters), 50 lbs per seat, and a 27.8-inch width that still fits most 30-inch doorways. Independent recline on each seat and travel-system compatibility with the Aria and Mesa infant car seats. Bridges the gap between the $529 Zoe Twin V2 (no car seat support) and the $1,699 Bugaboo Donkey 6. [src7]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### UPPAbaby Vista V3 vs Bugaboo Donkey 6 Duo
Both are convertible single-to-double premium strollers, but they target different families. The Vista V3 (~$1,000) is a **tandem** (front-to-back) at 25.7" wide — fits every standard doorway. The Donkey 6 (~$1,699) is a **side-by-side** at 29.1" wide and offers organic-cotton bassinet liners, breezy panels, and a 50% larger basket. Pick the Donkey 6 only if you value side-by-side seating and luxury materials enough to pay $700 more. [src7, src8]

**Pick Vista V3 if:** You prefer tandem (siblings can see each other), need a guaranteed-fit 30" doorway clearance, or want maximum value for premium features.<br>**Pick Donkey 6 if:** You want side-by-side seating and don't mind the wider footprint, prioritize luxury materials, or specifically want the convertible single→side-by-side path.

### UPPAbaby Minu Duo vs Bugaboo Donkey 6 Duo
UPPAbaby's 2026 Minu Duo (~$750) directly attacks the Donkey 6 from below. Both are side-by-side. The Minu Duo is narrower (27.8" vs 29.1"), lighter (27.4 lb vs 26.9–32.8 lb), and less than half the price. The Donkey 6 wins on luxury finish, larger basket, and convertible single-mode use. For most families, the Minu Duo is the rational pick. [src7]

**Pick Minu Duo if:** You want side-by-side under $1,000, don't need convertible single-mode, and value the Babylist "Best Overall" pedigree.<br>**Pick Donkey 6 if:** Budget is not a hard constraint and you want the most premium finish + convertible flexibility.

### Mockingbird Single-to-Double 3.0 vs UPPAbaby Vista V3
The value showdown of 2026. Both are tandems with similar widths (25.5" vs 25.7") and both convert from single. The Mockingbird ($549) hits 44 configurations with the full bundle and offers magnetic buckles + UPF 50+ LegShade. The Vista V3 ($1,000) wins on resale value, accessory ecosystem, and "premium feel." Reviewers consistently flag Mockingbird as the answer to "Vista features at half price." [src2, src6, src7]

**Pick Mockingbird if:** Budget is the deciding factor, you don't need third-party accessory compatibility, or you plan to keep this as your only stroller.<br>**Pick Vista V3 if:** Resale value matters, you want Mesa/Aria car-seat tight integration, or you value the premium fit-and-finish.

### Thule Urban Glide 3 Double vs BOB Revolution Flex 3.0 Duallie
The two serious jogging doubles. Thule's 3rd-generation Urban Glide (~$1,000, its earlier sale now gone) brings self-standing fold, twist hand brake, adjustable leg rests, and slightly lighter feel. The BOB Duallie (~$804) wins on mountain-bike-grade suspension and ride smoothness over rough surfaces. At June 2026 Amazon pricing the BOB undercuts the Thule by ~$200, so the BOB is now the value pick; pick the Thule only for its modern conveniences. [src2, src4, src7, src8]

**Pick Thule Urban Glide 3 if:** You want the modern features (twist brake, leg rests, self-standing fold) and run primarily on paved trails.<br>**Pick BOB Duallie if:** You jog on rough or off-road surfaces and want the best suspension money can buy.

### Silver Cross Jet Double vs Zoe Twin V2
Both are ultra-lightweight travel-friendly doubles. The Jet Double (~$700) at under 20 lbs has a suitcase-style fold and accepts infant car seats. The Zoe Twin V2 (~$529, DTC) is 23 lb but does NOT accept car seats — toddler-only. For newborns, the Jet Double wins by default. For older toddlers + airline travel on a budget, the Zoe still holds. [src2, src5, src7]

**Pick Jet Double if:** You travel with an infant or want maximum portability with car-seat compatibility.<br>**Pick Zoe Twin V2 if:** Both kids are past the infant stage, budget matters, and you don't need Amazon Prime delivery.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are Amazon prices as of June 2026. Sale events, bundles, and regional pricing may differ; the Donkey 6 in particular shows wide retailer-to-retailer variation. The Thule Urban Glide 3 Double is now at full $1,000 MSRP (its earlier sale has ended), while the Baby Jogger City Mini GT2 has dropped back to ~$548. The UPPAbaby Minu Duo is intermittently out of stock on Amazon (the ~$750 figure is its standard price; check the official UPPAbaby store if Amazon shows it unavailable).
- Car seat adapters are typically sold separately ($30-$80) and are brand-specific. The UPPAbaby Minu Duo car-seat adapter is a separate $90+ purchase. Verify compatibility before purchasing.
- Side-by-side strollers wider than 30 inches may not fit through standard doorways (30 inches) or narrow store aisles. The Bombi Bēbee (35.75 in) and the BOB/Thule joggers (31 in+) are the most likely to be excluded.
- Several travel-friendly side-by-sides (Zoe Twin V2, some Bombi configurations) do not accept infant car seats. They are not appropriate for newborns until the child can sit up unsupported (~6 months).
- Weight listed is stroller only — add 5-15 lbs for bassinets, second seats, and accessories. The Bugaboo Donkey 6 in full duo-bassinet configuration approaches 40 lbs.
