# Best Baby Cribs (2026)

## What are the best baby cribs in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Babyletto Yuzu 8-in-1 (~$594)** — only 8-in-1 convertible, GREENGUARD Gold, all conversion kits included. **Best value: DaVinci Kalani 4-in-1 (~$249)** — Wirecutter's best-for-most pick, GREENGUARD Gold, 4-in-1 NZ pine. **Best budget: Delta Children Frankie 4-in-1 (~$189)** — cheapest GREENGUARD Gold + JPMA 4-in-1 with daybed rail included (Walmart-exclusive). [src1, src7, src8]

## Summary

The 2026 crib market split clearly into three tiers: ultra-versatile premium convertibles led by the new **Babyletto Yuzu 8-in-1** (~$594, GREENGUARD Gold, includes all conversion kits + two mattresses), workhorse mid-tier 3-in-1 and 4-in-1 cribs from Babyletto, DaVinci, Storkcraft and Delta in the $189-499 range, and budget picks from Graco, Dream On Me, and IKEA at $99-200. The **DaVinci Kalani 4-in-1** (~$249) remains Wirecutter's best-for-most-families pick, while the **Babyletto Hudson 3-in-1** — now discounted to ~$315 from its ~$499 list — holds 9.9/10 across 2,000+ reviews and is a near-universal premium pick across Babylist, Babygearlab, and What to Expect. [src1, src7, src8]

For 2026 specifically: the **Babyletto Yuzu 8-in-1** is the year's headline launch, transitioning between bassinet, midi crib, full-size crib, toddler bed, daybed, and junior bed (all kits included) — addressing the long-standing complaint that conversion kits are sold separately; it has since settled to ~$594 from its ~$699 launch price. The **Delta Children Frankie 4-in-1** (~$189) brings GREENGUARD Gold to the budget tier with mid-century styling and includes the daybed/sofa rail in the box — it is Walmart-exclusive (no Amazon listing). The **Storkcraft Sicily 3-in-1** repositioned in 2026 from a sub-$300 budget pick to a premium-tier piece at ~$477 (now competes with the Hudson). The **Delta Children Canton 4-in-1** has been discontinued across Amazon and on Delta Children's own site (limited remaining stock only). Smart cribs (Cradlewise) remain a premium niche with auto-soothing and integrated monitoring. All picks meet current CPSC/ASTM standards. [src1, src2, src7]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Babyletto Hudson 3-in-1 vs DaVinci Kalani 4-in-1
Both are GREENGUARD Gold, both use New Zealand pine. Hudson costs ~2x as much for one less conversion stage but scores higher on design (9.9/10) and build quality. Kalani gives you the full-size bed conversion (rails sold separately) for half the price. [src1, src7, src8]
**Pick the Hudson if:** design and premium feel matter more than convertibility — you want a 3-in-1 that becomes a daybed and looks like furniture, not a temporary fixture.
**Pick the Kalani if:** you want the longest useful life at the lowest GREENGUARD Gold price — 4-in-1 with full-size bed conversion, 10+ colors, Wirecutter's best-for-most pick.

### Babyletto Yuzu 8-in-1 vs Babyletto Hudson 3-in-1
Same brand, same GREENGUARD Gold certification, very different value math. Hudson is the design-led 3-in-1, currently ~$315 (down from ~$499 list). Yuzu is ~$594 but transitions across three physical sizes (bassinet → midi → full) and includes every conversion kit + two mattresses. [src1, src7]
**Pick the Yuzu if:** you don't already own a bassinet and you want one crib from newborn through age 5 — total spend ends up cheaper than buying a bassinet + Hudson + separate conversion kits.
**Pick the Hudson if:** you already have a bassinet, prefer a furniture-grade aesthetic, and a 3-in-1 (crib → toddler → daybed) is enough.

### Delta Children Frankie 4-in-1 vs DaVinci Kalani 4-in-1
Both are sub-$300 GREENGUARD Gold 4-in-1s with the daybed rail included. Frankie is ~$60-100 cheaper and adds JPMA certification. Kalani has the stronger track record (Wirecutter pick, 1000s of reviews) and a wider color range. [src1, src8]
**Pick the Frankie if:** you're maximizing certifications-per-dollar — GREENGUARD Gold + JPMA at the lowest price, mid-century styling.
**Pick the Kalani if:** you want the most-vetted option in this price tier — Wirecutter's best-for-most pick with the broadest finish selection.

### Babyletto Yuzu 8-in-1 vs Cradlewise Smart Crib
Both replace a bassinet AND a crib. Yuzu does it mechanically (~$594, no electronics, lasts to age 5). Cradlewise does it with sensors + motion + sound (~$1,499-1,999, but only rated 0-24 months). [src1, src7]
**Pick the Yuzu if:** you want long-term use and don't need auto-soothing — Yuzu lasts 4x longer for half the price.
**Pick the Cradlewise if:** auto-soothing and integrated monitoring matter — you're paying for the tech, not the longevity.

### Storkcraft Sicily 3-in-1 vs Babyletto Hudson 3-in-1
Both premium GREENGUARD Gold 3-in-1s. Sicily sits at ~$477 after its 2026 price rise; the Hudson is currently discounted to ~$315 (from ~$499 list), so the Hudson is now the cheaper of the two. Hudson has a mid-century-modern open silhouette and ~10,000 reviews. Sicily has the arched-Italian aesthetic and multiple 2025 design awards. [src1, src7]
**Pick the Sicily if:** you want statement-piece arched design and award-winning aesthetics over best-seller pedigree, and price is secondary.
**Pick the Hudson if:** you want a proven pick with the broadest review base, dresser-matching Hudson collection, AND the lower current price.
