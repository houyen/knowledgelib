# Best Wi-Fi Routers Under $200 (2026)

## What are the best Wi-Fi routers under $200 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick under $200: TP-Link Archer BE550 (~$170)** -- tri-band Wi-Fi 7 with 6 GHz and all-2.5G ports, now the best in-budget choice after the BE9700 climbed over $200.<br>
**Best value: TP-Link Archer BE230 (~$87)** -- cheapest Wi-Fi 7 router on Amazon, dual 2.5G ports + EasyMesh.<br>
**Best budget: ASUS RT-AX1800S (~$58)** -- Wi-Fi 6 with free AiProtection for sub-300 Mbps plans in small homes. (Performance flagship: BE9700, but now ~$230, just over the cap.) [src1, src5, src8]

## Summary

The sub-$200 Wi-Fi router market settled into clear tiers by mid-2026. The TP-Link Archer BE9700 remains the performance leader -- 10 Gbps WAN, 320 MHz channels, tri-band Wi-Fi 7, 2,600 sq ft rated coverage, Tom's Hardware's top-ranked router across all price categories -- but its Amazon price has climbed to ~$230, putting it just over this guide's $200 cap. That promotes the TP-Link Archer BE550 (~$170) to the best pick that's genuinely under $200: it's the only other tri-band Wi-Fi 7 router with 6 GHz support in this budget, uniquely ships with five 2.5 Gbps Ethernet ports, and has dipped back below $170. [src1, src5]

Budget Wi-Fi 7 has held its ground. The TP-Link Archer BE230 sits at ~$87 on Amazon, keeping Wi-Fi 7 available under $90 with dual 2.5 Gbps ports, EasyMesh, and a 2.0 GHz quad-core processor. The ASUS RT-BE58U (~$145) remains the best security-focused pick with free lifetime AiProtection Pro and 1.75 Gbps peak wireless throughput. The Walmart-exclusive Archer BE3600 (~$99 at Walmart) is functionally equivalent to the BE230 but has no first-party Amazon listing -- Amazon buyers should default to the BE230 (links to TP-Link's official BE3600 page are provided for spec reference). [src1, src3, src8]

Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 6 routers are being squeezed by sub-$100 Wi-Fi 7. The TP-Link Archer AXE75 sits at ~$106 on Amazon but is still undercut by the BE230 at ~$87 with newer Wi-Fi 7. The ASUS RT-AX1800S has dropped to ~$58 -- the cheapest worthwhile Wi-Fi 6 option for internet plans under 300 Mbps on the tightest budgets. The Netgear Nighthawk Wi-Fi 7 RS200 sits right at ~$200 -- at the edge of the cap and a viable dual-band Wi-Fi 7 alternative to the BE230 for buyers who want Netgear's firmware. [src4, src7]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### TP-Link Archer BE9700 vs Archer BE550
Both are tri-band Wi-Fi 7 with 6 GHz. The BE9700 (~$230) adds a 10 Gbps WAN port, 320 MHz channels, six external antennas, and 600 sq ft more rated coverage; the BE550 (~$170) ships with five 2.5 Gbps ports on a smaller chassis. Performance gap is real but narrow: at close range Tom's Hardware shows the BE9700 ~10-15% ahead on 5 GHz throughput. The BE9700 now sits ~$60 above the BE550 and just over the $200 cap. [src1, src5]

**Pick BE9700 if:** internet plan is 2.5 Gbps+, home is over 2,000 sq ft, you want the highest single-router benchmark numbers, and you can stretch just past $200.<br>
**Pick BE550 if:** you want to stay under $200, internet plan is ≤2.5 Gbps, and you want every Ethernet port at 2.5 Gbps for a wired smart-home/NAS setup.

### TP-Link Archer BE230 vs ASUS RT-BE58U
Both are dual-band Wi-Fi 7 (BE3600) with 2.0 GHz quad-core processors. The BE230 (~$87) has dual 2.5 Gbps ports, EasyMesh, USB 3.0, and rates 2,000 sq ft. The RT-BE58U (~$145) has one 2.5 Gbps port, AiMesh, free lifetime AiProtection Pro security, dual-WAN, and rates 2,200 sq ft. [src6, src8]

**Pick BE230 if:** budget is the priority -- you get the same Wi-Fi 7 hardware and an extra 2.5G port for ~$58 less.<br>
**Pick RT-BE58U if:** you want commercial-grade security included free, dual-WAN failover, or you're already on the ASUS AiMesh ecosystem.

### TP-Link Archer BE230 vs Archer AXE75
The BE230 (~$87, Wi-Fi 7) and AXE75 (~$106, Wi-Fi 6E with 6 GHz) are within $20 of each other. The AXE75 has the 6 GHz band; the BE230 has newer Wi-Fi 7 (MLO + 4K-QAM + 320 MHz) and dual 2.5 Gbps ports. [src4, src8]

**Pick BE230 if:** you don't already own Wi-Fi 6E devices and want the newest standard plus 2.5 Gbps wired backhaul.<br>
**Pick AXE75 if:** you specifically want the 6 GHz band for less-congested 2.5 Gbps wireless throughput and own Wi-Fi 6E/7 client devices.

### Netgear Nighthawk RS200 vs TP-Link Archer BE9700
The RS200 sits at ~$200 and the BE9700 at ~$230. The BE9700 is tri-band Wi-Fi 7 with 6 GHz, 10G WAN, and 2,600 sq ft coverage. The RS200 is dual-band Wi-Fi 7 (BE6500), 2.5G internet port, 2,500 sq ft, and runs Netgear's app + Armor security ecosystem. [src1, src7]

**Pick BE9700 if:** you want the 6 GHz band, faster WAN, higher benchmark numbers, and no paid security subscriptions.<br>
**Pick RS200 if:** you specifically prefer Netgear's app/firmware or already own Netgear gear and want one-vendor support.

### ASUS RT-BE58U vs ASUS RT-AX1800S
Same-brand jump: AiProtection security on both, AiMesh on both. The RT-BE58U (~$145) is Wi-Fi 7 with a 2.5 Gbps port and 2,200 sq ft coverage; the RT-AX1800S (~$58) is Wi-Fi 6 with gigabit-only ports and 1,500 sq ft. [src4, src6]

**Pick RT-BE58U if:** internet plan exceeds 1 Gbps, home is over 1,500 sq ft, or you want Wi-Fi 7 MLO.<br>
**Pick RT-AX1800S if:** internet plan is under 300 Mbps in a small apartment and you want enterprise-grade security for under $80.
