# Best Mini PCs (2026)

## What are the best mini PCs in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Apple Mac mini M4 (~$1189 MSRP, Apple direct)** -- best overall performance-per-dollar in a 5x5-inch chassis with M4 silicon (Amazon base config intermittently out of stock ahead of the M5 refresh). **Best value (Windows): Geekom A9 Max (~$1,189)** -- Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 Copilot+ PC with 32 GB DDR5 and 80 TOPS AI. **Best budget: Beelink EQR6 (~$1189)** -- Ryzen 5 6600U, 24 GB DDR5, built-in PSU, near-silent. [src1, src2, src3]

## Summary

The mini PC market in 2026 has reached a new level of maturity, with compact desktops now delivering performance that rivals or exceeds full-size towers in many workloads. From sub-$300 office machines to $3,200 gaming powerhouses, today's mini PCs pack multi-core processors with up to 16 Zen 5 cores, DDR5 memory up to 128 GB, discrete laptop-class GPUs up to the RTX 5080, and integrated graphics powerful enough for 1080p gaming -- all in chassis volumes ranging from 0.47 to 4.5 liters. [src1, src2, src3]

The Apple Mac mini M4 remains the best overall value for most users, starting at $4019 MSRP (Apple direct) with the M4 chip delivering exceptional single-threaded and multi-threaded performance in a 5-by-5-inch footprint; the base config has gone intermittently out of stock on Amazon as inventory tightens ahead of the M5 refresh, so Apple direct is the most reliable place to buy. For Windows users seeking top-tier productivity, the Geekom A9 Max with its AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 and Radeon 890M iGPU offers a compelling Copilot+ PC experience, on sale at ~$1,189 (list $1,399) in June 2026. Gamers now have legitimate options including the new ASUS ROG NUC (2025) with an RTX 5080 mobile GPU that hits triple-digit framerates at 1440p, and the more affordable Minisforum AtomMan G7 Ti with RTX 4070 at ~$2,000. [src1, src2, src4, src5]

Budget-conscious buyers benefit from AMD's Ryzen 6000 and 7000 series trickling down to sub-$3300 machines like the Beelink EQR6, while enthusiasts and AI developers can look to the Framework Desktop, Minisforum MS-S1 Max, and the **GMKtec EVO-X2 AI** for AMD Strix Halo-powered workstations with up to 128 GB of unified LPDDR5x memory and Radeon 8060S integrated graphics -- though EVO-X2 128 GB list pricing on Amazon climbed to ~$3,300 in May 2026 as launch promo allocations sold through. The **Minisforum G1 Pro** ($1,440) brings a desktop-grade RTX 5060 to a slim PS5-style chassis, undercutting the AtomMan G7 Ti for gaming. NPU integration for on-device AI has become a key differentiator, with AMD's XDNA 2 architecture delivering up to 50 TOPS of dedicated AI acceleration. A Mac mini M5 refresh is expected at WWDC 2026 (June), so buyers with no urgency may wait. [src3, src6, src7, src8, src9, src10]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Apple Mac mini M4 (~$499 MSRP) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/apple-mac-mini-m4)
The Mac mini M4 is a 5-by-5-inch cube with a 10-core CPU, 10-core GPU, and 16-core Neural Engine that delivers exceptional performance for everyday tasks, creative work, and even light gaming. Starting at $499 MSRP from Apple direct with 16 GB of unified memory and a 256 GB SSD, it offers unbeatable price-to-performance in the mini PC segment, with near-silent operation and support for up to three displays; the base config has gone intermittently out of stock on Amazon ahead of the M5 refresh, so Apple direct is the most reliable place to buy. The M4 Pro variant ($1,399) scales to 24 GB of RAM and Thunderbolt 5 for professional workflows. [src1, src2, src3]

### Best Budget: Beelink EQR6 (~$499) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/beelink-eqr6)
The Beelink EQR6 (current Amazon SKU is the Ryzen 5 6600U variant with 24 GB LPDDR5 RAM and 500 GB NVMe SSD) packs the processor into a chassis measuring just 4.96 by 4.96 by 1.74 inches, with a built-in 85W power supply that requires only a single cable. At ~$499 on Amazon for the 6600U configuration with dual 2.5G LAN, WiFi 6, and BT 5.2, it delivers near-silent operation and handles office productivity, web browsing, and light creative work with ease. Windows Central praised it as their favorite budget mini PC in 2026, highlighting the whisper-quiet thermals and built-in PSU design. [src3, src8]

### Best for Windows Productivity: Geekom A9 Max (~$1,189) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/geekom-a9-max)
The A9 Max features AMD's Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 with 12 cores and 24 threads boosting to 5.1 GHz, paired with 32 GB DDR5 and a Radeon 890M iGPU. As a Copilot+ PC delivering 80 TOPS of total AI acceleration (including 50 TOPS from the XDNA 2 NPU), it handles video editing, virtualization, and multitasking in a compact all-metal chassis. Connectivity is comprehensive with four front USB-A ports, an SD card reader, dual 2.5 GbE, USB4, dual HDMI 2.1, and WiFi 7. Multiple reviewers noted it ran near-silent during two months of daily use. The 1 TB SSD config is on sale at ~$1,189 on Amazon as of June 2026 (list price $1,399, ~15% off). [src1, src2, src3]

### Best for Gaming: ASUS ROG NUC (2025) (~$4,019) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/asus-rog-nuc-2025)
The ROG NUC (2025) packs an Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX and a discrete RTX 5080 mobile GPU with 16 GB GDDR7 into a 3-liter chassis with triple-fan cooling. NotebookCheck rated it 85% ("Good"), reporting that it "masters even demanding games in Full HD and often also in QHD smoothly," with triple-digit framerates in almost every benchmark at 1440p and 60+ fps at 4K with DLSS enabled. The 32 GB DDR5-6400 memory is user-upgradeable to 96 GB. Amazon currently lists the full-spec Core Ultra 9 / RTX 5080 / 2 TB configuration around $4,019 (list price $5,299, ~24% off), up from ~$3,765 in May 2026; it remains the most powerful mini gaming PC available. [src1, src4, src5]

### Best Gaming Value: Minisforum AtomMan G7 Ti (~$2,000) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/minisforum-atomman-g7-ti)
For ~$2,000 less than the ROG NUC (2025), the AtomMan G7 Ti delivers strong RTX 4070 mobile GPU performance paired with an Intel Core i9-14900HX (24 cores, 32 threads). At 2.88 liters and 5.67 pounds, it supports up to 96 GB DDR5-5600 and dual PCIe Gen 4 SSDs in RAID 0 with up to 7,000 MB/s throughput. WiFi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4 are included. Expect smooth 1080p gaming across the board and solid 1440p in most titles with DLSS, though the cooling solution can get audible under sustained load. Amazon list edged up to ~$2,000 in May 2026 as launch promo discounts ended. [src1, src2, src4]

### Best for AI and Local LLMs: Framework Desktop (~$1,099) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/framework-desktop)
The Framework Desktop is a 4.5-liter Mini-ITX system powered by AMD Ryzen AI Max Strix Halo APUs with up to 16 Zen 5 cores and Radeon 8060S integrated graphics (40 RDNA 3.5 compute units). The top configuration ($2,270 fully configured) supports 128 GB LPDDR5x-8000 unified memory, making it the ideal mini PC for running large language models locally. Tom's Hardware rated it 3.5/5, praising strong 1080p gaming performance and the customizable design. The standard Mini-ITX form factor with FlexATX PSU makes it one of the most repairable mini PCs on the market. [src1, src6]

### Most Portable: Khadas Mind 2S (~$1,599) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/khadas-mind-2s)
At just 2 cm thin and 435 grams (0.96 pounds), the Khadas Mind 2S is pocket-sized yet packs an Intel Core Ultra 7 255H with 16 cores, 64 GB LPDDR5X, and a 2 TB SSD. A built-in 5.55Wh battery provides up to 25 hours of standby and enables instant-on usage. Tom's Guide called it "the ultimate modular mini PC," highlighting the dock ecosystem that adds eGPU (Mind Graphics) or expanded I/O (Mind Dock) as needed. Thunderbolt 4 and USB4 ports at 40 Gbps round out the connectivity. Fan noise under load is the main trade-off. [src1, src2]

### Best Strix Halo (128 GB): GMKtec EVO-X2 AI (~$3,300 on Amazon) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/gmktec-evo-x2)
Tom's Hardware calls the EVO-X2 the first widely shipping Strix Halo mini PC and "the cleanest way to evaluate what Ryzen AI Max+ 395 can do." The 16-core Zen 5 CPU pairs with the 40-CU Radeon 8060S iGPU at frame rates that approach RTX 4070 Laptop class. The 128 GB / 2 TB Amazon SKU now lists around $3,300 (up from $1,999-$2,049 at January launch) as initial promo allocations sold through; the 96 GB variant still surfaces on GMKtec direct for ~$1,499 when available. The 128 GB config exposes up to 96 GB to the GPU -- an extraordinary amount of GPU-addressable memory. Quad 8K display output, USB4, WiFi 7. At current Amazon pricing it no longer undercuts Framework Desktop or Minisforum MS-S1 Max -- consider those alternatives if you can wait for restocks. [src9]

### Best Gaming Under $1,500 (NEW 2026): Minisforum G1 Pro (~$1,440) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/minisforum-g1-pro)
The first mini PC to ship with a true desktop-class RTX 5060 (8 GB GDDR7, not the mobile variant). Paired with a Ryzen 9 8945HX (16 cores, 32 threads, 5.4 GHz) in a slim PS5-style chassis with 245W performance budget. Tom's Guide tested it and concluded it "works just as well in the living room as it does at your desk." Includes 5GbE, WiFi 7, BT 5.4, and dual HDMI + dual DP 2.1 outputs for quad 4K. At ~$560 less than the current AtomMan G7 Ti Amazon price while offering newer 50-series GPU silicon, it is now the best gaming-mini-PC value under $1,500. [src1, src4]

## Important Caveats

- Prices reflect US retail as of May 2026 and fluctuate frequently. International pricing varies significantly, especially for brands like Geekom, Minisforum, GMKtec, and Beelink that sell direct from China.
- Mini PCs with mobile-class GPUs (RTX 4060/4070/5070/5080) run at lower TDPs than their desktop counterparts, so gaming benchmarks will be 20-40% below desktop GPU equivalents with the same name.
- RAM and storage upgradeability varies significantly: some models use soldered LPDDR5x (Mac mini M4, Framework Desktop, Khadas Mind 2S, MS-S1 Max), while others offer SODIMM slots (ROG NUC 2025, AtomMan G7 Ti, NucBox K6).
- Thermal performance and fan noise are critical differentiators in this category. Models with higher TDP processors may throttle under sustained loads in confined spaces without adequate airflow.
- This comparison focuses on commercially available, ready-to-ship models. Some configurations (particularly Framework Desktop top-tier and Minisforum MS-S1 Max) may have limited availability or longer shipping times.
- The Framework Desktop is sold directly through frame.work and not available on Amazon -- its buy link points to the official Framework store page (frame.work/desktop).
