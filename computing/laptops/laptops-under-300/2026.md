# Best Laptops Under $300 (2026)

## TL;DR

**Top pick: HP Chromebook 14 (~$298)** — Intel N100, 8GB LPDDR5, 128GB UFS, ~10-hour battery — the best-rounded sub-$300 machine reliably in stock. **Best Windows: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i (~$248)** — Intel N100, 15.6" FHD, MIL-STD build. **Best budget: Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Go (~$210)** — 3.2 lbs, ~12-hour battery, in stock. Note: the two editorial flagships — the Lenovo Chromebook Plus and Acer Aspire Go 15 — have both spiked to ~$420 (over budget; buy only near their ~$280 MSRP). [src1, src2, src5]

## Summary

The sub-$300 laptop market in 2026 splits into two clear camps: Windows laptops with Intel N-series processors and Chromebooks powered by Intel Celeron/N100 or MediaTek chips. The editorial consensus best-overall pick remains the Acer Aspire Go 15 (Intel Core i3-N305, 8GB LPDDR5, 15.6" FHD IPS), but as of mid-July 2026 its Amazon listing has spiked to ~$420 with low stock — over the $300 ceiling. The same spike has hit the two other Core i3-N305 flagships: the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i Chromebook Plus (previously ~$279, now ~$420) and the ASUS Vivobook Go 15 (~$410). At their ~$280 MSRP these are the class of the field; above $300 they fall out of scope, so the in-stock, in-budget picks below lead this guide. [src1, src2, src4]

For Chrome OS users still shopping under $300, the HP Chromebook 14 (~$298) now delivers the best combination of performance and build quality reliably in stock — Intel N100, 8GB LPDDR5, 128GB UFS — with the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i Chromebook Plus the pick to grab if it returns to its ~$279 MSRP. At the value end, the Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Go (~$210) and the 15.6" Lenovo IdeaPad 3i Chromebook (~$259) offer solid web-browsing machines with 10-12 hour battery life. For Windows under $300, the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i (~$248) and HP Laptop 15 (~$290) are the in-budget options. Chromebooks consistently outperform Windows laptops at the same price point for web-based tasks due to Chrome OS's lower resource overhead. [src1, src3, src5]

The biggest upgrade in 2026 is DDR5 memory trickling down to budget models, and Intel's N150/N200 processors replacing older Celeron chips with meaningful performance gains. However, at this price point, expect compromises: some models still ship with HD (1366x768) screens, 4GB RAM, or slow eMMC storage instead of SSDs. [src2, src6, src7]

## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Acer Aspire Go 15 vs Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i Chromebook Plus
The two flagship sub-$300 picks. Both use the same Intel Core i3-N305 with 8GB RAM, so raw performance is identical — the difference is OS. The Aspire Go 15 (~$280 MSRP, currently ~$420, Windows 11) runs Office desktop, Zoom, and any legacy Windows app; the Chromebook Plus (~$279 MSRP, currently ~$420) runs Google Workspace and Android apps with faster boot, smoother day-to-day responsiveness, and ChromeOS Auto Update support through ~2034. As of mid-July 2026 both have spiked to ~$420 and neither is in budget — pick on OS and grab whichever returns to its ~$280 street price first; if you need a machine today, the HP Chromebook 14 (~$298) is the in-budget stand-in.
**Pick the Aspire Go 15 if:** you need Windows desktop software (Office, QuickBooks, Adobe Reader, IT-mandated apps) or a 15.6" FHD screen — and you can get it near its ~$280 list price.
**Pick the Chromebook Plus if:** your workflow is browser-based and the larger 256GB SSD plus longer software lifespan matter more than a Windows install — again, at its ~$279 MSRP, not the current ~$420. [src1, src2, src4]

### HP Chromebook 14 vs Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Go
The budget Chromebook pair. The HP Chromebook 14 (~$298) wins on RAM (8GB LPDDR5 vs 4GB LPDDR4X), processor (Intel N100 vs older Celeron N4500), and storage speed (128GB UFS vs 64GB eMMC). The Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Go (~$210) wins on price, portability (3.2 lbs), battery (~12h vs ~10h), and spill-resistant keyboard.
**Pick the HP Chromebook 14 if:** you keep 8+ browser tabs open or run Android apps — the 8GB RAM is the difference between snappy and laggy.
**Pick the Galaxy Chromebook Go if:** weight, battery life, and durability for school commutes matter more than raw speed. [src3, src5, src6]

### HP Laptop 15 vs Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i
The two in-budget 15.6-inch Windows machines now that the Acer Aspire Go 15 has spiked over $300. The HP Laptop 15 (~$290) pairs an Intel N200, 4GB RAM, 15.6" HD screen and ~11.5h battery. The Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i (~$248) is cheaper, adds a sharper 15.6" FHD screen, an Intel N100, 128GB SSD, and MIL-STD-810H durability — but also ships with 4GB RAM.
**Pick the HP Laptop 15 if:** you want the longest battery life (~11.5h) and a slightly larger keyboard deck.
**Pick the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i if:** you want the FHD screen, lower price, and military-grade durability — the better-rounded in-budget Windows pick. [src2, src4, src7]

### ASUS Vivobook Go 15 vs Acer Aspire Go 15
The AMD vs Intel matchup — both have spiked to ~$410-420 as of mid-July 2026, so this is a comparison for when prices return to their ~$270-280 street levels. The Vivobook Go 15 (~$410 now / ~$270 list, Ryzen 3 7320U, 8GB DDR5, MIL-STD-810H) edges Intel on multi-threaded performance and durability certification. The Aspire Go 15 (~$420 now / ~$280 list, Core i3-N305, 8GB LPDDR5) edges AMD on single-threaded responsiveness, battery life (~10h vs ~8h), and broader review-site endorsement.
**Pick the Vivobook Go 15 if:** you want better light-photo-editing performance or MIL-STD-810H durability for travel.
**Pick the Aspire Go 15 if:** you prioritize battery life and editorial consensus from RTINGS, Tom's Guide, and PCWorld. [src1, src3, src6]

### Acer Chromebook Plus 515 vs Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i Chromebook Plus
Two Chromebook Plus-certified models with ~$279 MSRPs, though as of mid-July 2026 the Acer 515 is "Currently unavailable" on Amazon and the Lenovo Slim 3i Chromebook Plus has spiked to ~$420 — so this is a matchup for when both return to street price. The Acer 515 has a larger 15.6" FHD screen and Intel Core i3-1215U (12th-gen U-series, faster peak performance). The Lenovo Slim 3i has a more portable 14" FHD screen, the newer i3-N305 (more efficient cores, better battery), and 256GB SSD vs 128-256GB on the Acer.
**Pick the Acer Chromebook Plus 515 if:** you want the bigger screen and peak CPU performance for occasional video calls plus heavy Workspace use.
**Pick the Lenovo Slim 3i Chromebook Plus if:** portability and battery efficiency matter more than screen size. [src1, src5]

## Key Market Trends (2026)

- **DDR5 reaches budget tier**: Multiple sub-$300 laptops now ship with DDR5 memory (Acer Aspire Go 15, HP Chromebook 14, Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3i), offering meaningful speed improvements over DDR4 at no price premium. [src2, src4]
- **Intel N-series replaces Celeron branding**: Intel's N100, N150, and N200 processors replace the old Celeron naming while delivering 15-30% better performance per watt. The Core i3-N305 pushes into territory previously reserved for $400+ laptops. [src1, src2]
- **Chromebook Plus standard raises the floor**: Google's Chromebook Plus certification requires minimum 8GB RAM, 128GB storage, and AI features — eliminating the worst budget Chromebook compromises. [src5]
- **UFS replaces eMMC**: Universal Flash Storage is 2-3x faster than eMMC for reads and writes, and is appearing in budget models like the HP Chromebook 14 and Acer Aspire Go 15. [src4, src7]
- **AI features at budget prices**: Both Chrome OS (Google AI) and Windows 11 (Copilot) now include AI assistants even on sub-$300 hardware, though performance varies with available RAM. [src2, src3]
