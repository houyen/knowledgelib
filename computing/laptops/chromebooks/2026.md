## What are the best Chromebooks in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Acer Chromebook Plus Spin 714 (~$699)** — Intel Core Ultra 5, aluminum chassis, Thunderbolt 4, stylus.
**Best value: Acer Chromebook Plus 514 (~$349–399)** — Plus-certified, 8GB/512GB, 13h battery.

## Summary

The Chromebook landscape in April 2026 is defined by three major shifts: Google's Chromebook Plus certification has raised the floor on hardware quality, ChromeOS 144 (January 28, 2026) brought native Gemini 3 integration to every Plus-certified device, and the CES 2026 wave from HP, Lenovo, ASUS, and Acer added Wi-Fi 7 and brighter displays across the mainstream tier. The best overall Chromebook is the Acer Chromebook Plus Spin 714 (~$699) for its Intel Core Ultra 5 115U processor, aluminum build, Thunderbolt 4, and versatile 360-degree hinge with stylus. The best value pick is the Acer Chromebook Plus 514 (~$349-399) which delivers an Intel Core 3 N355 processor, 8GB RAM, a 512GB SSD, and nearly 13 hours of battery life at a remarkably low price. [src1, src2, src4, src8]

For those willing to invest in a premium experience, the Lenovo Chromebook Plus 14 (~$649-749) features a stunning 14-inch OLED display with 100% DCI-P3 color, a MediaTek Kompanio Ultra 910 with integrated NPU, and 14+ hours of real-world battery life. Engadget measured 14 hours of video playback, while Tom's Guide called it "simply the best" Chromebook available. The Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Plus (~$499) is the thinnest option at just 0.46 inches and 2.58 lbs with an AMOLED display, and recently dropped well below its $699 launch price. [src1, src4, src5]

Budget buyers have more choices than ever. The ASUS Chromebook CX15 starts around $199 with a 15.6-inch FHD display and MIL-STD 810H durability, while the ASUS Chromebook CM14 Flip (~$299) offers a spill-resistant keyboard, antimicrobial coating, and 14-hour battery life for students and kids. For cloud gaming, the Acer Chromebook Plus 516 GE (~$549-649) pairs a 16-inch 2.5K 120Hz display with an Ethernet port for stable latency on GeForce NOW. The large-screen wave from CES 2026 is now shipping: the Lenovo Chromebook Plus i 15 (~$469.99 starting) is on sale with a 15.3-inch 2K 400-nit display, Intel Core 3 N355, 8GB LPDDR5, and an optional 120Hz touchscreen variant; the refreshed HP Chromebook Plus x360 14 (Intel Core 3 N355, 14" 2K 1920x1200 16:10) is shipping after its February 2026 release; and Lenovo's new Chromebook Plus 2-in-1 14" Intel ($579 MSRP) earned a 4-star Chrome Unboxed review on April 23, 2026 — though its real value emerges only when discounted to ~$372. Note: Tom's Guide's April 14, 2026 update now ranks the Lenovo Chromebook Plus 14 as the #1 overall pick, while RTINGS, PCWorld, and Engadget continue to favor the Acer Spin 714 — choose between them based on whether you prioritize OLED display (Lenovo) or aluminum chassis with Thunderbolt 4 (Acer). ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### Acer Chromebook Plus Spin 714 vs Lenovo Chromebook Plus 14
Both retail at ~$649-749 and represent the top of the 2026 lineup. The Acer wins on chassis (aluminum + Thunderbolt 4 + Wi-Fi 6E) and Intel Core Ultra 5 versatility; the Lenovo wins on display (14" OLED, 100% DCI-P3) and battery (Engadget measured 14h video). Tom's Guide's April 2026 update flipped to Lenovo at #1; RTINGS / PCWorld / Engadget still favor the Acer. [src1, src2, src3, src4]

**Pick A (Acer Spin 714) if:** you need Thunderbolt 4, x86 compatibility for occasional Linux apps, or stylus/360° convertibility.
**Pick B (Lenovo Plus 14) if:** you want the best Chromebook display ever shipped, fanless silence, and maximum battery.

### Acer Chromebook Plus 514 vs HP Chromebook Plus x360 14
Both are mid-range Plus-certified machines with Intel Core 3 N355. The Acer 514 is a clamshell at ~$349-399 with 512GB SSD; the HP x360 14 (~$259-599) trades capacity for a 360° convertible hinge and stylus support. The Acer 514 is the better pure-value pick; the HP x360 14 is the better 2-in-1 for mixed work + tablet use. [src2, src6, src10]

**Pick A (Acer Plus 514) if:** budget is the deciding factor and you don't need touch-fold form factor.
**Pick B (HP x360 14) if:** you want a convertible 2-in-1 without paying premium-tier prices.

### Lenovo Chromebook Duet 11 vs ASUS Chromebook CM14 Flip
Both target the budget/portable end (~$249-300). The Duet 11 is a true detachable tablet (1.13 lbs tablet, included keyboard/kickstand) with a 10.95" 2K display — best for media and light browsing. The CM14 Flip is a full 14" laptop with MIL-STD 810H durability and a 360° hinge — best for classroom abuse. [src3, src5, src6, src7]

**Pick A (Duet 11) if:** you want a tablet first, laptop second; portability and 2K display matter.
**Pick B (CM14 Flip) if:** you need a rugged everyday-use Chromebook with spill-resistant keyboard for kids or students.

### Acer Chromebook Plus 516 GE vs Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Plus
Both sit near $549-699 with 15.6-16" displays. The Acer 516 GE is purpose-built for cloud gaming (16" 120Hz, RJ45 Ethernet, RGB keys); the Samsung Galaxy Chromebook Plus is the thinnest premium Chromebook (0.46", 2.58 lbs) with an AMOLED screen for media and productivity. [src1, src2, src4]

**Pick A (Acer 516 GE) if:** GeForce NOW / Xbox Cloud Gaming is the primary use — RJ45 Ethernet alone is decisive.
**Pick B (Samsung Galaxy Plus) if:** portability + AMOLED display + thin-and-light Mac-like aesthetic matters more than gaming features.

### ASUS Chromebook Plus CX34 vs Acer Chromebook Plus Spin 514
Both Plus-certified mid-premium options. The CX34 (~$499-620) packs fast x86 silicon (Intel Core i5-1335U) — ideal for tab-heavy productivity. The Acer Spin 514 (~$699-725 for the MediaTek Kompanio Ultra 910 / 12GB variant) trades raw CPU for the most efficient ARM Chromebook chip plus 360° convertibility. [src2, src3, src4, src5]

**Pick A (ASUS CX34) if:** you want maximum x86 performance, Linux/Crostini compatibility, and student-friendly pricing.
