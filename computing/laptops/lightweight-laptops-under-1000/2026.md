## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### MacBook Air 13" M4 vs ASUS Zenbook A14 (2025 Snapdragon X)
The Apple M4 wins on raw performance, app ecosystem, and resale value; the Zenbook A14 wins on weight (2.18 lb vs 2.7 lb), battery life (32h video vs 18h), and price (~$749-950 vs ~$919). The Zenbook is also the lightest sub-$1000 Windows laptop available. ARM Windows compatibility is the only real risk on the Zenbook. [src1, src4, src11]
**Pick MacBook Air M4 if:** you want macOS, the best fanless silicon under $1000, and broadest app + accessory ecosystem.
**Pick Zenbook A14 (2025) if:** you need the absolute lightest 14" laptop, the longest battery life, and Windows + Copilot+ AI features.

### Lenovo Yoga Slim 7x vs Acer Swift Go 14
Both are Windows OLED options near $795-918. Yoga Slim 7x has the better display (14.5" 3K, 1000 nits, 60-90Hz) and longer battery (23.5h vs 12.5h) but uses Snapdragon X Elite (ARM); Acer Swift Go uses Intel Core Ultra 7 155H (x86, full compatibility) and is lighter on the wallet (~$810) with a more standard 14" WUXGA panel. [src5, src7, src8]
**Pick Yoga Slim 7x if:** display quality and battery life are your top priorities, and you can tolerate ARM Windows.
**Pick Acer Swift Go 14 if:** you need guaranteed x86 compatibility, more I/O (2x TB4), and a slightly lower price.

### HP Pavilion Aero 13 vs ASUS Zenbook A14 (2025)
