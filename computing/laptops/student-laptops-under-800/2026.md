# Best Laptops for Students Under $800 (2026)

## What are the best laptops for students under $800 in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 16 (~$670)** — Ryzen AI 7 350, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, 14+ hr battery, aluminum chassis; price dropped ~15% since spring. **Best value: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3x (~$560)** — Snapdragon X with class-leading 16:29 hr tested battery and 16 GB RAM. **Best budget: Acer Aspire Go 15 (~$380)** — Intel i3-N305 with 16 GB / 512 GB SSD. **Best macOS: Apple MacBook Neo (~$590)** — $499 with edu pricing. [src3, src5, src7, src8]

## Summary

The student laptop market under $800 was upended in March 2026 when Apple launched the MacBook Neo at $599 ($499 with education pricing). Powered by the A18 Pro chip, the Neo delivers a tested 13:28 hours of battery life (Tom's Guide), a 13-inch Liquid Retina display, and the full macOS ecosystem in a 2.7-lb aluminum chassis. For students who want macOS, it is now the default recommendation -- no sale-hunting required. However, its 8 GB RAM (non-upgradeable) means Windows alternatives still win for heavy multitasking and STEM workloads. [src5, src7]

For students who need 16 GB RAM or prefer Windows, the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 16 is now the best Windows all-rounder: its price has dropped ~15% to ~$670 (from ~$786 in spring), bringing the AMD Ryzen AI 7 350, 16 GB DDR5 RAM, 512 GB SSD, and a premium aluminum chassis well under $700. For maximum battery life, the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3x (~$560) delivers 16:29 hours of tested runtime (Tom's Guide), 16 GB LPDDR5 RAM, and a 15.3-inch 1920x1200 IPS touchscreen in a 3.42-lb chassis -- the longest-lasting Windows option here, though it has risen ~$40 since spring. The Acer Aspire Go 15 (2025) at ~$380 is still the best pure-budget option with 16 GB RAM and 512 GB SSD. [src3, src7, src8]

Key factors for students in 2026: portability (under 4 lbs ideal), battery life (10+ hours for a full day of classes), 16 GB RAM for smooth multitasking, and 512 GB SSD minimum. Nearly every laptop on this list supports Wi-Fi 6 or newer and charges via USB-C. The MacBook Air M4 (13-inch) sits at ~$799-$924 depending on retailer (Amazon listing ~$924 in June 2026 as M5 supply ramps; $799 / $699 edu at Apple and some retailers), making it the premium option for students who want M-series performance with 16 GB RAM. For Chromebook users, the ASUS Chromebook Plus CX34 (~$650) is now the most reliably in-stock ChromeOS pick under $800; the OLED Lenovo Chromebook Plus 14 with MediaTek Kompanio Ultra 910 remains the quality benchmark (13:19 hr battery, fanless 2.58-lb design) but its current Amazon listing has spiked above $1,000 on remaining stock, so shop it elsewhere or watch for a restock. [src1, src2, src7, src9]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall (Windows): Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 16 (~$670) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lenovo-ideapad-slim-5-16-student-laptops-under-800)
The IdeaPad Slim 5 hits the sweet spot of price, performance, and build quality -- and its ~15% price drop to ~$670 since spring makes it the clear Windows pick for most students. Its aluminum chassis feels premium well beyond its price, and the 16-inch 1920x1200 IPS touchscreen offers ample workspace for split-screen multitasking. The AMD Ryzen AI 7 350 handles everything from heavy browser sessions to light photo editing, while 16 GB DDR5 RAM and a 512 GB SSD ensure smooth multitasking with room for files. PCWorld awarded it 4.5 stars, praising its 14+ hour battery and full sRGB color coverage. The 16 GB RAM and x86 compatibility advantage over the MacBook Neo make it the better pick for STEM students running multiple apps. [src3, src4]

### Best Battery Life (Windows): Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3x (~$560) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lenovo-ideapad-slim-3x)
The IdeaPad Slim 3x sets a new battery life record for budget Windows laptops: 16 hours and 29 minutes in Tom's Guide testing. The Qualcomm Snapdragon X processor delivers efficient ARM-based performance with 16 GB LPDDR5 RAM, a 15.3-inch 1920x1200 IPS touchscreen, and Copilot+ PC AI features including an NPU for on-device tasks. At 3.42 lbs, it is lighter than most 15-inch alternatives. The trade-offs: the display and speakers are mediocre, GPU performance is weak for gaming, and some legacy x86 software may have ARM compatibility issues. A second SSD slot allows storage expansion. Note its price has risen ~$40 to ~$560 since spring, so it now sits only ~$110 below the larger Slim 5 16. [src7, src8]

### Best macOS Value: Apple MacBook Neo (~$590, ~$499 edu) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/macbook-neo-silver)
Apple's biggest surprise of 2026 -- a full macOS laptop for $599 ($499 with education pricing). The A18 Pro chip delivers strong single-threaded performance (47% faster than M1 in single-core benchmarks), 16 hours of battery life from a compact 36.5 Wh battery, and a 13-inch Liquid Retina display at 500 nits brightness. At 2.7 lbs, it is tied with the MacBook Air as the most portable option on this list. The trade-offs: only 8 GB RAM (non-upgradeable), no backlit keyboard, no True Tone display, and one USB 2.0 port alongside one USB 3.0 port. For students doing light coursework in the Apple ecosystem, it is an incredible value. [src5, src7]

### Best Premium: Apple MacBook Air 13-inch M4 (~$799-$924) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/macbook-air-13-m4-student-laptops-under-800)
With the M5 MacBook Air launching at $1,099, the M4 model trades around $799 ($699 with education pricing) at Apple and some retailers, and ~$924 on the Amazon listing as of June 2026 (only ~10 units left on the listed Sky Blue configuration). It outperforms the MacBook Neo with 16 GB RAM, the more powerful M4 chip, a brighter 2560x1664 Liquid Retina display, Thunderbolt 4 ports, a backlit keyboard, and 18-hour battery life. For students who need macOS with the power to run Xcode, Final Cut Pro, or multiple demanding apps simultaneously, the extra spend over the Neo is well spent -- chase the $799/$699-edu price rather than the Amazon listing. [src1, src4]

### Best Budget: Acer Aspire Go 15 (2025) (~$380) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/acer-aspire-go-15-2025)
The best laptop under $400 for students who need maximum specs per dollar. The 2025 model offers 16 GB DDR5 RAM and 512 GB SSD -- specs matching laptops costing twice as much. The 15.6-inch Full HD IPS display and Intel Core i3-N305 handle everyday productivity without strain. The main trade-offs are a dim display and no keyboard backlight. [src2, src3]

### Best for STEM Students: HP Pavilion Plus 14 (~$680) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hp-pavilion-plus-14-2025)
STEM students need more processing power for coding, data analysis, and computational tools. The AMD Ryzen 5 8640HS provides strong multitasking performance, and the 360-degree hinge allows tent, stand, and tablet modes. Battery life is adequate at around 10 hours. [src3, src4]

With the OLED Lenovo Chromebook Plus 14's Amazon listing spiking above $1,000 on remaining stock, the ASUS Chromebook Plus CX34 (~$650, up from ~$550 in spring) is now the most reliably in-stock ChromeOS pick under $800. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### MacBook Neo vs MacBook Air M4
The Neo (~$590) is the right macOS pick for the majority of students -- $499 with edu pricing, 2.7 lbs, 16 hr battery, and a Liquid Retina display. The Air M4 (~$924 on the current Amazon listing; ~$799, or $699 edu, at Apple and some retailers) doubles the RAM to 16 GB, adds Thunderbolt 4, a backlit keyboard, a brighter 2560x1664 display, and an extra 2 hours of battery. [src1, src5, src7]
