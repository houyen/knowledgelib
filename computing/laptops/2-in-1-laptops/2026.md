# Best 2-in-1 Laptops (2026)

## What are the best 2-in-1 laptops in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: HP OmniBook Ultra Flip 14 (~$1,750)** -- 17.5-hour Lunar Lake convertible with 2.8K HDR OLED [src1, src2]. **Best value: Lenovo Yoga 9i 2-in-1 Aura Edition Gen 10 (~$1,450)** -- flagship audio + 32GB/1TB OLED now discounted as Gen 11 launches [src3]. **Best budget: Lenovo Yoga 7i 2-in-1 Gen 10 (~$2499-$1,220)** -- the cheapest genuine OLED Lunar Lake convertible on the shelf [src5].

## Summary

The 2-in-1 laptop market in spring 2026 is in an unusually dynamic state: Intel's Panther Lake (Core Ultra Series 3) has been shipping in convertibles since Q1, Lenovo's new Yoga 9i Gen 11 (Panther Lake) is now confirmed for May 2026 starting at $1,949 with a new Canvas Mode tilt for artists, Asus' redesigned **Zenbook Duo (2026, UX8407)** uses Panther Lake and stretches battery life to nearly 20 hours, and Microsoft's two-stage Surface Pro 12 rollout puts Intel Core Ultra variants in spring and Snapdragon X2 + OLED versions in summer 2026 (July-Sept). HP's CES 2026 OmniBook Ultra 14 clamshell shipped in January with Panther Lake and an exclusive 85 TOPS Snapdragon X2 Elite NPU; an OmniBook Ultra Flip refresh is expected mid-2026. Meanwhile the Lunar Lake (Core Ultra 200V) generation remains the shipping workhorse at most price points. [src6, src7, src8]

The **HP OmniBook Ultra Flip 14** remains the consensus top mainstream pick going into Q2 2026, delivering a 2.8K OLED display with HDR True Black 600, Intel Core Ultra 7 256V (Lunar Lake), and 17.5 hours of tested battery life in a 2.97-pound chassis. Street pricing has climbed to ~$1,750 for the 16GB/2TB Amazon config as the original launch price softens [src1, src2, src4]. The **Lenovo Yoga 9i 2-in-1 Aura Edition Gen 10** is now down to ~$1,450 on Amazon as the Gen 11 takes the flagship slot, keeping its crown for audio and build quality. The **Gen 11 Panther Lake** successor (~$1,949, ships May 2026) adds 32GB LPDDR5X-7467 memory, a Canvas Mode drawing tilt, and improved NPU performance for Copilot+ features [src3, src5, src7]. For large-screen enthusiasts, the **Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro 360 16** delivers a 16-inch 3K AMOLED panel with S Pen included, up to 22 hours of battery, at ~$2,000 (32GB/1TB) [src1].

The detachable segment is still anchored by the **Microsoft Surface Pro 11**. Amazon's keyboard-bundled SKU runs ~$1,299 (16GB/1TB OLED + Surface Pro Keyboard), or ~$2034 tablet-only at Microsoft Store, weighing just 1.97 pounds with Snapdragon X Elite and up to 14 hours of real-world battery [src1, src3]. The Surface Pro 12 is rolling out in two stages -- Intel Core Ultra variants in spring 2026 and Snapdragon X2 Plus/Elite OLED variants summer 2026 (July-Sept) -- with starting prices expected to be at or above current Surface Pro pricing. Wait if you can; the OLED upgrade is meaningful [src8]. For creative professionals, the **ASUS ProArt PX13** pairs AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 with NVIDIA RTX 4050/4060/4070 in a 3.04-pound convertible [src1, src3]. For dual-screen productivity, the new **Asus Zenbook Duo (2026)** at $2,099-$2,299 is the most ambitious 2-in-1 on the market, with dual 14" 3K 144Hz OLEDs and 19h 43min tested battery on Panther Lake [src6, src9]. Mid-range buyers can still get genuine OLED -- the **Lenovo Yoga 7i 2-in-1 Gen 10** runs ~$1,220 on Amazon in the 16GB/1TB OLED config, and lower-tier 16GB/512GB Yoga 7i builds drop closer to $900 at OEM direct [src3, src5]. Prices across the category range from ~$900 for mid-range OLED convertibles to ~$2,700 for the high-end ROG Flow Z13 gaming tablet (128GB) [src1, src4]. Note: 2-in-1 convertibles are often confused with detachable Windows tablets -- if you want a tablet-first device with an optional keyboard, see the Windows tablets card instead.

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: HP OmniBook Ultra Flip 14 (~$1,750) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/hp-omnibook-ultra-flip-14)
The OmniBook Ultra Flip 14 earns the top spot with a best-in-class combination of display quality, battery life, and build for the price. Its 14-inch 2.8K OLED display delivers HDR True Black 600 certification with variable 48-120Hz refresh, and the Intel Core Ultra 7 256V (Lunar Lake) processor hits 17.5 hours in battery testing -- a transformative result for a convertible. Amazon's current Ultra 7-256V / 16GB / 2TB config is ~$1,750; lower-spec builds at HP direct dip to $1,250-$1,400. The CNC-machined aluminum chassis weighs just 2.97 pounds and includes Thunderbolt 4, Wi-Fi 7, and a 9MP IR AI camera. HP confirmed at CES 2026 that a next-gen OmniBook Ultra with Panther Lake and Snapdragon X2 Elite options is coming mid-2026, so heavy-workload buyers may want to wait. [src1, src2, src4]

### Best Premium Build (Lunar Lake): Lenovo Yoga 9i 2-in-1 Aura Edition Gen 10 (~$1,450) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lenovo-yoga-9i-2in1-aura-edition)
The Yoga 9i Gen 10 continues to deliver the best audio and keyboard experience of any convertible. Its 14-inch 2.8K OLED reaches 1,100 nits peak brightness with 120Hz refresh, and the signature four-speaker system with rotating-hinge tweeters is unmatched. The 75Wh battery provides 13-15 hours of mixed use with Rapid Charge Express. Amazon has discounted the 32GB/1TB SKU to ~$1,450 as the Gen 11 takes over the flagship slot -- making the Gen 10 the single best value at the $1,500 tier today. [src1, src3, src5]

### Best for Drawing and Canvas Mode: Lenovo Yoga 9i 2-in-1 Aura Edition Gen 11 (~$1,949) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/lenovo-yoga-9i-2in1-gen11)
Announced at MWC 2026 and shipping May 2026, the Gen 11 brings Intel Panther Lake (Core Ultra 7 355), 32GB LPDDR5X-7467, and a new Canvas Mode that tilts the screen slightly to improve writing and drawing ergonomics. The 2.8K 120Hz OLED and MIL-SPEC aluminum chassis carry over. Comes with a magnetic stylus. Early reviews call it a refined evolution rather than a revolution -- buy only if you want Canvas Mode or the latest NPU for Copilot+ AI features; the Gen 10 remains the better value today. Lenovo is known for steep post-launch discounts, so MSRP at launch is rarely the price you actually pay 60-90 days later. [src7]

### Best Dual-Screen: Asus Zenbook Duo (2026, UX8407) (~$2,099) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/asus-zenbook-duo-2026)
The Zenbook Duo 2026 is the most ambitious 2-in-1 on the market. It ships with two 14-inch 3K 144Hz Lumina Pro OLED touchscreens (1,000 nits, 100% DCI-P3), Intel Core Ultra X9 386H/388H (Panther Lake, 50 TOPS NPU), 32GB LPDDR5X, a 99Wh battery, and a detachable Bluetooth keyboard with built-in stand. Tom's Hardware measured 19h 43m of real-world battery with the keyboard attached. Hinge gap shrinks to 8.28mm and the chassis is 5% smaller than the prior-gen Zenbook Duo. At $2,099-$2,299 it is not for everyone, but no other shipping 2-in-1 offers true dual-screen productivity. [src6, src9]

### Best Large Screen: Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro 360 16 (~$1,757) -- [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/samsung-galaxy-book5-pro-360-16)
The Galaxy Book5 Pro 360 remains the standout 16-inch convertible for 2026.
