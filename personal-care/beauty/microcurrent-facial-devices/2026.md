# Best Microcurrent Facial Devices (2026)

## What are the best microcurrent facial devices in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: NuFACE Trinity+ (~$395) — the consensus best overall and "best for lifting," with 3 frequencies, FDA clearance, and a 25% boost button.**
**Best value: NuFACE Mini+ (~$150) — same FDA-cleared microcurrent and app as the Trinity in a portable, lower-cost body, now heavily discounted.**
**Best budget: Solawave 4-in-1 (~$110) — the viral multitasker combining galvanic current, red light, massage, and warmth.**
Nanocurrent specialist ZIIP Halo (~$400, brand-direct) is the splurge pick for long-term skin health.
[<a href="https://www.purewow.com/beauty/best-microcurrent-devices">src6</a>, <a href="https://www.mindbodygreen.com/articles/nuface-vs-ziip">src4</a>]

## Summary

Microcurrent — low-level electrical current that stimulates the facial muscles to lift and tone — is one of 2026's most-searched at-home beauty categories, marketed as a "non-invasive facelift" and trending heavily on TikTok [src1, src6]. The consensus best overall across PureWow, Editorialist, and Skinthusiast is the **NuFACE Trinity+** (~$395), which delivers 3 customizable frequencies/intensities plus a 25% boost button, is FDA-cleared, and is praised as beginner-friendly while still satisfying experienced users [src2, src6]. Its smaller sibling the **NuFACE Mini+** (~$150, recently discounted from ~$250) runs the same microcurrent and app at a lower price, making it the value pick [src4, src6].

The most-cited rivalry is NuFACE vs **ZIIP Halo** (~$400). ZIIP layers *nanocurrent* (a much smaller current) on top of microcurrent — micro targets the muscle for an immediate lift, while nano is claimed to work at the cellular level (ATP, collagen, elastin) for longer-term skin health [src4, src7]. Reviewers who prioritize comfort, portability, and long-term results lean ZIIP; those who want a stronger, more adjustable lift and a lower entry price lean NuFACE [src4, src5, src7]. The **FOREO Bear 2** (~$261) is the best beginner device thanks to its Anti-Shock System (which scans skin resistance 100x/second and prevents zaps) and 5 adjustable intensities, and it earns the strongest jawline-definition praise [src3, src5, src8].

For multitaskers, the **Therabody TheraFace PRO** (~$420) is the most versatile, combining microcurrent with percussive massage and LED light, while the **Solawave 4-in-1** (~$110) is the budget viral pick (galvanic current + red light + massage + warmth, no app) [src6]. Pro-grade buyers gravitate to **7E Wellness MyoLift** devices and the lightweight **Bio-Therapeutic bt-Sculpt** [src6]. Across every device, two rules dominate: results require ongoing use (they reverse if you stop), and a conductive gel is mandatory [src2, src6].

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Value: NuFACE Mini+ (~$150) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/nuface-mini-plus)
The portable sibling of the Trinity+ runs the same FDA-cleared microcurrent and the same NuFACE app for ~$245 less, with 3 settings, and has recently been discounted to around $150. ## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### NuFACE Trinity+ vs ZIIP Halo
The defining matchup. NuFACE wins on adjustable strength, attachment ecosystem (LED, eye/lip), a lower-cost Mini entry point, and not requiring the app. ZIIP wins on nanocurrent (claimed cellular/long-term benefit), comfort, weight, and portability. Roughly the same flagship price (~$395-400). [src4, src7]

**Pick NuFACE Trinity+ if:** you want a stronger, adjustable, attachment-expandable lift and the option to skip the app.
**Pick ZIIP Halo if:** you prioritize comfort, portability, and long-term skin health from nanocurrent, and don't mind app-only control.

### NuFACE Trinity+ vs FOREO Bear 2
Trinity+ is the all-rounder with deeper customization and the larger attachment ecosystem at ~$395. Bear 2 (~$230) is gentler and more beginner-proof thanks to its Anti-Shock System, and reviewers give it the edge on jawline definition specifically. [src3, src5, src8]

**Pick NuFACE Trinity+ if:** you want maximum lift, customization, and add-ons.
**Pick FOREO Bear 2 if:** you're new to microcurrent, want auto-adjusting comfort, or your main goal is jawline contour for less money.

### NuFACE Mini+ vs FOREO Bear 2
Both sit in the value mid-tier, and after recent discounts Mini+ (~$150) undercuts Bear 2 (~$230). Mini+ gives you real FDA-cleared NuFACE microcurrent and the NuFACE app/attachment compatibility in a compact body. Bear 2 adds T-Sonic massage and the auto-adjusting Anti-Shock comfort layer. [src4, src5, src6]

**Pick NuFACE Mini+ if:** you want the proven NuFACE system and ecosystem at the lowest price.
**Pick FOREO Bear 2 if:** you want the gentlest learning curve plus massage, and value jawline sculpting.

### Therabody TheraFace PRO vs Solawave 4-in-1
Both are multitaskers, but at very different tiers. TheraFace PRO (~$420) is a serious 8-in-1 with genuine microcurrent, percussive massage, and LED. Solawave (~$110) is an entry-level galvanic-current glow wand. [src6]

**Pick TheraFace PRO if:** you want a powerful do-everything device and will use the massage + LED modes.
**Pick Solawave 4-in-1 if:** you want an affordable, gentle daily depuffing/glow wand and don't need clinical-strength toning.

## Important Caveats

- Prices are approximate US street prices as of July 2026. They swing widely on promotions (the category spans ~$110 to $595). ZIIP Halo is brand-direct/Sephora (its Amazon listing is frequently out of stock — buy from ziipbeauty.com); MyoLift QT Plus, NuFACE FIX, and the original FOREO Bear now have live Amazon listings but stock and pricing move — check the brand site if a listing is unavailable.
- Microcurrent results are not permanent. They require ongoing use (commonly 5x/week for 60 days, then 2-3x/week maintenance) and reverse within weeks if you stop. [src2, src6]
- A conductive gel or serum is required for every device here; budget ~$30-50 per ~3 months on top of the device price. [src2]
- FDA clearance covers facial stimulation safety, not long-term anti-aging efficacy. Independent long-term clinical evidence for at-home microcurrent is limited, and several devices (Solawave, MyoLift Mini, bt-Sculpt, TheraFace) are not FDA-cleared. [src5, src6]
- Microcurrent is contraindicated for people who are pregnant, have epilepsy, a pacemaker or implanted electrical device, active cancer, or metal/dental implants in the treatment area. Consult a doctor first if any apply.
