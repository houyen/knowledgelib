## Head-to-Head Comparisons

### FOREO Luna 4 vs FOREO Luna 4 Plus
Both share the same 16-intensity T-Sonic cleansing engine and medical-grade silicone, so cleansing performance is identical. The Plus adds near-infrared LED light therapy and dual microcurrent pins for anti-aging — but at ~$399 versus ~$113 for the standard Luna 4 (June 2026 street prices), you pay roughly $285 for cosmetic anti-aging features that do nothing for cleansing. [src7, src8]
**Pick Luna 4 if:** you want best-in-class cleansing at the best price and don't need LED/microcurrent.
**Pick Luna 4 Plus if:** you specifically want to consolidate cleansing, red-light therapy, and microcurrent toning into one device and the ~3x price is acceptable.

### FOREO Luna 4 vs PMD Clean Pro
The Luna 4 (~$113) wins on battery longevity (600 uses/charge), intensity range (16 levels), and pure cleansing pedigree. The PMD Clean Pro (~$104) counters with ActiveWarmth heat therapy that opens pores and boosts serum absorption — a feature the Luna 4 lacks entirely. After recent discounts both sit near $100-$115. [src1, src2, src3]
**Pick Luna 4 if:** cleansing power, granular intensity control, and longest battery life matter most.
**Pick PMD Clean Pro if:** you want heated massage for anti-aging serum absorption alongside solid silicone cleansing.

### PMD Beauty Clean vs FOREO Luna 4 mini
The PMD Beauty Clean (~$59) is a longtime review-site value favorite: four SonicGlow modes, no replacement heads, and the best dollar-for-dollar value among dedicated budget picks. The Luna 4 mini (~$139) more than doubles the price but adds medical-grade silicone, 12 intensity levels, and a two-zone head better suited to sensitive/reactive skin. [src1, src2]
**Pick PMD Beauty Clean if:** you want proven, effective silicone cleansing at the lowest price with no fuss.
**Pick Luna 4 mini if:** you have sensitive or rosacea-prone skin and want fine intensity control plus FOREO build quality.

### Michael Todd Soniclear Elite vs PMD Clean Pro
The Soniclear Elite (~$149) uses antimicrobial bristles at 18,000 moves/min for aggressive mechanical exfoliation — better for oily, thick skin with sebum plugs — but needs brush-head replacements every 3-6 months (~$30-$60/year). The PMD Clean Pro (~$104) is silicone with zero recurring cost plus ActiveWarmth heat. After the PMD discount, the silicone option is now both cheaper upfront and cheaper to own. [src1, src4]
**Pick Soniclear Elite if:** you have oily/thick skin and want the deepest bristle-based exfoliation, accepting refill costs.
**Pick PMD Clean Pro if:** you want gentler silicone cleansing, heat therapy, and no recurring brush-head spend.
