# Best Electric Shavers (2026)

## What are the best electric shavers in 2026?

## TL;DR

**Top pick: Braun Series 9 Pro+ 9660CCPS (~$450) — new 96M cassette + Pro SensoAdapt 300x/sec, consensus best-overall.**
**Best value: Braun Series 8 8567cc (~$230) — Wirecutter "best value", 5-in-1 SmartCare, 132-min real-world battery.**
**Best budget: Braun Series 3 ProSkin 3040s (~$60) — reliable wet/dry foil with MicroComb, the only budget foil worth owning.**
Premium street prices reset upward 20-60% in May 2026 across all flagships. [src1, src2, src7]

## Summary

The electric shaver market in 2026 is dominated by three brands: Braun, Panasonic, and Philips Norelco. Braun and Panasonic produce foil shavers (oscillating blades behind a thin metal screen, moved in straight strokes), while Philips uses a rotary design with three circular spinning heads that pivot independently. The biggest news this year is Braun's 96xx generation Series 9 Pro+ (models 9660CCPS and 9697CC), which introduces the new 96M cassette with titanium-nitride-coated blades and a Pro SensoAdapt sensor that reads beard density 300 times per second — a massive upgrade from the previous 13x/sec AutoSense. Across major review sites, the Braun Series 9 Pro+ remains the consensus best overall shaver for most men, combining premium closeness with exceptional skin comfort. [src1, src2, src7]

For the absolute closest shave, the Panasonic Arc6 ES-LS9A-K delivers 84,000 cross-cutting actions per minute via six blades and a 22-direction flexible head — the closest an electric razor has come to matching a manual blade. MenReviewHub rates it 10/10 for closeness, though comfort drops to 7/10 due to its aggressive cutting and louder 74 dB operation. For rotary users, the Philips Norelco i9000 Prestige Ultra remains the flagship (TIME Best Invention 2025), while the current Philips Norelco Shaver Series 9000 Space Grade Edition (S9979/92, ~$280) has replaced the older 9500 S9985/84 — featuring SenseIQ AI, Dual SteelPrecision blades, and a Quick Clean Pod with 3 cartridges. The previous 9500 S9985/84 is currently out of stock on Amazon. [src3, src5, src6]

Street prices reset sharply upward in May 2026 — most flagships are now 20-60% higher than at the April refresh. The Braun Series 9 Pro+ 9660CCPS climbed to ~$450 (was ~$350), the Panasonic Arc6 ES-LS9A-K to ~$500 (was ~$300), and the Philips i9000 Prestige Ultra to ~$460 (was ~$350) — likely driven by tariff impact on Made-in-Germany and Made-in-Japan grooming devices. Mid-tier and budget tiers also rose: Braun Series 8 to ~$230, Panasonic Arc5 LV67 to ~$180, Remington F5-5800 to ~$50. The Braun Series 8 (~$230) remains the strongest mid-range value with much of the Series 9 Pro+'s technology at roughly half the price. Replacement heads now run $30-70 (the Braun 96M cassette costs ~$60-70) and should be changed every 12-18 months. [src1, src2, src4]

## Best for Each Use Case

### Best Overall: Braun Series 9 Pro+ 9660CCPS (~$450) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/braun-series-9-pro-plus-9660)
The 2026 revision of Braun's flagship introduces the 96M cassette — the most significant shaving head upgrade in a decade — with two new titanium-nitride-coated blades inside the outermost foil elements. The Pro SensoAdapt sensor now reads beard density 300 times per second (up from 13x/sec), delivering real-time motor adjustment per stroke. ShaverCheck calls comfort "stellar" and rates it as "one of the best out there for users with sensitive skin." MenReviewHub scores it 9/10 closeness and 10/10 comfort. The 6-in-1 SmartCare Center cleans, charges, lubricates, dries, and stores the shaver. The 9697CC variant (~$430) adds a ProComfort massage head with hair-releasing sonic bristles. For those wanting the previous generation at a lower price, the 9567cc (~$357) remains excellent. Note: street prices climbed sharply in May 2026 — the 9660CCPS was ~$350 in April. [src2, src6, src7]

### Best for Closest Shave: Panasonic Arc6 ES-LS9A-K (~$500) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/panasonic-arc6)
The six-blade head — two finish blades, two lift blades, and two titanium-coated thick stubble blades — delivers the closest electric shave available, approaching manual razor territory. The ultra-fast linear motor drives 84,000 cross-cutting actions per minute, and the 22-direction flexible pivoting head follows every facial contour. MenReviewHub rates it a perfect 10/10 for closeness, noting it "literally obliterates hair." Drawbacks include louder operation at 74 dB, slightly shorter battery life at 50 minutes, and more aggressive cutting that may irritate sensitive skin (comfort rated 7/10). The shaver-only ES-LS8A-K variant runs ~$364, saving ~$135 by dropping the cleaning station. The LS9A held at its May 2026 level while the LS8A eased slightly. [src2, src3, src6]

### Best Rotary Shaver: Philips Norelco i9000 Prestige Ultra (~$460) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/philips-norelco-i9000-prestige-ultra)
The flagship rotary shaver for 2025-2026, recognized as one of TIME's Best Inventions. SenseIQ Pro technology powered by AI adapts cutting speed and force to beard density and hair type with a dual-function live-action pressure sensor. 360-degree rotating NanoTech precision blades deliver 165,000 cutting actions per minute. Five custom shave modes let you dial in aggressiveness. ShaverCheck notes it handles longer 3-5+ day beards exceptionally well, where other rotary shavers struggle. Best for men with thick, coarse, or varied-growth-pattern beards. Hydro SkinGlide coating with 500,000 microbeads/cm² reduces friction by 50%. [src2, src4]

### Best Mid-Premium Rotary: Philips Norelco Shaver Series 9000 Space Grade Edition (~$280) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/philips-norelco-9500)
The current-generation Philips Series 9000 (S9979/92) replaces the older 9500 S9985/84 (now out of stock on Amazon) and remains the best mid-premium rotary with a Lift & Cut shaving system, SenseIQ Technology that reads hair density and auto-adapts cutting power, and 360-degree flexible heads. Ships with the Quick Clean Pod (3 cartridges), a dedicated charge stand, beard styler, pop-up trimmer, and travel case. ShaverCheck rates the Series 9000 line "one of the most comfortable rotary razors out there." Comes with a 5-year warranty and 2-year self-sharpening blade lifetime. [src2, src5]

### Best for Sensitive Skin: Philips Norelco S9000 Prestige SP9841 (~$300) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/philips-norelco-s9000-prestige)
GQ and multiple expert reviews highlight the S9000 Prestige for men who experience razor burn and redness. The Hydro SkinGlide coating uses up to 500,000 microtech beads per square centimeter to reduce friction by 50%. SenseIQ reads hair density 500 times per second and automatically adapts cutting power. Dual SteelPrecision blades are nano-particle strengthened and hardened with nano-particles. UltraFlex Suspension lets all heads adapt independently to every facial contour. For foil users with sensitive skin, the Braun Series 9 Pro+ is the top alternative — its ProComfort head stretches skin before cutting to minimize irritation. [src4, src5]

Wirecutter's "best value" designation, bridging the gap between the Series 7 and Series 9 Pro. Titanium-coated for corrosion resistance, 100% waterproof, made in Germany. ShaverCheck recommends it as "very comfortable" and suitable for sensitive skin. With Series 9 Pro+ now over $450, the Series 8 at ~$230 is the new go-to value pick — under half the flagship price with 80% of the performance. [src1, src2, src8]

### Best Value for Closeness: Panasonic Arc5 ES-LV67-K (~$180) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/panasonic-arc5-lv67)
ShaverCheck considers the Arc5 "unbeatable in 2026, especially in the USA" for price-to-closeness ratio. For men who prioritize closeness above all else and don't mind manual cleaning, this is the sweet spot — though prices have crept up from ~$130/$200 in April to ~$180/$240 in May. [src2, src3]

Battery provides 45 minutes of use (a week of shaves) with a 5-minute quick charge for one shave. Your Best Digs notes it as "the only design we think is worth considering" in the affordable foil category. Steady at ~$60 while premium tiers climbed. [src2, src4, src8]
