## What are the best hair dryers in 2026?

## TL;DR

## Summary

The hair dryer market in 2026 is split into three tiers: premium smart dryers with intelligent sensors and infrared technology ($300-$550), increasingly capable mid-range options ($150-$250), and budget models ($50-$150) that deliver 80-90% of the performance. The **Dyson Supersonic Nural** (MSRP ~$500) remains the editorial consensus best overall hair dryer of 2026 — Consumer Reports, CNN Underscored, Wirecutter, and Runway Magazine all rank it #1 for its Nural sensor network that monitors scalp proximity and adjusts temperature to 131&deg;F in Scalp Protect mode, measures heat 40 times per second, and auto-pauses when set down. However, as of July 2026 the Nural is **Currently Unavailable on Amazon** — buy direct from Dyson.com, Sephora, or Ulta if you want the Nural; otherwise the **Dyson Supersonic r** ($549.99) is now the safer Dyson buy: 11.6 oz, Hyperdymium motor, RFID-enabled attachments, and reliably in stock [src1, src4, src7]. The newer **Dyson Supersonic Travel** ($299.99) launched April 20, 2026 — Dyson's most affordable hair tool, 32% smaller and 25% lighter than the Nural (0.7 lb), universal 100-240V voltage, TSA-friendly, and IN STOCK on Amazon [src9].

The mid-range segment has become aggressive on both features and pricing. The **Shark HyperAIR IQ** (~$210, up from $180 in April) still earns NBC Select's "best smart hair dryer" with dual optical sensors that automatically recognize attachments and measure outlet temperature 1,000 times per second [src2, src3]. The **ghd Helios** ($280, on a 15% sale off its $329 list) is the speed champion: TechRadar measured a 1 minute 6 second rough-dry time — beating even the Dyson Supersonic Nural's 2 minute 6 second time — thanks to its 1,875W motor and 75 mph airflow [src5, src8]. The **L'Oreal AirLight Pro** ($475, up from ~$375 in April) uses infrared light to dry 14% faster while using 11% less energy, with app-based customization, though it now sits at the premium price point [src1, src8]. The **Shark SpeedStyle Pro Flex** (HD542, ~$300) is the new contender in the multi-attachment category, with a folding handle, Scalp Shield temperature regulation (1,000x/sec, capped at 230&deg;F), and the QuickSmooth/DefrizzFast attachment trio [src11].

Budget options have meaningfully moved upward in May 2026 as inflation and Amazon price normalization caught up. The **Rusk W8less Professional** ($94.96) remains Wirecutter's top overall pick — under 1 lb, ceramic and tourmaline far-infrared heat, 7 speed settings [src4]. The **Conair InfinitiPro SmoothWrap** ($70.84) still delivers 1,875W with plasma ion technology at a fraction of premium dryer pricing [src1, src3]. The **BaBylissPRO Nano Titanium Portofino** holds at **$149.99** — still the salon professional's pick at 2,000W with nano titanium ionic technology [src2, src3]. The **Laifen Swift Special** has settled to ~$127 (25% off its $170 list, down from a May peak of ~$161) on its current Amazon listing — a 110,000 RPM brushless motor in a 0.9 lb package, back in mid-budget territory [src5, src6].

### Best Editorial Pick: Dyson Supersonic Nural (~$500, buy direct) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dyson-supersonic-nural)
The Nural remains the unanimous Consumer Reports / CNN Underscored / Wirecutter / Runway Magazine pick for best overall hair dryer of 2026. It dried an eight-inch hair sample in 54 seconds in testing — the fastest in CNN Underscored's lab. As of July 2026 the Nural is intermittently out of stock on Amazon — buy direct from Dyson.com, Sephora, or Ulta. [src1, src4, src7]

### Best Travel: Dyson Supersonic Travel (~$300) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dyson-supersonic-travel)

Even at $71, it still outperforms many dryers costing three to four times more and is consistently ranked the best budget option by CNN Underscored and NBC Select. Price holds at ~$210 (only 13 left in stock at fetch time — buy quickly). [src2, src3, src5]

### Best for Curly Hair: Dyson Supersonic Nural (~$500) or Supersonic r (~$550) — [Check price](https://knowledgelib.io/go/dyson-supersonic-nural)
For curly and coily hair, the Dyson Supersonic line excels because its intelligent heat control prevents the over-drying that leads to frizz and loss of curl definition. The Nural's Wave+Curl diffuser is Dyson's newest attachment, designed to enhance natural waves and curls without disrupting their pattern, and the wide-tooth comb helps shape and lengthen curly and coily hair. The Supersonic r supports the same attachment system. The ability to toggle between low-heat settings combined with Scalp Protect mode (Nural only) makes gentle, even diffusing possible at any distance. [src1, src5, src7]

The T3 AireLuxe is the best dryer for fine and delicate hair thanks to its five heat settings and three speed settings that allow precise, gentle control. RapidAire IQ technology reduces drying time while retaining the hair's natural hydration, and its ion generator produces 10 million negative ions per second to reduce static and frizz without weighing down fine strands. The volume-boost switch adds body and lift. Note: as of July 2026 the AireLuxe is Currently Unavailable on Amazon — buy direct from T3 or Sephora. [src2, src5, src6, src10]

Note: it has climbed from ~$105 in April to $149.99 in May 2026 — still salon-class but no longer the bargain it was. [src2, src3, src6]
